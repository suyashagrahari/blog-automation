# Competitors — never link, never cite, never paraphrase

**A competitor is any site selling into the same buyer for the same job.**

Their blog post is not a source; it's the thing being beaten. Reading them in
Phase 1 is research. Reproducing their framing is how a page becomes the sixth
identical result.

## The rule

- Do not link to them.
- Do not cite them as a source.
- Do not paraphrase their structure, framing, or section order closely.
- Do not use their statistics, even with attribution.

If a competitor published original data that genuinely matters, **find the primary
source they used and cite that instead.** If they *are* the primary source, either
state the fact without attribution (only when it's common industry knowledge) or
leave it out and find a different fact.

## Categories to treat as competitors

**Online gifting and personalised-gift retailers** — same buyer ("I need to make
someone feel special remotely"), same occasions:

- Ferns N Petals (fnp.com)
- IGP (igp.com)
- Winni (winni.in)
- Archies (archiesonline.com)
- BigSmall (bigsmall.in)
- Oye Happy (oyehappy.com)
- Zoomin (zoomin.com)
- Printvenue, Presto, Chumbak, and equivalent personalised-gift shops
- Giftalove, Floweraura, MyFlowerTree, Interflora India

**Digital invitation and e-card builders** — direct substitutes for a SubhSandesh
page:

- Canva (invitation/greeting templates specifically)
- Greetings Island, Punchbowl, Paperless Post, Evite
- WedMeGood, ShaadiSaga, WeddingWire India (invitation-builder content)
- Indian "digital invite" and "caricature invite" studios
- Any "create a free digital invitation" tool

**Greeting-message and wishes content farms** — they compete for the exact
long-tail queries these posts target:

- 143greetings, WishesMsg, BestMessage, Shayari/wishes aggregators
- "Happy Birthday wishes for…" listicle sites

**Adjacent page-builder tools** used for the same job:

- Linktree / Carrd / Notion-as-a-love-letter style guides
- "Make a website for your girlfriend" tutorial sites

## Not competitors — safe to cite

- Government and official statistics (TRAI, MeitY, Census, RBI, IAMAI reports)
- Peer-reviewed research and universities
- Industry bodies and standards organisations (W3C, Google Search Central docs)
- Non-competing companies' original research (Statista, Pew, Deloitte, Kantar,
  Nielsen, Ericsson Mobility Report)
- Reputable data journalism and mainstream news
- Wikipedia, for entity disambiguation only (0–2 links, see `tone-and-links.md`)

## When in doubt

Ask: *would this site's owner consider SubhSandesh a rival for the same customer?*
If plausibly yes, don't cite it. There is always another source for a fact worth
including — and if there isn't, the fact probably isn't worth including.
