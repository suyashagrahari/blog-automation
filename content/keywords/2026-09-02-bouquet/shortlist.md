**The distinction that decides this whole run:** a SERP full of *gift retailers*
is an editorial vacuum — nobody answers the question, they only sell against it,
and that is a slot a post can take. A SERP full of *dedicated flower-meaning
blogs* is not a vacuum — those are topical authorities answering the query
properly, and a low-authority general-interest blog does not displace them.

Gate 4 counts a marketplace as weak for exactly this reason. It does not count
`petalrepublic.com` as weak, and neither should you.

**Consequence: the openings are in occasion gifting, not in flower knowledge** —
the reverse of what the demand data alone suggests. The flower-meaning axis had
84 open candidates and the highest raw demand in the run, and it is the *worst*
place to spend the next ten posts.

## Honest limits on these numbers

- **No Ahrefs, Semrush or Keyword Planner.** Every volume figure is a band
  labelled `EST`, placed from autocomplete `prefix_hits`. **There are no KD
  numbers in this run at all** — difficulty is a weak-result count, which is a
  different and cruder thing. If you paste any tool export these get replaced.
- **No Search Console export.** Third run in a row without it. It is the only
  keyword data that is true about this site, and it takes two minutes to produce.
  With it, Phase 1 would find pages already sitting at position 5-20 across those
  103 flower articles — which is almost certainly cheaper traffic than any new
  page in this table.
- **`WebSearch` was exhausted (200/200) before this run started.** All 13 SERPs
  came from DuckDuckGo's HTML endpoint, India locale. That is weaker evidence than
  `WebSearch`: no AI-Overview detection, no PAA, no ads signal, and DDG's ranking
  is not Google's. It rate-limited hard — **3 of 16 attempted SERPs failed
  entirely** and are recorded as failures, not as zero-weak results. Treat every
  weakness count here as indicative, and re-pull the top 5 with `WebSearch` in a
  fresh session before committing a quarter to them.
- **`own_position` was never established.** No per-keyword `site:` pass was run,
  so every row says `none`. Given 103 live flower articles that is very likely
  wrong for some rows, and it is the largest single inaccuracy in this file.
- **Dedupe is title-level**, Jaccard on slug + title tokens against 935 live
  articles. It catches "we already wrote this"; it does not catch "we already
  rank for this".

## What would change this picture

The single highest-leverage move for `/bouquet-gf` is **not** another post. It is:

1. **Pull Search Console and re-run Phase 1 against the 103 existing flower
   articles.** A post already at position 8 needs an edit, not a competitor. On a
   103-article base that is where the cheapest traffic in this whole cluster is.
2. **Consolidate.** `virtual-bouquet`, `virtual-bouquet-online-free`,
   `free-virtual-flower-bouquet`, `virtual-flower-bouquet`, `digital-bouquet`,
   `digital-flower-bouquet`, `digital-flower-bouquet-free` and
   `virtual-bouquet-maker-online` are eight pages competing for one intent. That
   is the self-cannibalisation Gate 3 exists to prevent, and it already happened.
   Merging them into one strong page would likely move more traffic than
   anything in the table above.
