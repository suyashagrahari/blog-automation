Unique domains across the measured set: **96 result slots → 81 unique domains.**
Nothing like the "13 × 10 = 130 competitors" figure that counting slots would give.

## The finding that should change the plan

**Product companies already win these SERPs in three of the nine languages measured.**

- **French** — `fr.lovebox.love` (connected message box) at #1, `flexilivre.com`
  (photo books) at #2 on `tu me manques message`
- **German** — `famwalls.com` (wall art) in the top 10 on `ich vermisse dich sprüche`
- **Turkish** — `ciceksepeti.com`, `hediyesepeti.com`, `blog.sosyopix.com`,
  `hediyemen.com` hold 4 of 10 slots on `seni özledim sözleri`

A personalised-gift brand ranking on "I miss you" message keywords is not a theory
that needs testing. It is the established pattern in those markets, which is the
strongest argument in this run for publishing outside English.

**Italian and Spanish are the opposite**: quote farms only, no product site anywhere
in the top 10. Open ground, and nobody has proved the intent converts there.

The last five are group-card SaaS and own the farewell/colleague SERP outright. That
is a different product from a private one-to-one page, and it is not worth contesting.

## Publisher walls, by language
- **Indonesian** — CNN Indonesia, Katadata, Kumparan, Liputan6, Merdeka, Sonora,
  Halodoc. National news desks with staff listicle writers. Do not enter.
- **Turkish** — national papers (Milliyet, Hürriyet) hold the head terms, but the
  reply-intent and long-tail queries are pure forum. Enter there.
- **Spanish / Italian / Portuguese** — quote farms, mostly a decade old, no product
  pages. Enterable with a better page.
- **Filipino** — nothing at all. Wikipedia, TikTok and a machine-translation dump.

## Targeted vs ranking
Every keyword in `keyword-inventory.csv` is a keyword a page **targets** or a query
the harvest proves people type. None of it is rank-tracking data. No page fetches were
performed in this run — the fetch budget was spent on breadth across nine languages
instead of depth on any one of them, which is a deliberate trade and a real limitation.
