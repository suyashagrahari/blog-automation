`own_position` in this run is measured; every one reads `none-or-unmeasured`.

## What makes this run different

The two prior runs asked *what does India type after "miss you"*. Neither looked
outside India, and the live corpus reflects that: 81 pages, zero of them in Spanish,
Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Indonesian, Arabic,
Russian, Filipino, Japanese or Korean. That gap, plus the generic head terms both
prior runs gated out for being under four words, is the whole subject of this run.
