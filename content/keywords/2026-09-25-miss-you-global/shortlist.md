## Honest labels before anything else

- **Every volume figure is `EST`.** No Ahrefs, Semrush or Keyword Planner access for
  any of these markets. Bands come from autocomplete `prefix_hits`, SERP furniture and
  market size, and they are bands, never point numbers.
- **The anchor is `miss you quotes` at band 5** (10,000+/mo, US). Every other band in
  the sheet is placed relative to it. Change the anchor and the whole sheet moves.
- **Disclosed deviation:** the Gate 6c band table is written for the India market.
  This run applies the same thresholds to **each market's own** volume, because a
  Dutch keyword at 2,000/mo is a band 3 opportunity *in the Netherlands*. Bands are
  therefore comparable within a market and **not** across markets. Expected clicks are.
- **13 SERPs measured, 137 rows archetype-inferred.** The `evidence_type` column says
  which is which for every row.
- **All SERPs were US-served.** See the disclosure at the top of `serps.md`.
- **No Search Console export.** With 81 live pages in this cluster, that export would
  be worth more than this entire run. Ask before the next batch.

## 5 · The honest read

**The premise needs one correction.** You asked for high-volume generics, and they are
here — but the four biggest English generics in this cluster are shared with famous
songs, and the biggest non-English ones (`tu me manques`, `ich vermisse dich`,
`tęsknię za tobą`) are each shared with **a TV series** — French, German and Polish
searchers all pair the phrase with cast, episode-count and streaming queries, and
with Harlan Coben's name. Volume that belongs to a TV show does not convert on a
greeting-page site.
*(Corrected 2026-09-25, then refined: an earlier version called all three "the same
Harlan Coben Netflix series" under localised titles. Checked by two agents, it splits
by market — it HOLDS for German (de.wikipedia has* Ich vermisse dich*, Originaltitel*
Missing You*, Q124795248), is FALSE for French (no French article for Coben's series;*
Tu me manques *on Wikipedia is a 2019 Bolivian drama), and is UNVERIFIED for Polish.
The autocomplete evidence for a series entity on all three head terms is real; the
one-series generalisation was mine and was drawn from a single market. See
`candidates.md`.)*
The real opportunity in this seed is not the head terms. It is that **every market on
earth asks how to say it in someone else's language**, and SubhSandesh already has the
hub page for exactly that.

**The realistic ceiling.** Sum of expected clicks across the 15 shortlisted pages is
roughly 4,500/mo at full maturity, and that assumes position 3 on every one. The
improve-existing set is worth about 1,100/mo on pages that already exist.

**The competitive case is already proven in three languages.** Product blogs — Lovebox
and Flexilivre in France, four gift retailers in Turkey, a wall-art brand in Germany —
already rank on exactly these keywords. That is not a theory about whether a
personalised-page brand can win emotional-message SERPs. It is the current result.

**Cannibalisation is the live risk, not competition.** 81 pages already exist here.
Phase 8b reassigned 9 keywords to existing pages and folded 12 more into cluster
primaries. Any new English page in this cluster needs a reason it is not one of the 81.

**The feedback loop.** Ask for the Search Console export before the next miss-you
batch; 81 indexed pages make it the highest-value input available and it takes two
minutes. `npm run keywords:inventory` will show next run which of these 15 got written.

## 6 · Handoff to `blog-optimisation`

Send these first: **`c11-tr` (Turkish reply-intent)**, **`c13-ph` (Filipino
languages)**, **`c05-pt` (Brazilian Portuguese)**, **`c03-translate-en` spokes**.

Two warnings for whoever drafts them:

1. **The facts gate will bite.** `content/facts.md` has no miss-you page type and no
   non-India segment at all. A Turkish or Filipino post cannot cite India-measured
   first-party numbers as if they were local. Either measure the segment first or
   state the scope in the post.
2. **Verify every non-English line against a dictionary**, the way the propose waves
   did for the Indian languages. The Filipino loanword claim, the French dative
   construction, the Portuguese "saudade is untranslatable" claim and the Japanese
   会いたい ≠ "I miss you" point are all *findings from a SERP*, not verified facts yet.
