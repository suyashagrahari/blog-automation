## Read
- **Seven of the eight days are India-centric searches.** US-served SERPs for "rose day 2027" and "kiss day 2027" return the *June/July* US novelty holidays (Red Rose Day, National Kissing Day). The US demand for Feb 7–13 days is small and mostly Indian diaspora. The US volume is in **Valentine's Day** itself.
- Quote/wish SERPs for the 7 week-days are owned by Indian gift stores (FNP, GiftaLove, MyFlowerTree, OyeGifts) and news (TOI, ET, HT, NDTV, IE) whose titles all say **2026** — the stale-year weakness repeats on every measured SERP. A 2027-titled page published Dec–Jan has a real opening.
- US Valentine's Day head SERPs (messages, gifts) are held by American Greetings, Good Housekeeping, Country Living, Wirecutter, CNN — 0–3 weak. These are high-volume, low-winnability; the winnable US ground is long-tail modifiers (virtual, long distance, digital card, with photo, for him/her + specific situation).
