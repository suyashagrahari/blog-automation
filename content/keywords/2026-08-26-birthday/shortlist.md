## What changed from v1, and why

v1 shipped 30 keywords of which 26 contained the literal phrase "birthday wishes" and all
30 were informational. Three defects were found and fixed:

1. **The clustering rule was never applied across the list.** Phase 8a says 3+ shared
   top-10 results means one keyword. Checking the 30 against each other collapsed
   `janamdin ki hardik shubhkamnaye` + `birthday quotes in hindi` +
   `birthday wishes for brother in hindi` into `birthday wishes in hindi`, and
   `birthday wishes for boss in hindi` into `birthday wishes for wife in hindi`.
   v1's click total was double-counted by ~640/mo as a result.
2. **Five keywords were never SERP-checked.** All five are now checked. `for niece` and
   `for uncle` dropped from 5+ weak to 3-4 (their expected clicks fall from 132 to 88);
   `birthday wishes in hindi` — v1's highest band — had a "why winnable" line describing
   a *different* keyword's SERP, now replaced with its own; `ka reply kaise de` is
   confirmed and turns out to have the weakest SERP in the whole run.
3. **Six new axes added**, on request. Half of them do not work as blog posts — see below.

## The Gate 2 finding on the new axes

`birthday cake with name`, `birthday song with name`, `birthday photo frame with name` and
`birthday dp for whatsapp` all have very high demand and zero coverage — and a blog post
**cannot rank in any of them**, because those SERPs are image generators, streaming
platforms and stock-photo libraries, not articles. Writing posts for them would produce
pages that never rank.

Two of those axes were rescued by finding the *message-shaped* query inside them:

| Axis | Head term (cut) | What to write instead |
|---|---|---|
| Cake | `birthday cake with name` — tool SERP | **`what to write on a birthday cake`** — a blog SERP whose #1 and #2 are a Kenyan and a Nigerian bakery blog |
| Song | `birthday song with name` — Spotify/YouTube | `birthday songs for whatsapp status` — one blog (bakingo) ranks; conversion is weak |
| Photo frame / DP | `birthday dp for whatsapp` — Unsplash/Pixabay | nothing viable found |
| Numerology | `birthday numerology calculator` — needs a tool | **`birthday date personality test`** — 29 autocomplete prefixes, the highest demand measured in this entire run |

|---|---|---|

| `birthday cake design for husband` | G2+G5 | e-commerce bakeries — cremecastle, fnp, bakingo, theobroma — plus Pinterest ×4. Transactional cake-ordering intent. SubhSandesh does not sell cakes |
| `birthday song with name` | G2 | 1happybirthday, birthdaysongmaker, aimakesong are generators; YouTube and Spotify hold the rest. Tool and streaming SERP |
| `birthday song with name in hindi` | G2 | Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube, JioSaavn. Competing with streaming platforms is not winnable |
| `best birthday songs hindi` | G2 | only 4 results — YouTube, Spotify, JioSaavn and one blog. Effectively a streaming SERP |

## What was NOT measured

1. **No tool volume.** Every band is inferred from SERP furniture and autocomplete prefix
   counts. Ten minutes in Keyword Planner would replace every `EST` and could reorder this.
2. **No Google Trends data.** Comparison groups are in `trends-groups.md`.
3. **No Search Console export.** 168 live birthday posts, zero top-10 appearances across
   60 SERPs. Striking-distance problem or indexing problem — only GSC distinguishes them,
   and it decides whether these 32 posts land in the same hole.

## Conversion-fit caveats to carry into the briefs

- **Decoration** (#3) and **song** (#14) are top-of-funnel. The searcher wants balloons or
  an MP3; SubhSandesh sells a shareable page. Real traffic, weak conversion — worth writing,
  not worth over-investing in.
- **Urdu** (#21) needs a native reader before publishing. The rakhi batch left six posts
  blocked on exactly this, one marked "not closable by any agent."
