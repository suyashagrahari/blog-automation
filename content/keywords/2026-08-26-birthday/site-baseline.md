This is the whole finding of Phase 1. The site has saturated the **English template**
axis and the **English relation** axis, and has never once published on the **language**
axis or the **social/emotional** axis. In an India-targeted greetings market those two
are where the volume actually sits.

## Own it already — improve, don't rewrite

No birthday keyword in this run has an existing page at position 5-20, because
`subhsandesh.in` did not appear in **any** of the 32 India-localised SERPs harvested in
Phase 4. Every row in the shortlist is therefore `new-page`, not `improve-existing`.

That is itself worth stating plainly: 168 Milestone Birthday posts are live and none of
them ranks in the top 10 for a single one of the 32 queries checked. Without a Search
Console export I cannot tell whether they rank at 11-30 (fixable by editing) or are
unindexed (a different problem). **This is the single highest-value thing to resolve
before writing 30 more posts.**

## Own-site collisions found — keywords deliberately CUT for cannibalisation

| Keyword | Collides with | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| `birthday wishes maker with name` (6 autocomplete prefixes — the highest demand in the entire run) | `free-birthday-wish-website-maker`, `create-birthday-wish-link-online` | **CUT.** Improve the two live pages instead |
| `birthday wishes maker with name and photo` (5 prefixes) | same | **CUT** — same reason |
| `birthday wishes maker online free` | same | **CUT** |
| `birthday message maker` | `birthday-message-maker-for-girlfriend` | **CUT** |
| `birthday wishes video maker` | `miss-you-video-maker-with-photos`, `raksha-bandhan-video-maker-with-photo-and-music` | **CUT** — the pattern is already built twice |
| `heart touching birthday wishes for best friend` | `long-birthday-wishes-for-best-friend`, `birthday-wishes-for-best-friend` | **CUT** — too close to two live posts |

The site has **80+ live `*maker*` / `*interactive*` pages.** The maker axis is the most
in-demand shape in the autocomplete harvest and it is also the most cannibalised part of
the site. Writing new maker posts would compete with existing ones.
