## Who does NOT rank here

FNP appeared once (position 9, `online gift for girlfriend`). IGP, Archies, Amazon and
Flipkart did **not** appear in any of the three `virtual|digital|online gift` SERPs.
They dominate the `gift for girlfriend` physical-intent SERPs instead. That split is the
central finding of this run.

## Recurring weak results across all 6 SERPs

Reddit (r/LongDistance, r/Gifts), Pinterest idea boards, Instagram reels, YouTube,
Quora. Between 4 and 6 of every 10 results. Reddit ranking #1 on `digital gift for
girlfriend` is the clearest single signal that this category has no authoritative page.
