## The structural finding

Two completely different SERPs hide under the word "gift":

1. **`virtual` / `digital` / `online gift`** → held by small personalised-page startups —
   gifft.me, qlovy.com, surprises.gift, ourgiftverse.com, digiflower.net, messagear.com.
   These are SubhSandesh's **direct product competitors**, not retail giants. Winnable.
2. **`gift for girlfriend` / `birthday gift` / `under ₹500`** → physical-product intent,
   served by Amazon, Target, Myntra, Wirecutter, Neiman Marcus. Higher volume, but a
   personalised-page product does not satisfy the searcher (Gate 5), and the ₹-band
   queries are being served **US-dollar** results entirely.
| digital gift for girlfriend | 5/10 | **WINNABLE — our exact category** | qlovy #2, gifft.me #3, **giftsqr.com #6**, digiflower.net #8, etsy + Reddit #1, Pinterest x2, IG, YT |
| long distance relationship gift ideas online | 5/10 | medium — affiliate/physical, mostly US | endlessdistances, amazon, ediblearrangements, lovebookonline, theknot, lovebox.love + Reddit, Quora, YT, Pinterest |

## The competitive set (who we are actually fighting)

Not IGP / FNP / Archies. The `virtual|digital|online gift` SERPs are held by small
personalised-page startups, every one of them beatable by a site with 900+ live pages:

`gifft.me` · `qlovy.com` · `surprises.gift` · `ourgiftverse.com` · `digiflower.net` ·
`giftsqr.com` · `blog.messagear.com`

`digiflower.net` competes directly with SubhSandesh's existing `digital-bouquet` pages.
