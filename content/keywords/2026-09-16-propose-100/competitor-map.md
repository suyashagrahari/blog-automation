## URL architecture of the three domains that matter

**bemyval.co** — flat, one page per ask-type. Root sells the product; spokes are
`/girlfriend-proposal-website-maker`, `/valentine-…`. Holds positions 1 and 9 on the
same SERP with two pages. Anchor text reveals a spoke per relationship label.
**Copy this pattern**; it is why they own four slots between two operators.

**yourlovepage.online / .com** — two domains, one operator, hub-and-spoke.
`.com` is the brand hub listing "By occasion → Proposal website"; `.online` holds the
money page `/proposal-website`. Ranks on both the product SERP *and* the long-distance
advice SERP — the only competitor that crosses clusters.

**pixeryhub.com** — `/propose` and `/create`, two pages, no blog. Titles lead with
"Free … Link Generator" and the SERP description names the mechanic explicitly
("custom message, theme, instant sharing"). Thin site, high ranking, no content moat.

**What to do about it:** run these 8 question-shaped queries yourself in ChatGPT,
Gemini and Perplexity and paste the cited domains back. It is a 20-minute task and it
produces real citation data, which nothing here substitutes for:

```
how do I propose to my girlfriend online
what is the best website to propose to your girlfriend
how to propose on WhatsApp
what to write in a Propose Day message for your girlfriend
how to propose in a long distance relationship
what is Propose Day and when is it in 2027
how do you make a proposal website for free
what should I send my girlfriend on Propose Day
```

The expected finding, based on every prior run in this repo: answer engines cite
**third-party roundups over vendor pages**. If that holds, getting named in a "best
proposal website makers" roundup beats optimising your own tool page — a different and
usually faster job.

## What none of them cover

1. **Propose Day as a *product* occasion.** Every Propose Day result is either a quote
   list or a physical gift. Not one of the proposal-page makers — bemyval, yourlovepage,
   pixeryhub, momentocard — has a Propose Day page at all. They are Western startups
   who do not know the Indian Valentine week exists. **862 autocomplete suggestions
   sit in that gap.**

2. **Any Indian language.** Zero proposal-page makers serve Hindi, Marathi, Bengali,
   Kannada, Telugu or Malayalam. The language SERPs belong entirely to news media
   publishing copy-text lists.

3. **The advice-to-tool bridge.** The advice SERPs are Quora, Reddit and TikTok; the
   tool SERPs are the startups. Nobody has written the page that answers "how do I
   propose over chat" *and* hands the reader the thing to send. That page is the
   shortlist's #4 and #6.
