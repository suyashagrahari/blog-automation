## Read this before the numbers

**No Search Console export — the ninth run asking.** Every position in this run is from
a `site:` check, and every volume is an inference band. The single highest-value thing
you can do for the next run is export Queries + Impressions + Position + Clicks from
Search Console. It takes two minutes and it is the only keyword data that is true about
your own site.

`expected_clicks` is a product of three estimates (band midpoint × CTR × win
probability). **Its job is ordering, not forecasting.** Do not read 130/mo as a promise
of 130 visits.

`online proposal website free` returns **10 of 10 business-proposal software**:

### 3. Your highest-volume keywords are not your highest-click keywords

This is the direct answer to "extract 50 too-much-volume keywords first."

| Requested tier | Keywords | `winnable` | `medium` | cut |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T1 — 50 high-volume** | 50 | **5** | 45 | 0 |
| **T2 — 20 medium-volume** | 20 | **15** | 1 | 2 (+2 merge) |
| **T3 — 30 long-tail** | 30 | **8** | 18 | 4 |

The 50 highest-volume keywords produced **5 winnable pages**. The 20 medium-volume
keywords produced **15**. That inversion is the whole reason Gate 6 runs last.

**Why.** Tier 1 is almost entirely `propose day quotes / wishes / shayari` — the
searcher's job is finished by **copying a line of text** and leaving. Gate 5
arithmetic, stated plainly:

```
propose day quotes      20,000 searches × 0.1% conversion =  20 conversions
virtual propose day      2,000 searches × 5%   conversion = 100 conversions
```

They are **not cut** — they are bucketed `medium` and targeted at position 6-10 rather
than position 3. Your live corpus is already 905 articles heavy in wishes and messages
posts, so this is a content play you are demonstrably already running, which is exactly
the exception Gate 5 names. But do not build them first, and do not expect them to
convert.

**The rescue inside Tier 1** is the autocomplete tell: `propose day wishes with name`
(7 prefixes) and `propose day quotes with name` (6). Same topic, completely different
job at the end of the click — a *named, shareable page*, which is your product. Those
two are the only Tier 1 entries that score `transactional`, and they are in the
shortlist below at #5 and #6.

## The honest read

**The niche is not contested where it matters, and the premise was right.** Eight of ten
results on the core product SERP are weak, and the strongest incumbents are two-person
startups (bemyval and yourlovepage hold four of ten slots between them). There is no
Amazon, no FNP and no national publisher anywhere in the proposal-page territory.

**But the traffic ceiling is modest.** The realistic winnable set is 12 pages totalling
roughly **1,000 clicks/mo EST** at the target positions, and that figure is a product of
three estimates. The `medium` bucket holds most of the raw volume and most of it will
not convert.

**Where the premise was wrong:** you asked for 50 very-high-volume keywords first. The
50 highest-volume keywords in this niche yielded **5 winnable pages**; the 20
medium-volume ones yielded **15**. High volume in this niche means "someone wants to
copy a Hindi shayari for their WhatsApp status", and that is a different business from
yours. The 100 are delivered in the tiers you asked for and every one is labelled, but
build from Tier 2 down, not Tier 1 across.

**The biggest opportunity is not in this list.** It is the 46 live pages, invisible on
every SERP measured, whose positions nobody has looked at. This run cannot see them.
Search Console can.
