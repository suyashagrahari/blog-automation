## Google vs answer engines

Phase 5 was not run as a separate citation harvest in this run — only Google-shaped
searches were used, and no synthesised-answer citation sets were captured. What the
Google SERPs already show, though, is the pattern the skill predicts: on the commercial
queries the surfaced results are **vendor pages**, while the editorial slots go to
third-party roundups (`lettersbyheart.com/blog/best-digital-gifts-for-couples`,
`justmeantforyou.com/blog`, `marriage.com`, `theknot.com`). Answer engines
disproportionately cite that second group.

**Implication:** the highest-leverage GEO move for `/love-gf` is being *named in other
people's roundups* of love-page builders and digital gifts for couples — not another
self-published product page. Treat that as outreach work, tracked separately from this
shortlist. To get true citation data, run the 10 question-shaped queries in
`candidates.md` through ChatGPT / Perplexity / Gemini directly and paste the cited
domains back; nothing in this run can substitute for that.

## Architecture worth copying

- `yourlovepage.online` runs a blog that feeds its own tool pages, with one post per
  audience segment (girlfriend / boyfriend / partner). That is the internal-linking shape
  `/love-gf` should have.
- `truthordarequiz.com` puts the current year in the title and refreshes it. Cheap, and it
  closes the stale-year opening.
- `tenorshare` runs a programmatic "20 samples: love letter in `<language>`" set. It is
  thin AI content and it still ranks, which is the clearest evidence available that the
  language vein has demand and no real incumbent.
