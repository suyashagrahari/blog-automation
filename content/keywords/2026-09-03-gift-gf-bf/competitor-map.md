## The five that matter

1. **`confettigifts.in` — 15 of 34.** An Indian gifting retailer ranking on *blog*
   URLs. This is the exact play available to SubhSandesh, executed by someone with a
   shop attached. It is on the do-not-link list: read it, never cite it, and go to
   whatever primary source it used.
2. **`theknot.com` — 13 of 34.** US wedding authority, real editorial weight, and
   beatable **only** on India-specific ground. Do not write a generic listicle at it.
3. **`pinterest.com` (28) + `reddit.com` (22) + `quora.com` (14).** 64 slots, ~24% of
   the parsed page-1 real estate, all of it unedited. This is the opening.
4. **`fnp.com` (16) + `oyehappy.com` (16) + `igp.com` (8) + `amazon.in` (16).** The
   retailer wall. They rank because they sell, not because they answer, which is why
   4.8 of ~8 results per SERP count as weak.
5. **`gifft.me` and `memento.com`.** Both appear for `how to surprise your boyfriend
   on his birthday online` — digital-gift-page products ranking on the intent
   SubhSandesh actually serves. Proof the asset type can hold this SERP.
