This is the concrete cost of the skill's "market locality is not optional" rule,
and it is worth more than any row in the table below.

### The Gate 4 recount — a correction, made after the first drafts

**These weakness counts were revised upward on 2026-09-03 and the reason matters.**
The first pass classified weak results against a fixed list of well-known shops
(`amazon.in`, `fnp.com`, `igp.com`, and so on). A writer drafting the
Valentine's-week post pointed out that `confettigifts.in` and `jusst4you.com` —
both scored **strong** — are gifting *retailers*. So were `boontoon.com`,
`zwende.com`, `boxupgifting.com`, `giftory.com`, `saugatonline.com`,
`uncommongoods.com`, `incrediblegifts.in` and a dozen more: every unfamiliar
Indian gifting store was being counted as editorial competition.

Recounted, with retailer and product pages moved to weak and one genuinely
off-intent page (a personal-injury law firm ranking for
`how to surprise my boyfriend when he comes home`) moved with them:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Mean weak results | 4.8 | **6.4** of 6–9 parsed hosts |
| Rows in the 5+ tier (win 0.60) | 22 of 34 | **32 of 34** |
| Total expected clicks/mo (all 34) | 288 | **342** |

Three rows jumped from 9 clicks to 21: `how to surprise my girlfriend with
flowers` (4/8 → **8/8** — the entire SERP is florist shops, none of which answers
the question), `romantic gift ideas for boyfriend` (3/7 → 5/7) and
`how to surprise my boyfriend for no reason` (4/8 → 5/8).

**Deliberately NOT reclassified:** `pampers.com`, `thebump.com` and
`clearblue.com` on the pregnancy keyword. They really do publish
pregnancy-announcement articles, so on that query they are on-intent editorial,
not shops. Reclassifying them would have flattered the number.

**The lesson for the next run:** a fixed-list weakness classifier systematically
understates a long tail of small local competitors. Judge the host, do not pattern-match it.

### Read the total honestly before writing anything

318 clicks across **30 posts is about 11 clicks per post.** Wave 3 of the bouquet
cluster scored 205 clicks across **10** posts — **20.5 per post**, and one single
keyword there (`what to gift for wife on anniversary`) was worth 132 on its own.

**This cluster is broader and flatter. Per post it is still roughly half as
valuable as the bouquet work**, even after the recount. Nothing in this harvest
reached the ≥22x demand threshold, so no row lands in volume band 3 and the
per-keyword ceiling is 21 clicks. If the question is "what should I write next for
maximum traffic", the answer is not these 30 — it is the Search Console pass
described at the bottom.

What these 30 *are* good for: they close a structural hole. The site has one gift
page aimed at a man and 154 aimed at women, and an internal-linking cluster of 21
boyfriend-gift posts is worth more than the sum of its rankings.

## Method, and its limits — read before trusting any number here

- **No Ahrefs, Semrush or Keyword Planner. There are no KD figures in this run.**
  Every volume is a band labelled `EST`, placed from autocomplete `prefix_hits`
  on the same thresholds waves 1–3 used (≥22x → 1,000–3,000; ≥13x → 300–1,000;
  else <300). "Difficulty" here is a weak-result count, which is cruder and
  different. You asked for low-KD keywords; this is the closest honest substitute.
- **34 of 40 SERPs measured.** `WebSearch` quota is exhausted (200/200) and
  DuckDuckGo hard-blocked the IP mid-run, so the 34 were pulled through a real
  Google session at `gl=in&hl=en`. Google then began returning 429 with a CAPTCHA
  challenge and **the run stopped there rather than push further.** The 6
  unmeasured keywords are listed below and are **not** in the 30.
- **`own_position` is measured this time, not assumed.** `subhsandesh.in` appears
  in **zero** of the 34 SERPs, so every row reads `none (not in top 6–9)`. Waves
  1–3 could only say `none` because nothing was measured; this is a real check,
  though it only covers page 1, not the top 20.
- **No Search Console export. Fifth run in a row without it.** It remains the
  only keyword data that is true about this site.
- **No Google Trends data.** `prefix_hits` orders these keywords against each
  other; it says nothing about absolute volume, and no interest-over-time was
  fetched.
- **Phase 8a collapsed only 3%** (34 → 33 clusters, one merge). That is far below
  the skill's typical 30–50%, and the reason is that clustering had to run on
  *distinctive* hosts — 11 hosts appear in 8+ of the 34 SERPs, so counting shared
  URLs naively would have merged nearly everything. Low collapse here means these
  really are separate SERPs, but the method is weaker than URL-level overlap.
- **Dedupe is title-level** — Jaccard on slug+title tokens against the 975 live
  blog slugs and the 170 drafted primary keywords. All 30 scored < 0.6. It
  catches "we already wrote this"; it does not catch "we already rank for this".
- **The `husband` axis was never harvested.** All nine seeds said
  boyfriend/girlfriend, so husband phrasings barely appear in the pool (5 rows,
  all 1x, all junk co-occurrences). That is a **seed artifact, not a market
  fact** — wave 3 measured `promotion gift for husband` at 7x from different
  seeds. Do not read this file as evidence that husband demand is absent.
- **41 pre-existing own-site collisions** (overlap ≥ 0.6) sit in the rakhi and
  birthday clusters and were left standing — a consolidation job on published
  pages, out of scope here, flagged so it is a decision and not an oversight.
