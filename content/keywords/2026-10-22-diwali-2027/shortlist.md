## Read this first
1. **Volumes are EST, not tool data.** Ranking is by live India autocomplete demand (`prefix_hits`, English + Hindi). It orders keywords correctly against each other; it is not a monthly search number. Paste a Keyword Planner export and the bands can be replaced.
2. **Diwali 2026 (Sun 8 Nov 2026) is 17 days away — nothing published now ranks for it.** This list targets Diwali 2027. `publish_by` is mostly **3 Sept 2027** (8 weeks before), so build pages through spring/summer 2027.
3. **The fastest wins are product-shaped:** "diwali wishes maker", "diwali wishes with name/photo", "diwali wishes template for company/business" (24 rows) — the searcher wants to *make and send* something, which is exactly SubhSandesh. 14 rows overlap posts you already drafted (`happy-diwali-wishes-in-hindi`, `diwali-wishes-with-name-and-photo`…) and are tagged `improve-existing`.
4. **Wishes/captions/shayari SERPs are soft (8/10 weak) — every editorial title still says 2023–2026.** A 2027-titled page has a real opening.
5. **High volume, low conversion:** rangoli/drawing (image & video intent), essays/speeches (school reading), muhurat (drikpanchang owns it; accuracy-critical — two wrong-date pages are currently ranking). Kept because you asked for the most-searched terms; labelled low intent fit.
6. **Gift SERPs are shops** (Milton, Nestasia, Pure Home); blogs rank only on long-tails like "diwali gift ideas for employees under 1000".
