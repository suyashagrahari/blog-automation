**All thirteen are position 5-20 or better** — the highest-ROI segment in the skill.

Three things follow, and they shape the whole run:

1. **The rankings cluster on the girlfriend side.** Twelve of thirteen name
   girlfriend / gf / her. The highest-*demand* phrasings in the harvest are the
   boyfriend ones (`virtual gift for boyfriend` 50x, `online surprise for boyfriend`
   47x, `web page for boyfriend` 24x) and we rank for none of them. That asymmetry —
   ranking pages framed for her, demand skewed to him — is the specific edit to make.
2. **Several are the same page.** `gift link for gf` @5, `surprise link for girlfriend`
   @6, `surprise message link for girlfriend` @6, `virtual gift for girlfriend` @7,
   `online gift maker for gf` @6 and `virtual gift for girlfriend free` @4 all return
   *Free Girlfriend Day Gift Online — Create a Personalized Page*. **One page is
   ranking for six keywords while titled for a single narrow occasion.**
3. **Two point at `/apology-dashboard`** (`digital sorry card for gf` @9,
   `sorry surprise link for girlfriend` @4) — the **#3 template, 753 pages, 22.0% of
   everything created** — and this harvest surfaced only two keywords for it. That is
   an under-harvested theme, not a covered one.

Nine of the thirteen were only found in the second SERP wave, after the rate-limit
pause. Stopping at 41 SERPs would have reported a materially wrong picture of what
the site already owns.

`own_position` is `none` on the other 48 rows, and on this axis that claim is weak:
with 325 live slugs already carrying `online`/`digital`/`virtual`/`link`/`maker`/
`free` tokens, "not on page 1 of the SERP I pulled" is not "we have no page aimed at
this".

## 1d. First-party demand from `content/facts.md`

Used as a tie-breaker, not a filter. Measured 2026-09-03:

- `/love-gf` — 1,001 pages, **29.2% of all 3,425 created**, and 14,828 views, 35.2%
  of all page views. The most-used and most-viewed template on the site.
- `/apology-dashboard` — 753 created, 22.0%.
- `/darling` — 349, 10.2%. `/birthday-gf` — 258, 7.5%.
- **99.4% of started pages are actually published and shared** (3,405 of 3,425).
- **42.7% of creators password-protect the page before sharing** (1,462 of 3,425).

The last two are the ones that matter on this axis. Every keyword in this harvest
describes *sending a link to a person*, and the first-party number says that when
someone builds one of these on SubhSandesh they almost always finish and send it.
That is a conversion-fit argument no keyword tool can produce, and it is the reason
Gate 5 passes so much of this list.
