collapse is the finding.

every domain below is a Google-ranking domain. Nothing here is citation data.

## The two SERP families — this is the whole strategic finding

The vocabulary of the query, not the topic, decides who owns the SERP.

**Family A — "link / surprise / virtual gift / love letter / website".** Owned
end-to-end by single-purpose builders at or below SubhSandesh's authority. Typical
SERP: `gifft.me`, `qlovy.com`, `2-luv.com`, `surprises.gift`, `aiskyla.com`,
`giftsqr.com`, plus Pinterest and Reddit. **Weakness counts of 7-10 out of 8-10.**
This is where every opening in the shortlist is.

**Family B — "card / invitation / ecard".** Owned by established platforms:
`paperlesspost.com`, `canva.com`, `greetingsisland.com`, `americangreetings.com`,
`bluemountain.com`, `jibjab.com`, `punchbowl.com`, `adobe.com`, `evite.com`,
`123cards.com`, `moonpig.com`, `cardly.net`. **Weakness counts of 0-4.** Four of the
five Gate 4 cuts in this run are Family B queries.

The same searcher, the same product, two different words — and the difference between
a 9/9 weak SERP and a 0/10 one. `digital birthday card for girlfriend` is 0/10 weak;
`surprise link for girlfriend` is 7/8. Choose the vocabulary deliberately.

## What to study, and what not to link to

**Study `gifft.me`.** 43 of 61 SERPs is not an accident — it holds `virtual gift`,
`online surprise`, `surprise link`, `gift link`, `digital love letter` and
`virtual gift website` simultaneously, which means one site is covering the whole
vocabulary with dedicated pages per phrasing. That architecture is the lesson.

**Do not link to any of them.** Every Family A domain is a direct product competitor
to SubhSandesh, and the blog skill's `references/competitors.md` carries a do-not-link
list. Any post written from this shortlist must cite research sources, not these.
This is a sharper constraint than on previous runs: on the bouquet and gift-bf axes
the incumbents were retailers, which are safe to name; here they are substitutes.

## Two page-type warnings

1. **`github.com` took 8 of 10 results** on `website for girlfriend github` — only 2
   unique hosts parsed, below the 4-host floor, so no weakness verdict was recorded.
   It is a developer-repo SERP: the searcher wants source code. Gate 2 cut. And
   `website for girlfriend reddit` parsed **zero** hosts — an all-Reddit SERP.
2. **`play.google.com` / `apps.apple.com`** appear on `digital love letter app` and
   `digital invitation card maker online free`. App-store listings cannot be
   outranked by a blog post or a web template, and their presence is a signal that
   Google reads the query as "find me an app".
