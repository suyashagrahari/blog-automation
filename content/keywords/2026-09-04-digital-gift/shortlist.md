### How the clustering was done, and how uncertain it is

Phase 8a's rule is 3+ shared results in the top 10. Google now wraps every organic
result in an opaque `/goto?url=` redirect, so **destination URLs are not in the served
HTML** and results are identified by **host + page title** instead.

Two linkage methods, deliberately run as a bracket:

| Method | Clusters | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Single-linkage, only web-wide broad hosts discounted | **12** | over-merges by chaining: A~B, B~C puts A and C together |
| **Complete-linkage, hosts in >25% of SERPs discounted** | **40** | cannot chain; discounts `gifft.me` (43/61), `2-luv.com` (26/61), `qlovy.com` (23/61), `aiskyla.com` (20/61) as broad-authority-on-this-axis, per the `gates.md` note |

The 13 themes used throughout this run are built from the **strict** groups, merged
only where the strict run left obvious singletons of one theme.

**Stated caveat: 3 of the 13 themes — `virtual-gift-partner`, `surprise-link`,
`love-website-builder` — group keywords the strict run kept in separate clusters.**
Those three are the ones most likely to be more than one page each. The honest range
is that these 50 keywords are somewhere between 8 and 15 pages, and 8 is the number
this file recommends.

## Gate 2 — the warning that changes what you do with this list

**50 of the 61 measured SERPs are tool-page SERPs, not blog SERPs.** Positions 1-5 are
product pages that *build the thing*: `gifft.me`, `qlovy.com`, `2-luv.com`,
`lovepaper.app`, `yourlovepage.com`, `mewtrucard.com`. Only 10 are blog SERPs.

`references/gates.md`: a blog post wins a listicle or guide SERP; against tool pages
it "rarely" wins.

**So most of this shortlist should not go to `blog-optimisation`.** The right asset for
the four improve-existing themes and for `digital-love-letter`, `digital-card-partner`,
`propose-day-link` and `qr-code-gift` is a **template / landing page**. The genuinely
blog-shaped keywords are the ten marked `blog post` in `keyword-inventory.csv`, which
concentrate in `virtual-date-night` and `whatsapp-surprise`.

This is the opposite of the last three runs, where almost everything was a blog SERP.
Handing all 50 to the blog skill would produce posts aimed at SERPs a post cannot win.

## Method, and every limit on it

- **No Ahrefs, Semrush or Keyword Planner. There is no KD figure anywhere in this
  run.** The user asked for low-to-medium KD; what is here instead is a **weak-result
  count out of the page-1 results actually served**. Cruder than KD, different from
  it, and on long-tails generally more accurate. Do not read "8/8 weak" as difficulty.
- **Every volume is a band labelled `EST`**, placed from autocomplete `prefix_hits` on
  the *same thresholds the 2026-09-03 runs used* (≥22x → 1,000-3,000; ≥13x →
  300-1,000; else <300) so the runs stay comparable. **`prefix_hits` is ordinal demand
  from Google, never a monthly figure.**
- **Anchor:** `virtual gift for boyfriend`, 50x summed prefix-hits, the highest-demand
  row in the harvest, banded 1,000-3,000. Every other band sits relative to it.
- **61 of 62 SERPs measured**, all `gl=in&hl=en`. Google returned HTTP 429 at request
  41; the remaining 21 were measured after a ~35-minute pause. Nothing here is
  DuckDuckGo or Bing data, and no locale was mixed.
- **AI Overview fired on 45 of 61.** The Gate 6d CTR halving is applied to exactly
  those 45 rows and to no others.
- **Page-1 depth only** — 2 to 10 distinct results per SERP, mean 8.2. `own_position:
  none` means "not on page 1", not "not in the top 20".
- **No Search Console export. Sixth run in a row without it** — and it costs most
  here, because this axis is where the site is already densest. The 13 own-page
  rankings above were found by accident.
- **No `site:` pass.** With **325 of 975 live blog slugs already carrying an
  `online`/`digital`/`virtual`/`link`/`maker`/`free`/`send`/`app` token — 33% of the
  live blog** — `own_position: none` on the other 48 rows is the largest single
  inaccuracy in this file.
- **No Phase 5 answer-engine pass.** Nothing here is citation data; `competitor-map.md`
  describes Google rankings only. The five queries to run are in `serps.md`.
- **No Google Trends data.**
- **Expected clicks is a product of three estimates.** Its job is ordering, not
  forecasting.
- **Do not link to the incumbents.** Every Family-A domain is a direct product
  substitute for SubhSandesh, which is a sharper constraint than on the bouquet and
  gift-bf runs where the incumbents were retailers.
