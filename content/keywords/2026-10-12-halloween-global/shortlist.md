# Shortlist — Halloween (global), 500 keywords → 400 posts → 8 batches

Run `2026-10-12-halloween-global` · markets US, UK, CA, AU, IN · **33,448 live autocomplete rows** (6 harvests) · **38 SERPs measured** (Firecrawl, locale-served; 6 via WebSearch flagged lower-confidence) · 1 SERP-overlap test.

## Read this first — three hard truths

1. **Halloween 2026 is in 19 days (Sat 31 Oct).** Under Gate 0 a new page needs 6–12 weeks to rank. **None of these 400 posts can be expected to rank organically for Halloween 2026.** Every row is bucketed `next-cycle` with a `publish_by` date for Halloween 2027 (Sun 31 Oct 2027). Publishing now is still right — pages that are 12 months old by October 2027 have their best chance — but this October's traffic would come from distribution (Instagram/WhatsApp/Pinterest sharing of the pages and of `/happy-halloween`), not Google.
2. **2–4 million visitors in 20 days is not reachable through SEO with this list.** The whole 400-post set models to **~124,617 clicks/month at the October peak** (EST, gates.md formula: volume midpoint × 11% CTR at position 3 × win probability). Even that assumes position 3 on every post a year from now. Millions in 20 days needs viral distribution or paid traffic.
3. **No tool volume exists here.** Volumes are `EST` bands from live autocomplete demand (ordinal, not monthly searches). Halloween head terms in the US genuinely run to hundreds of thousands–millions in October, but the head SERPs are owned by Today, Reader's Digest, Good Housekeeping, IMDb, Adobe, retailers — measured, not assumed. The opening is the long-tail.

## What the evidence says to build

- **The romantic Halloween niche is the strongest opening, and it is SubhSandesh's product.** The highest cross-prefix demand in all 9,608 US suggestions is romantic: `halloween for boyfriend and girlfriend` (27), `halloween date ideas at home` (25), pickup lines (24–25), `halloween nicknames for boyfriend` (19). The romance SERPs are the weakest measured: **boo basket for boyfriend 10/10 weak, for girlfriend 9/10, ask someone to be your boo 9/10 (no page answers it), couple photoshoot 9/10, texts to crush 8/10, long-distance 8/10, bucket list 8/10.** `/happy-halloween` literally asks "Will you be my boo?".
- **Hard, measured:** halloween quotes (2/10 weak), captions head (4), puns (3), generic wishes (3, card brands), movies (3, IMDb/Variety/Netflix), couples costumes head (1 — retailer SERP, Gate 2), makeup (2). These stay in the set at their honest low win probability; their expected clicks are small for that reason.
- **Global angle (measured locale-served):** UK says *chat up lines* (31) where the US says pickup lines; AU says *dress ups* and searches *halloween quotes for whatsapp status* (33); CA searches *wishes in french*; IN searches *captions for whatsapp status* and *halloween celebration captions*. Country explainers (UK 6/10 weak, India 6/10 with the Bhoot Chaturdashi angle) are winnable.
- **Clustering cut the list honestly.** Measured: cute / funny / plain *halloween pickup lines* share 7+ URLs → one post. 28 adjective-only twins were folded into hub posts (`topics.pre-merge.txt` keeps the original). 500 keywords = 400 post primaries + 100 strongest absorbed variants; every post's full variant list is in `batches/batch-N.md`.
