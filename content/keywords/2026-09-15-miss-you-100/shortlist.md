## Read this before the numbers

**There is no tool volume in this run, and that is a real limitation.**
Ahrefs' free Keyword Generator endpoints that the 2026-09-08 run used
(`ahrefs.com/v4/stGetFreeKeywordGenerator*`) now return **404** — they have been
withdrawn. Keyword Surfer's free API (`api.keywordsur.fr`) is unreachable from this
machine. No Semrush or Keyword Planner access exists here, and **no Search Console
export was supplied — the seventh run asking.**

So every volume figure below is labelled `EST` and is an inference band, never a point
number. What the bands *are* grounded in:

- **15 live India SERPs**, measured this run, counted for weak results
- **5,979 India-geo autocomplete rows**, which give ordinal demand, not volume
- **Three observed live positions of our own pages** (#3 Kannada, #3 Kannada/appa, #9 Urdu)
- **Calibration carried from the 2026-09-08 run's 11 Ahrefs-priced rows**, where a
  regional-language "miss you … quotes" phrase priced `>1,000/mo` — that anchors the
  regional cluster at band 3, and everything else is placed relative to it

**The anchor is `miss you … quotes in <regional language>` = band 3 (1,000-3,000/mo).**

### Two things the autocomplete data got wrong, and the SERPs caught

1. **`prefix_hits` is not a volume proxy on this axis.** The highest-scoring rows in the
   whole 5,979-row harvest were the `yaad aa rahi ho …` translation phrasings (11, 10, 9
   prefix hits). Their SERP is **Bollywood song lyrics** — filmyquotes, gaana, YouTube,
   Scribd, TikTok. Zero greeting intent. The entire cluster is cut at Gate 1. This
   repeats the 2026-09-08 finding and should now be treated as settled.
2. **`miss you dp` / `miss you images` look like the best keywords in the set and are the
   worst.** 9 weak results out of 10 — the highest weakness score anywhere in this run.
   But the weakness is Pinterest, Unsplash, Adobe Stock and Vecteezy, i.e. it is an
   **image pack**, not a blog SERP. A blog post cannot enter it and a reader who wanted a
   JPEG does not convert. Gate 2 and Gate 5, not Gate 4. All 10 image/DP rows are cut.

### How to read `Exp. clicks/mo`

`expected_clicks = band_midpoint × CTR(target position) × win_probability`, with the
band, CTR and win-probability tables taken verbatim from `references/gates.md`. A `0`
means the row failed a gate — it is not a volume statement. Rows are **not** precise;
they exist to order the list, and two rows within ~30% of each other are a tie.

**Two-tier confidence.** `evidence_type = SERP-MEASURED` means a live SERP was pulled
for that cluster this run (14 clusters). `SERP-ARCHETYPE` means the row was scored from
a measured sibling cluster with the same structure — e.g. `miss you quotes in gujarati`
inherits from the measured Marathi and Kannada SERPs. Archetype rows are honest
inferences and are weaker evidence than measured rows. The column is in the CSV.

## The honest read

**1. You asked for high volume and medium KD. On this axis those two rarely co-exist in
English.** Every English head term measured — `miss you quotes`, `i miss you quotes`,
`missing you quotes`, `miss you status` — returns the same wall of US lifestyle
publishers with 2-3 weak results in 10. A low-authority Indian domain does not enter
that SERP by writing a better listicle. That is why 4 of your 10 Bucket B picks are cut
rather than ranked: ranking them would have been a nicer-looking sheet and a worse plan.

**2. The volume *is* there — it is just in Hindi, Marathi, Kannada, Urdu, Gujarati and
in the words "shayari" and "caption".** Those SERPs run 6-9 weak results out of 10, they
are held by Pinterest, Instagram, YouTube and abandoned blogspot pages, and **you already
rank #3 in one of them.** That is not a theory about winnability; it is a measured
position on a live SERP.

**3. The biggest single finding is a format mismatch, not a keyword.** 46 of your 112
live miss-you pages target the word *message*. India types *quotes* (545 demand rows),
*shayari* (218), *images* (270), *caption* (174). You have **zero** caption pages and
**five** quotes pages. Fixing that ratio is worth more than any individual keyword in
this sheet.

**4. Realistic ceiling.** The 83 surviving rows sum to roughly **7,000-8,000 clicks/mo**
if every page lands position 3, which they will not. A realistic 6-month read on the top
10 clusters is **1,500-2,500 clicks/mo**, and that number is an estimate built on
estimated bands — treat it as an order of magnitude, not a forecast.

**5. Was "miss you" the right seed?** Yes, but not the right *scope*. With 112 pages
already live this is a mature axis, and the returns now come from format coverage and
regional languages rather than from new relationship variants. The next seed worth
running is not another miss-you phrasing — it is `quotes` and `caption` as formats
across the axes you already own.
