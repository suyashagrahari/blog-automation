| **why rakhi on right wrist / significance** | 10,000–30,000 | G5 | Definition intent. Top of funnel at best. |
| **rakhi / raksha bandhan** (bare) | 100,000–300,000 | G1 | Head terms. Navigational, zero chance. |

**The arithmetic on that first row**, because the number is more persuasive than the
principle:

```
"raksha bandhan 2026 date"      50,000 searches × 0.1% = 50 conversions
"wishes with name and photo"     2,000 searches × 5%   = 100 conversions
```

The date keyword has **25× the volume and half the conversions.** That is the entire
argument for gating before sorting by volume.
