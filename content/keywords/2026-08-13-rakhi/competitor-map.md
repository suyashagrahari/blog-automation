## URL architecture of the top 3

**igp.com** — the topical authority. Hub-and-spoke: `/blog/category/rakhi/` is the
hub, spokes are `/blog/happy-rakhi-wishes-for-brother/`,
`/blog/raksha-bandhan-wishes-in-hindi/`, `/blog/rakhi-messages-for-siblings/`,
`/blog/rakhi-message-long-distance-brother/`,
`/blog/long-distance-rakhi-celebration-ideas/`,
`/blog/funny-raksha-bandhan-wishes-quotes-for-brother/`,
`/blog/why-rakhi-tied-on-right-wrist/`, plus `/rakhi/rakhi-frequently-asked-questions`.
Blog category list (fetched) shows ~30 festival categories — this is a
festival-content machine, not a rakhi specialist. Every post terminates in a product
table with prices (₹849–₹1475 rakhi hampers). **The blog exists to sell rakhis.**

**floweraura.com** — flat, two-branch: `/blog/<topic>` for guides and
`/quotes/raksha-bandhan/for-cousin` for the quote sets. The `/quotes/` branch is a
templated directory — one page per relationship — which is why it appears across so
many relationship long-tails at once.

**mynameart.com** — the archetype SubhSandesh is actually competing with. Category
hub at `/greeting-cards/raksha-bandhan`, one editor page per design at
`/create-card/<design-slug>` (e.g. `happy-raksha-bandhan-wishes-in-hindi-image`,
`raksha-bandhan-greeting-card-download`, `unique-rakhi-greeting-card`). Internal
anchors reveal the full cluster map: `bhai-dooj`, `birthday-wishes-with-photo`,
`anniversary-wishes-with-photo`, `month-baby-milestones`, `its-boy`, `its-girl` —
**the same occasion-per-page architecture SubhSandesh uses.** Its `<title>` still
says 2024. The whole site is one design pattern replicated across ~40 occasions.

## Google-only vs answer-engine-only

- **Both:** `igp.com`, `floweraura.com`, `rakhibazaar.com`, `adobe.com` — the real
  competitive set. Beating these needs either a better artifact or a gap they can't
  fill without cannibalising their own commerce.
- **Google only:** `canva.com`, `rakhi.com`, `postermywall.com`, `mynameart.com`,
  `createcustomwishes.com`, all Pinterest. **Every name-personalisation tool is in
  this group** — they rank and are never cited. Vulnerable to answer-engine
  displacement, and cheap to displace.
- **Answer engines only:** `nestasia.in`, `gladful.in`, `bablarakhi.com`,
  `tohfatales.com` — small gifting blogs cited for the long-distance questions
  without owning the Google top 3. They won citation with ordinary content on an
  under-served question. That is the cheapest pattern in the run to copy.

**The structural finding:** across every question-shaped rakhi query, the cited set
is *entirely* gifting-commerce blogs. **Not one tool page is cited anywhere.** There
is no incumbent tool to displace in the answer-engine layer — the slot is empty.

## What none of them cover

Every "how do I celebrate Raksha Bandhan when we're apart?" page resolves to
**courier a physical object**. Verified by fetching rakhibazaar's virtual-celebration
post: its own H2 is *"Send Your Rakhi & Gifts in Advance with Rakhi Bazaar"*, and its
idea list is "send sweets, send dry fruits, send a hamper, ship to the United
States". igp, nestasia, gladful, bablarakhi and tohfatales all do the same thing.

They cannot do otherwise. Their revenue is the shipment. A post that concluded
"here's a free digital thing you can send in 30 seconds" would cannibalise the
business that pays for the post.

**That is the gap, and it is structural rather than accidental** — which is what
makes it defensible. SubhSandesh's artifact *is* the answer those posts are
structurally unable to give. The name-personalisation sites that could give it
(mynameart, mynamepix, lifekafunda) don't write content at all — they publish editor
pages with 2021 and 2024 titles and no blog.

Nobody occupies the intersection: **a real digital artifact, plus content that
explains when to use it.**
