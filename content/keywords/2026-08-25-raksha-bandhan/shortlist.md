### 1. raksha bandhan 2027 wishes  ·  raksha bandhan 2027 quotes
- **Action:** new-page · **Bucket:** winnable · **Publish by:** 2027-06-22 — **but write it now, see below**
- **Expected clicks:** ~430/mo EST — 6,500 × 11% (pos 3) × 0.60. **Highest in the run.**
- **Est. volume:** band 4, 3,000–10,000/mo EST peak. Anchored against the 2026 equivalent, which is the same query one cycle earlier.
- **Currently ranking:** blog.bluestone.com (titled **2026**) · blissfulreads.com · iforher.com (titled **2026**)
- **Why winnable — the strongest evidence in this entire run:** the query returns **only six results, and not one of them is titled 2027.** BlueStone says 2026. iforher says 2026. vyapargrow says 2026. rakhibazaar carries no year at all. Google is substituting last cycle's pages because 2027 content does not exist. The one 2027-titled result is a *panchang* page (shrimahalaxmicalendar), which does not compete for wishes intent. **6 of 6 wishes results are stale-year or thin.**
- **Why write it now rather than at the publish_by date:** `publish_by` is the latest safe date. Publishing this month makes you the **only 2027-titled wishes page for eleven months**, accumulating age and links while every competitor's page still says 2026. This is the single clearest instance of Axis 9 (year freshness) in the run, and waiting until June 2027 throws the advantage away.
- **Page to build:** `/blog/raksha-bandhan-2027-wishes` — blog. Structure it to be re-datable annually rather than rewritten.
- **The 2026 twin:** `happy raksha bandhan 2026 wishes` and `raksha bandhan wishes 2026 for brother and sister` sit on an equally weak SERP (BlueStone ×2, **Vedantu — a tutoring company**, CaratLane, **BabyOrgano — baby products**, iforher; no Wikipedia, no panchang, no news). Both are bucketed **`next-cycle` on Gate 0 only** — the festival is 3 days away. They are the same cluster; the 2027 page is how you capture them.
- **Time to rank:** 8–12 weeks
