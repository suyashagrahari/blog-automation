## Read this first — the calendar

- **Navratri 2026 is 11–19 Oct. Today, 12 Oct, is day 2.** Dussehra is 20 Oct, and Sharad Purnima garba is 25 Oct.
- Under Gate 0 (gates.md), a new page needs **6–12 weeks** to rank. **None of these 96 keywords can be expected to rank organically before Navratri 2026 ends.** 95 of 96 are bucketed `next-cycle` (Navratri 2027 = **30 Sep – 8 Oct 2027**, Dussehra 9 Oct 2027).
- `publish_by` is the *latest* safe date, so **publishing now is still the right move.** A page live in Oct 2026 has 11 months of age and indexing behind it when the 2027 peak arrives. Anything it picks up in the last 8 nights of 2026 (long-tails with 8–9 weak results, Sharad Purnima on 25 Oct) is a bonus, not a forecast.
- **5–10 lakh visitors in 10 days is not reachable through organic search with this list.** The whole 96-keyword set models to **~8,099 clicks/mo at the 2027 peak** (EST, gates.md formula). Hitting lakhs in 10 days needs distribution: WhatsApp/Instagram sharing of the SubhSandesh pages themselves, Google Discover, paid ads. SEO cannot deliver that.

## The honest read
- **The niche is soft.** eduyush.com (titles still say 2025), flick.social, and scraped mirrors on .gov.br, .edu and onrender subdomains fill most SERPs. Stale years are everywhere. The only fresh 2026 incumbent is **hindutone.com**, which appears in about 14 of 41 SERPs.
- **The romantic angle is empty.** Wishes for girlfriend, wife, crush or long-distance partners, couple captions, and garba-partner quotes and shayari: no result on any of those SERPs targets them. This also matches SubhSandesh's own data, where 96.3% of pages are romantic.
- **Positions are US-served.** WebSearch cannot localise to India. Weak counts are directionally right but not India-verified. Volume bands are inference (`EST`), anchored on autocomplete ordinal demand, and labelled as peak-window rates.
- **Feedback loop:** no Search Console export was supplied (asked every run). Ask again around **2026-11-15**: whatever Batch 1 picks up in late-season impressions is the only true volume data this list will ever get.
