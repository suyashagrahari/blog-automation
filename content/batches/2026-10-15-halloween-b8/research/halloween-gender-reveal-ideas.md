## Phase 2: gap
- **Table stakes:** pumpkin, cauldron/potion, piñata, balloons, cake/candy, costume/team colours.
- **Gap:** none of the five cites any documented reveal fire or death; at least two actively recommend smoke bombs. None separates predicted sex from gender, none addresses intersex/DSD or discordant test results, none names who holds the secret per idea, none gives a sex-neutral option. Costs are vague or absent.
- **Fan-out:** what is a gender reveal / what does it reveal; is a smoke bomb safe; how to keep the secret; how accurate; cheap ideas; remote family; disappointment; neutral alternative.
