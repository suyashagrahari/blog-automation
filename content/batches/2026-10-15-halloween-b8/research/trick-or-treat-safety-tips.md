## Phase 2 — Gap

- Table stakes: reflective tape + flashlight, route planning, adults with under-12s, corners/crosswalks, inspect candy, porch lights, households clear paths.
- Gap: no page ranks the tips by the size of the risk, none cites a study, none admits that candy poisoning by strangers is unproven or that dog/trip advice has no evidence base, none covers tween phone/tracking plans or photo-sharing privacy.
- Stale data: the SERP cites none; "popular hours 5:30–9:30 pm" is undated.
- Fan-out: biggest danger, riskiest time, adults per child, reflective vs light colours, candy checking, tweens alone, masks, dogs, house prep, photos online.
- Angle: wins by being the only post that ranks trick-or-treat safety tips by measured risk and says where evidence is missing, using the 48.3% phone-open and 38.5% password rates for the photo tip.
