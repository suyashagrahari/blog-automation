## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes (from the letters): one warm line, safety/respect note, costume respect.
- Gap: no page offers teacher-to-student lines by grade band, by format, wording for non-celebrating students, or photo-privacy rules.
- Fan-out: board message, newsletter wording, LMS announcement, costume compliment, non-celebrating students, university tone, when to send, posting photos (FERPA / UK GDPR), how much praise.
- Angle: only post giving original teacher lines by grade band and format, sized by a 2023 inflated-praise experiment, plus FERPA/ICO photo rules.
