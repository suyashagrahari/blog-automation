## Phase 2: angle
This post is the only couples playlist sequenced by moment: dinner, costume prep, dance, then a slow-dance wind-down. Every recording year is checked on MusicBrainz, and the tempo bands come from a 282-table field experiment. It adds a couples music-and-stress study and the 48.3% phone-open figure.

Fan-out questions answered: how many songs; romantic but not scary songs; dinner music; building the list on Spotify without Premium; Apple Music for both partners; the last slow-dance song; whether Unchained Melody counts as a Halloween song; putting a song on a card; clashing tastes.

## Phase 3: sources (all fetched and verified)
1. Wuttke-Linnemann et al., *Scientific Reports* 2019, PMC6424968 (Springer Nature, CC BY), full text read. 40 heterosexual couples over 5 days with 6 assessments a day. Men had lower cortisol when women had listened to music, and the effect was stronger when the couple shared music preferences.
2. Malcman, Azar, Shavit, Rosenboim, *Behavioral Sciences* (MDPI) 2024, PMC11673941, full text read. 282 tables in total. Slow music (72 BPM or less) gave a mean stay of 80.3 min, fast music (94 BPM or more) 57.3 min, and the control 69.2 min.
3. Palmer, Yi, Smykovskyi, *Annals NYAS* (Wiley) 22 June 2026, PMC13284876, full text read. The review covers studies from 2011 to 2025. It says moving rhythmically with a partner can enhance bonding, and that married couples showed in-phase heart-rate and breathing synchrony during positive discussions. The licence is CC BY-NC, so the post cites the review and links to it without reproducing anything.
4. Spotify Support, "Start or join a Jam": Premium is needed to host. Free users can join and add songs. Phones within about 1 m can join.
5. Apple iPhone Guide, "Collaborate on a playlist": sharing needs an Apple Music subscription. Collaborators can add, remove, reorder and react. The owner can turn on Approve Collaborators.
6. Apple iPhone Guide, "Play music together using SharePlay": the host needs iOS 18+ and a subscription. Participants need iOS 18+ but no subscription.
