## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: costume contest, polls, behind-the-scenes, UGC, Reels/TikTok, countdown-style rhythm.
- Gap: no page gives a dated 31-day calendar with weekdays; no page checks contest ideas against platform rules (Facebook Pages Terms ban tag-a-friend / share-to-enter); none cites FTC prize disclosure; none cites engagement research on formats; accessibility (captions for sound-led Reels, flash limits) absent.
- Fan-out: what is a Halloween content calendar; what to post each day; which format performs; contest rules; alt text/captions; how many posts; when to start; what to post 1 Nov.
- Angle: wins by being the only post that turns the idea list into a dated 31-day format calendar backed by two engagement studies plus Meta/FTC contest rules, using the 48.3% phone-open rate and the lead-time analogue.

## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched)
1. Al-Khalifa et al. 2025, Acta Informatica Medica (Europe PMC PMC12858275) — 1,304 posts, 11 GCC dental school Instagram accounts, 12 months; Reels highest engagement; carousels 25.8%, Reels 17.4%. Full text read.
2. Harrison et al. 2026, JMIR Cancer (Europe PMC PMC13475934) — 306 Facebook posts; mean engagement 5.3 → 17.7 after multiformat strategy; photo 98.5% → 67.3%, text-dominant 1.5% → 32.7%, infographics 0 → 7.7%; no control for day of week/seasonality. Full text read.
3. Facebook Pages Terms (last revised 2018-03-08), facebook.com/policies/pages_groups_events — promotions clause E.3; content-collection consent clause.
4. 16 CFR 255.5 on eCFR (88 FR 48102, 2023-07-26) — "possibility of ... winning a prize" is a material connection.
