## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: storyline, puzzle ideas, locks, atmosphere, a finale/prize.
- Gap: nobody ships ONE complete room with every solution, a flow plan (linear vs parallel), a hint system, a costed kit, two difficulty tiers, or the never-lock-a-door rule (teachingideas does the opposite).
- Fan-out: how long; how many players; which puzzles; cost; hints; kids version; is it safe; two players.
- Angle: see batchMeta.angle.

## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched via Europe PMC REST full text)
