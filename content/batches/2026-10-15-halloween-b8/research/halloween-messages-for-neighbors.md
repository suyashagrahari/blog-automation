## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: short wishes for neighbors; "please don't knock" consideration; party advance notice.
- Gap: nobody sorts neighbor notes by purpose, gives delivery dates, states a real quiet-hours rule, or explains that the porch light is a convention (and that police safety advice can contradict it). No page offers a note for an elderly neighbor that makes "no" easy, or an apology note.
- Stale data: none cited by any ranking page.
- Fan-out: what to write; how to say you're not participating; does porch light off mean no; how early to warn about a party; what time a party must end; new-neighbor welcome; thanking a decorator; elderly neighbor; apologizing; texting vs paper; Halloween 2026/2027 dates.

Angle: wins by being the only post that sorts neighbor notes by job with dated 2026/2027 delivery windows, the Noise Act night-hours line and cohesion research, grounded in the 48.3% phone-open rate and the Raksha Bandhan lead-time analogue.
