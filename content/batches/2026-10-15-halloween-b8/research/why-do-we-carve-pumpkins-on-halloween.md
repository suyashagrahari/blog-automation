## Corrections to the prompt / brief
- "Hawthorne 1835 Feathertop" is wrong: Feathertop's pumpkin head is carved but unlit and the story is not from the 1830s; the 1830s jack-o'-lantern use is in "The Great Carbuncle" (Twice-Told Tales, 1837).
- Carved-lantern sense: Wiktionary 1834 vs OED 1837 (via Wikipedia); the OED was not read.
- The earliest printed Jack legends found (1836, 1851) carry a lantern; the coal and turnip are later additions, first printing not found.
