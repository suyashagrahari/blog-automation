## Phase 2 — Gap

- Table stakes: "no single right age"; a poll figure; a mention that some towns have laws; behaviour over age; etiquette.
- Gap: nobody quotes the current ordinance text. Every page repeats Chesapeake (some still say "over 12 / jail"), none notices that Norfolk, Virginia Beach, Hampton, Newport News and Portsmouth still cap it at 12 in their 2026 code supplements, or that Chesapeake's 1970 text carried up to six months in jail. Nobody gives the poll's method, or aligns the poll's "too old" ages against the legal caps. Nobody covers how to end it well (a last-year ritual) rather than whether.
- Stale data: TIME 2017 range; "73 million" Census 2020; FOX copy says Halloween is a Tuesday (2023).
- Fan-out: is there a legal age limit / which cities / what age do most people say / is 13, 14, 15, 16 too old / teen etiquette / how to tell a child / what if a child with a disability looks older.

Angle: wins by being the only post that quotes the current municipal code text for six Hampton Roads cities (five still at 12 in 2026 supplements; Chesapeake's 1970 jail clause vs its 2019 rewrite) and lines it up against the YouGov poll, using SubhSandesh's platform figures only for the keepsake-card section.
