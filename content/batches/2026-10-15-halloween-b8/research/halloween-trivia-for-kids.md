- Table stakes: easy/medium/hard tiers, answers shown, printable feel, origin and custom questions.
- Gap: no ranking page sources any answer; none bands by developmental age with a matching answer format (yes/no & pointing → MC → open); none covers animal science beyond "bats/owls are spooky"; none gives the real moon phase on 31 Oct 2026/2027; none bases hosting on learning research; two include slasher-film questions in a kids list.

- Fan-out: easy questions for 5-year-olds; is the moon full on Halloween; do owls hoot; is a spider an insect; did Halloween start in Ireland; scoring mixed ages; quiz length; what was carved before pumpkins; Halloween weekday 2026/2027.
- Angle: wins by being the only kids' Halloween trivia set that bands 40 questions by age and backs every answer with a named, dated source, including the actual 2026/2027 Halloween moon, using the 48.3% phone-view share to keep questions phone-short.
