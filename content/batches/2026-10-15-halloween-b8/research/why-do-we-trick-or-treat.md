- 1917 vs 1927: both right for different forms (plural 1917 Ontario; exact wording 1927 Alberta), per DCHP-3.
- Best & Horiuchi 1985: Social Problems, publisher Oxford University Press (Crossref); originally UC Press. Paywalled.
