## Phase 3 — sources (phenomenon: perceived vs actual similarity)
findpapers queries: "perceived similarity actual similarity romantic attraction couples", "similarity in preferences relationship satisfaction partners", "choices reveal values preference inference"; Europe PMC REST: "perceived similarity" AND couples OA, "similarity" AND "speed dating" OA, partner "preference prediction" OA.
1. Miculka & Imhoff 2026, Int Rev Soc Psychol (Ubiquity), europepmc PMC13089360, CC BY — abstract read via Europe PMC API: couples substantially similar; little support similarity predicts satisfaction; no convergence.
2. Tidwell, Eastwick & Finkel 2012, Personal Relationships (Wiley), doi.org link — abstract only (Crossref): ~12 dates of 4 min; perceived not actual similarity predicted romantic liking.
3. Dziura et al. 2023, SCAN (OUP), europepmc PMC10516339 — abstract: 60 participants; perceived similarity predicted self-reported interaction quality.
4. Meier et al. 2025, Innovation in Aging (OUP), europepmc PMC12199151 — abstract: 667 respondents 50+; >80% felt they knew partner's preferences; actual accuracy 35%–81%.
Rejected: Montoya et al. 2008 meta-analysis (SAGE JSPR, banned); 2025 JSPR scoping review (SAGE, banned); Baxter et al. 2022 PNAS (used in b7). None of the four appears in b1–b7.
