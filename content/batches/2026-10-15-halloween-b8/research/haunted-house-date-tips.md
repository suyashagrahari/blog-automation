## Phase 2 — gap analysis
- Table stakes: go on a weeknight / early, pick intensity, dress sensibly, use the fear as a bonding moment.
- Gap: nobody explains the touch vs no-touch split, how a posted safe word actually works (and where it doesn't), that couples are grouped with strangers, health warnings, or what to do with a partner who hates it. The adrenaline-attraction claim is repeated uncited.
- Stale data: every competitor is 2012–2018.
- Fan-out: do actors touch you; what is a safe word; quietest night; how long; will we be alone; good first date; partner too scared; who shouldn't go; why some love it; mood afterwards; how to ask.
- Angle: wins by being the only post that sorts the date by named attractions' posted touch and safe-word rules, adds recreational-fear research on mood and fan types, and puts the invite details where 48.3% of SubhSandesh pages are read.
