## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: ghosts, skeletons, bats, witches/brooms, black cats, spiders, owls, jack-o'-lanterns, harvest symbols (scarecrow, candy corn).
- Gap: no ranking page gives a date or a primary document for any symbol; all repeat Celtic-origin claims.
