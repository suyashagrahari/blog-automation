## Phase 2: gap
- Table stakes: a list of four-role ideas; something about coordinating.
- Gap: neither live page gives cost per person, a role split that avoids a "boring" part, a photo layout for four, or any evidence on why four works.
- Stale: both pages are 2017 and 2019; pop-culture picks with retail links.
- Fan-out: what are four-person costumes; how to split roles; cost per person; easiest last-minute; photo poses for four; what if one drops out; can one page serve the group.
