2. Europe PMC PMC13188394 — Zhong & Pan, *BMC Psychology* 14 Apr 2026: 67 children aged 4–5, 16 sessions / 8 weeks; self-control gains vs control except "consciousness"; percussion group reminded to stop when music ceases. Full text read. Teacher-rated, small n.
