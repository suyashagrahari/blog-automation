## Phase 2 — gap
- **Table stakes:** many crafts; paper plates, tubes, painted pumpkins; "easy", toddlers to tweens.
- **Gap:** none gives every craft an age band + minutes + mess score + which step needs an adult; none groups by mess tolerance; none gives carving-injury data or says when a child can carve; none explains paint labelling (AP vs CL vs ASTM D-4236); none addresses small parts for under-3s (abcdee's toddler crafts use googly eyes and pasta).
- **Stale data:** none cited at all.
- **Fan-out:** easiest crafts for 2-year-olds; low-mess crafts; age to carve a pumpkin; safe paint; googly eyes safety; recycling-bin crafts; session length for preschoolers; what to light a jack-o'-lantern with; sending a craft to grandparents.
- **Angle:** wins by being the only post that rates every craft by age, minutes, mess and adult step, backs "paint, don't carve" with NEISS injury data and the AAP, and explains the D-4236 label trap.

## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched)
1. **Lersilp et al. 2026**, Occupational Therapy International (Wiley, CC BY) — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC12932967 — full text read via Europe PMC JATS. 30 preschoolers 2–6, 5 age groups, 20 sessions / 10 h, 22/30 improved, biggest gains age 4; teachers flagged crayon size.
2. **Johnson et al. 2022**, Am J Emerg Med 60:83–87 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35930995/ — ABSTRACT ONLY. NEISS 2012–2021: 20,579 injuries; 87.6% hand; thumb 33.5%, index 25.0%; 10–19 31.5%, <10 19.5%; 45.6% last week of Oct; peak 30 Oct. (Europe PMC "PMC9490831" hit is the issue TOC, not the paper. Elsevier/sciencedirect at cap, so linked PubMed.)
3. **AAP HealthyChildren.org**, Halloween safety tips (updated 2026-09-29) — pumpkin decorating: small children decorate not carve, markers, adults cut, kids scoop; flameless candle / flashlight / glow stick; votive if candle.
4. **ACMI Materials Safety** — https://acmiart.org/materials-safety — AP seal for grade 6 or younger, toxicologist-certified; CL seal also labelled per ASTM D-4236, never for grade 6 or lower. (acmiart.org/index.php/... URL 404s.)
5. **16 CFR 1501** (eCFR) — small-parts rule for under-3s (36 months); §1501.3 exempts crayons, chalk, modelling clay, paint sets, paper articles. (cpsc.gov LHAMA page 403'd on fetch.)
