## Brief claims checked

- "FTC guidance on contests/sweepstakes": the FTC material fetched covers contest *disclosure* (hashtags). "No purchase necessary" and lottery rules are state law, so the body mentions them only as a state-law question and cites nothing for them.
- "verify seasonal-shopping timing (Think with Google / Census)": no US timing source was reachable within the 2-search budget without NRF. The Think with Google page found covers MENA 2022. The calendar rests on the ITAA pilot and the Raksha Bandhan analogue, and both are disclosed.
