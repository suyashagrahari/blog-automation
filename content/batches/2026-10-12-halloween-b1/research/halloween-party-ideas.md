- **Stale data:** none cited anywhere, so nothing to supersede; every number on our page is either sourced or labelled as an estimate.

- **Fan-out → H2s:** what makes a party work · timeline · food/drink per guest · cost for 10/20/40 · décor + fire risk · dry ice/allergies/getting home · SubhSandesh fit/limits.
