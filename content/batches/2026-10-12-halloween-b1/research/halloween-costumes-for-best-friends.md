## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched; full text read via Europe PMC REST unless noted)
1. Parkinson, Kleinbaum & Wheatley 2018, Nature Communications, PMC5790806 (CC BY) — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC5790806 — 279-student graduate cohort network; fMRI on 42; neural responses to movies most similar among friends and decline with social distance. Subject test: friendship similarity. Peer-reviewed, OA.
2. Rennung & Göritz 2016, Zeitschrift für Psychologie, PMC5137339 (CC BY-NC: cited and linked, nothing reproduced) — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC5137339 — 60 experiments; random-effects Hedges' g = 0.48 (95% CI 0.39–0.56); g = 0.30 with blinded experimenters; only k = 4 (attitudes) / k = 1 (behaviour) used participants who knew each other well, so familiarity cannot be tested. Caveat stated in body: synchrony is moving in time, not matching outfits.
3. Kikuchi et al. 2026, Brain and Behavior, PMC12848522 (CC BY) — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC12848522 — girls aged 9–10; friends n = 14 vs strangers n = 14; salivary OT rose after a facilitated drum circle in friends only; authors flag small sample, girls only. Exploratory; stated as such.
4. Lakkis, Lorenz & Mayers 2022, Optometry and Vision Science, PMC9553256 (CC BY-NC-ND, cited only) — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC9553256 — US contact lenses regulated by FDA as class II/III medical devices; patients who do not receive the lenses prescribed to them are at greater risk of adverse events, up to sight-threatening microbial keratitis.
