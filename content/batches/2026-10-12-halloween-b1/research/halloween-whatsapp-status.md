## Phase 2: gap analysis

- **Table stakes:** mood-sorted lists (spooky, funny, cute/sweet, short), puns, a few attributed classics.
- **The gap:** no ranking page explains the Status itself — 24-hour expiry, that every contact sees it by default, how to set a text Status's font and background colour, video/music limits, or that text Status cannot carry mentions. None has Hinglish lines. None addresses Indian readers who do not celebrate Halloween. None offers the "send to one person instead of everyone" option.
- **Stale data:** today.com and chatbooks are 2022 originals; no data cited anywhere.
- **Fan-out sub-queries:** what to post as a Halloween status; how long a status lasts; how to add a background colour; character limit for text status; video status length; can I add a song; can I hide it from family; Hinglish Halloween status; I don't celebrate Halloween status; how to send a Halloween message to one person.
- **Angle:** Wins by being the only post in the India top 10 that pairs 52 original Status-sized lines (8 Hinglish, 7 for people who don't celebrate) with WhatsApp's own published Status limits and steps, using SubhSandesh's 48.3% phone-open and 38.5% password-protect rates to separate a broadcast Status from a one-person message.

**Text-Status character limit:** the brief asked to verify it on faq.whatsapp.com. The Help Center publishes no character limit for text Status (searched the create, music, privacy and archive pages). The post says so instead of printing a figure; the lines are all kept under 60 characters.

**Fetch method note:** faq.whatsapp.com renders articles client-side; ctx fetch and Firecrawl both returned empty/unsupported. The article text was read through the r.jina.ai rendering proxy (Android and Web tabs), saved under scratchpad `halloween-whatsapp-status-jina-*.md`.
