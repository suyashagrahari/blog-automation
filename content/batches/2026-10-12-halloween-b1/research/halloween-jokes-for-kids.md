## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched; caps checked)
1. Paine et al. 2022, Developmental Psychology, CC BY — europepmc.org PMC9494906 (full text). N=110, dress-up play with siblings; McGhee stages: nonsense words from year 2, puns/riddles by ~7.
2. Mayseless & Reiss 2021, PLOS ONE, CC BY — europepmc.org PMC8654164 (full text). 35 children 6–8; preschoolers laugh because it makes no sense; resolution not before ~6 (Shultz 1976). Also cited by whosmarted (competitor) — primary source, allowed.
3. Jackson et al. 2021, JABA, CC BY — europepmc.org PMC8360189 (full text). 4 children 5–6.5 in McGhee's pre-riddle stage taught double-meaning jokes; all maintained.
4. Zipke 2008, The Reading Teacher — eric.ed.gov EJ817116 (abstract via ERIC API; page reset connection). Ages 7–9, riddles among most successful elements; riddle-writing procedures.
5. Zipke, Ehri & Cairns 2009, Reading Research Quarterly — eric.ed.gov EJ848119 (abstract via ERIC API). N=46, M 8y7m; gain on WRMT-R, not GMRT4.
