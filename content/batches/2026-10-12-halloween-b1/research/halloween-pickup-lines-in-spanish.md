## Brief claims found false or unverified

- "tirar los perros" as Mexico usage: DEM lists "echarle los perros".
- Argentina street-harassment law: not verified on an official source within budget; omitted from the post.
- Day of the Dead "1–2 Nov" (BRIEF §1): consistent with INAH (1 and 2 November feasts); UNESCO's page says end of October to beginning of November.
- Prompt allowed UNESCO 'if not over cap': its URL was already in 2 sibling posts, so INAH was used instead.
- Popular framing of Día de Muertos as pre-Hispanic: INAH's own essay says the 1-2 November feasts are European in origin.
