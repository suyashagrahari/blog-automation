## Phase 2 — gap analysis

- **Table stakes:** two lines facing, sticks in both hands, strike with partner, rotate partners, eye contact, light grip.
- **The gap:** no ranking page writes the pattern as counts, gives a tempo, or explains *why* eye contact works. Nobody names stick length/material. Nobody gives the effort level.
- **Stale data:** gulfnews 2013, hindupad 2013.
- **Fan-out sub-queries:** how to hold dandiya sticks; basic dandiya steps with partner; 2-clap vs 4-clap; how partners rotate; dandiya song speed; why you keep missing the partner's stick; how tiring is dandiya; hitting knuckles; left-handed partner; what length stick; how to ask someone to be your dandiya partner.
- **Angle:** Wins by being the only dandiya partner steps page that writes the 2-clap and 4-clap patterns as count tables tied to measured song tempos (90–126 BPM) and strike rates, with the eye-contact advice backed by visual-entrainment research instead of asserted.
