## Phase 2 — gap analysis

- **Table stakes:** the greeting नवरात्रीच्या हार्दिक शुभेच्छा, short WhatsApp-ready lines, status lines, Durga/देवी blessings.
- **The gap:** no ranking page mentions भोंडला/हादगा, अंबाबाई (Kolhapur) or तुळजाभवानी; none explains नवरात्र vs नवरात्री or शुभेच्छा vs शुभकामना; none dates 2026/2027 correctly; digit.in calls Navratri a Bengali festival. Nobody explains where Mumbai's nine colours came from.
- **Stale data:** digit.in and tring's colour table are 2024.
- **Fan-out sub-queries → H2s/FAQs:** which form is correct (नवरात्र/नवरात्री); Hindi vs Marathi phrasing; Ghatasthapana wishes; Bhondla wishes; Ambabai/Tulja Bhavani wishes; Dasara wishes; nine colours (why, which this year); when to send; status lines; inviting one person to garba; group invitations.
- **Angle:** wins by being the only navratri-wishes-in-marathi page organised around Maharashtra's own Navratri moments, each sourced, with timing anchored on SubhSandesh's 48.3% phone-view share and the Raksha Bandhan 49.4% same-day analogue.
- **Sibling separation:** navratri-wishes-in-hindi is organised by recipient pronoun and tithi; this page is organised by Maharashtrian ritual moment (Ghatasthapana → Bhondla → Ambabai → Tulja Bhavani → Shilangan). No /blog/ links.
