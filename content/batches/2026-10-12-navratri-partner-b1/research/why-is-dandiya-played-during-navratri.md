## Phase 2: gap analysis

- **Table stakes:** Durga vs Mahishasura over nine nights; garba vs dandiya difference; sticks as swords; Krishna raas; garba around a lamp-pot (garbha deep).
- **The gap:** every page states the sword story and the Krishna story as fact, and none cites a scholar. None says the historical record is thin. None explains why people say dandiya is *played* (Gujarati ramvu). None gives the documented order of a night with a source. None gives the mechanics: even number, 12-18 inch sticks, 8-beat kaherva. None covers the diaspora with dates (the 1970s Uganda expulsion) or Mumbai's stage nights (Falguni Pathak).
- **Stale data:** two results are 2025-stamped news; heraldgoa dates to 2015.
- **Fan-out sub-queries → H2s:** what is dandiya raas vs garba; why Navratri / why Amba; are the sword/Krishna stories true; when in the night; how it spread; religious or party; how to ask a partner.
