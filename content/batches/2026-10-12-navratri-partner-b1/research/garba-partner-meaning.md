## Phase 2: gap analysis

- Table stakes: garba is a Gujarati circle dance for Navratri around a lamp/garbo; dandiya raas is the stick dance with partners.
- The gap: nobody defines the *phrase*. Nobody separates the circle role, the raas role, the slang date sense and the paid rental sense. Nobody explains why the slang exists (ticketed, named, nine-night passes priced by gender).
- Stale data: utsavpedia 2013. Nothing current on the phrase.
- Fan-out sub-queries → H2/FAQ: what does it mean; do you need a partner for garba; garba vs dandiya partner; is it a boyfriend/girlfriend; garba buddy; is rent-a-garba-partner real; why passes are priced by gender; how many nights; asking a best friend; can SubhSandesh find one (no).
- Angle: wins by being the only post that defines the phrase rather than the dance, separating four senses and grounding the slang in a measured pass economy (40,000 dancers a night; 6:1 gender price ratio at one Vadodara ground).
