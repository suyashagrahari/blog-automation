- Table stakes: date, time, venue, dress code, RSVP.
- Gap (none of top 10): which WhatsApp surface to use and its real limits; a timed multi-message sequence; dandiya-only lines (sticks, borrow, pass pickup, kids' slot, plus-one); link preview behaviour; RSVP chasers.
- Stale: tring's 2025 dates.

- Angle: wins by writing a message per WhatsApp surface against WhatsApp's own published limits, timed by first-party lead-time data.
