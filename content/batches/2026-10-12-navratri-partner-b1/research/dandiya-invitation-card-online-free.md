## Phase 2 — gap

- Table stakes: templates, the event details (date, time, venue, dress code), sharing on WhatsApp/Instagram.
- The gap: none of the returned pages compares the formats (image vs video vs text vs interactive link) on cost, watermark, export, group size and RSVP, and none separates the society/group invite from the one-person invite. None states watermark terms from the tool's own page.
- Stale data: smartpuja "2023" (now dead); onrender mirror "2021".
- Fan-out sub-queries → H2s: what is an online dandiya invitation card; which maker; cost/watermark/export side by side; which for a society of 200; which for one person; how late can I make it; when the interactive link is wrong.

**Angle:** This post wins by being the only one that compares image, video, text and link invitations on watermark, export and RSVP using each tool's own pricing page, and that tells a 200-guest society to skip the one-to-one link, using SubhSandesh's 38.5% password and 48.3% phone-open rates.

## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched; verification noted)

1. Garimella & Eckles (2020), HKS Misinformation Review — https://misinforeview.hks.harvard.edu/article/images-and-misinformation-in-political-groups-evidence-from-whatsapp-in-india/ — full essay read. >5,000 public political WhatsApp groups in India, >5 million messages, >250,000 users, Oct 2018–Jun 2019; "a large fraction of the messages shared on WhatsApp are images". Caveat stated in body: political groups.

Koçak & Yüksek Vergiveren (2019), OJCMT 9(4) e201922 — https://www.ojcmt.net/article/group-based-communication-contents-and-practices-of-whatsapp-group-use-by-generations-and-genders-5900 — open access, **abstract read**. n=633 (Turkey); 52.2% share chain messages (caricatures, videos, photos, celebration messages).
3. Milkman, Beshears, Choi, Laibson & Madrian (2011), PNAS — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC3127912 — **abstract read** via the Europe PMC REST API (the article page itself returns 403 to fetchers). Control 33.1%; date+time prompt +4.2 pp (significant); date only +1.5 pp (not significant).
4. Hennrich, Arnold & Wensing (2021), BMC Med Res Methodol — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC8590365 — CC BY, **abstract read** via Europe PMC REST (the BMC page served a client challenge). n=757 personalised vs 754 generic; 2.8% vs 2.4%; RR 1.17 (95% CI 0.62–2.21), not significant.
