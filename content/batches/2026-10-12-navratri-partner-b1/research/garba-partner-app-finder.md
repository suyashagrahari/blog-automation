### Google Play: search "garba partner", hl=en_IN gl=IN

Three Navratri partner finders appeared among garba-song apps and unrelated "partner" business apps.

1. **Garba Partner: Find Your Group** (com.intechualsolutions.garbapartner, InTechual Solutions, "Made in Ahmedabad"). 1+ downloads, 6 reviews, 5.0, rated 12+, contains ads, in-app purchases, updated 9 Oct 2026. Claims: city/venue/level/style matching, mobile-verified accounts, auto-screened photos, clean-language chat filter, report/block, a "Women only" request setting, adults 18+. "Garba Partner Plus" is an optional subscription that gives chat, unlimited requests ("free members get 5 a day"), filters and no ads. The data label says it *shares* approximate location (analytics, advertising, fraud), crash logs, diagnostics, device IDs and app interactions. It *collects* photos (optional), other in-app messages (optional), purchase history (optional), approximate location, name, user IDs, phone number, other info, device IDs, app interactions and other user-generated content. Encrypted in transit; deletion available. Three visible reviews, all dated 8 Oct 2026: "Great app…", "Garbage, partner is like a dance.", "Very nice app."
2. **Partner Community: Garba Meet** (app.partner.community, dev "AI Apps and Games"). 10+ downloads, rated 12+, in-app purchases, no ratings shown, updated 7 Oct 2026. Claims: dancer filters in Surat, Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Rajkot, Mumbai; matching by level and favourite ground; double opt-in, with Instagram revealed only on mutual approval; "Duration-based event partner requests (1 Hour, 4 Hours, Full Night)"; a "Fair Credit-based request system"; recharge balances. The data label says no third-party sharing. It collects crash logs, diagnostics, photos, name, phone number, purchase history and device IDs. Encrypted; deletion available.
3. **GarbaSathi** (com.clonosys.garbasathi, MINDRIPPLE TECHNOLOGIES PVT LTD). 1+ downloads, rated 18+, no ratings shown, updated 6 Oct 2026. Claims: upload a selfie and add a location or area so people nearby can discover you; connection requests; after a mutual connect, exchange Instagram IDs and contact details. It advises meeting at a public venue and telling someone you trust. The data label says "doesn't collect or share any user data". **This conflicts with Google's definition of "collect" (data transmitted off device).** The body says the label and the description cannot both be right. It does not accuse the developer of anything.

Play search "garba dating" returned general dating apps (happn, Mingle2, Jaumo and others) plus garba-song apps. "dandiya partner" returned only delivery and driver "partner" apps. "garba buddy" returned garba songs and a garba-pass app.

No app was installed. Download bands, review counts and age ratings are as displayed on the day and will change. No rating or download number is invented.
