## Brief claims checked

- "Dandiya, unlike garba, structurally needs a partner": **partly false**. Vatsyayan (via Parmar fn 311) records older Indian dandiya as often a solo dance with one's own sticks; the USC informant says garba dancers also keep switching partners. The body states both.
- "Partners rotating down the line every few beats": sourced as moving two places per eight-beat cycle (David via Parmar); the USC informant says "the next person". The body says "every eight beats".
