## Phase 2 — gap

- Table stakes: "victory of good over evil", Ravan/Rama + Durga/Mahishasura, wishes for family / friends / colleagues, short WhatsApp lines.
- **Gap (none of the 9 cover it):** (1) the 2026 regional date split — Drik Panchang lists Bengal Vijayadashami Wed 21 Oct and the Mysuru palace calendar keeps Vijayadashami + Jumboo Savari Wed 21 Oct, a day after Delhi's Tue 20 Oct; (2) what each tradition actually *says* (Shubho Bijoya after immersion, pranam/kolakuli; apta leaf as "sona"; Ayudha Puja at work; Kullu starting on Vijayadashami); (3) any native-script lines; (4) any 2026 or 2027 date at all.
- Stale data: every dated competitor gives a 2021–2024 date.
- Fan-outs → H2/FAQ: what is Dussehra; wishes per tradition; dates 2026/2027; wishes for family/colleagues/friends; when to say Shubho Bijoya; apta words; Ayudha Puja for colleagues; one broadcast vs per-region; when to send; Kullu.

**Angle:** wins by being the only post that sorts Dussehra wishes by the tradition the recipient keeps, with each region's verified 2026 date, original lines in five languages, and the 49.4% on-the-day lead-time analogue.

## Brief claims checked

- BRIEF §1 "Vijayadashami / Dussehra 2026: Tue 20 Oct" — true for New Delhi, but **Drik Panchang itself lists "Bengal Vijayadashami" on Wed 21 Oct 2026 (Sun 10 Oct in 2027), and the official Mysuru palace calendar keeps Vijayadashami / Jumboo Savari on Wed 21 Oct 2026**. A single 20 Oct date is wrong for Bengal and Mysuru.
- BRIEF §1 "Durga Ashtami and Maha Navami … SAME day, Mon 19 Oct" — true on Drik Panchang New Delhi, but the Mysuru palace calendar puts Mahanavami / Ayudha puja on Tue 20 Oct, and Drik Panchang's own Navratri table lists Ayudha Puja on Tue 20 Oct. Siblings writing "Ayudha Puja 19 Oct" would be wrong.
