## Phase 2 — cannibalisation check (the reason this brief exists)

Both siblings named in the assignment were read in full before a line was drafted.

### `will-you-be-my-girlfriend-proposal-page-online` (wave 2, 2026-09-17)

### Where this post differs — stated honestly

**Overlap that is real and must be acknowledged.** Both siblings already carry a
"what goes on the page" list. A third additive checklist of page sections would have been a
duplicate, and the assignment's stated differentiator ("what sections a proposal page needs")
is, on its own, **already covered**.

**What is left that is genuinely new**, and what this post was built on instead:

1. **The list is a ranking, not an inventory.** The seven entries are ordered by
   *personalisation load* — how much each slot changes whether the page reads as written for
   one person — ending on "the theme, the colours and the animation", explicitly the lowest
   and explicitly to be left alone. Neither sibling ranks anything.
2. **The fields are a diff against the template, not a build list.** Every entry answers
   *what the template already decides* vs *what only you supply* — a split neither sibling makes.
3. **"What to cut" is a field.** Both siblings are purely additive; neither tells the reader
   to delete anything. Six of the seven cuts here are subtractive instructions.
4. **The spine is the template objection itself** — *does it still count if I did not start
   from nothing?* — answered with mass-customisation and handmade-effect research. Sibling 1
   cites the IKEA effect only in reverse, as a warning that effort does not buy an answer.
   No incumbent and no sibling addresses perceived uniqueness.
5. **A freshly measured SERP category split** (posters vs repositories vs fillable pages) that
   neither sibling could have: sibling 1 measured the *business-vs-romance* split instead.

**Verdict: distinct, but adjacent.** The distance comes from the ranking, the fixed-vs-yours
split, the cut field and the research lane — not from the topic, which genuinely is close to
sibling 2. If this batch later decides the cluster is over-split, this is the page to drop,
not either sibling, because both of them are load-bearing for phrasings this one does not own.

### Cross-links instead of competition
`/love-gf` and `/proposal` carry the two asks. The girlfriend-vs-marriage question is
resolved in two sentences and handed to the template, not re-covered — sibling 2 owns it.

## Phase 3 — sources

Lane: **mass customisation and perceived uniqueness — whether something built from a template
still feels personal.** Terms queried through `findpapers.mjs` (Crossref + Europe PMC):
`mass customisation perceived uniqueness study`, `IKEA effect self assembly valuation study`,
`customisation product attachment research`, `co-creation consumer value perceived uniqueness`,
`I designed it myself effect mass customization`, `product uniqueness driver customer utility
mass customization`, `personalisation perceived effort recipient gift`,
`configurator choice overload mass confusion customization`,
`constraint creativity structure generative benefit study`,
`value increment of mass-customized products empirical assessment`,
`self-designed product pride of authorship willingness to pay`.
OA locations resolved through the Unpaywall API rather than guessed.

| # | Source | Subject test | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Franke & Schreier, *Marketing Letters* 19(2), 2008 — WU Vienna repository | Pass | Perceived uniqueness of a self-designed product adds to utility **independently** of aesthetic and functional fit; moderated by need for uniqueness. This is the direct answer to "does template-built still count". |
| 2 | Fuchs, Schreier & van Osselaer, *Journal of Marketing* 79(2), 2015 — mediaTUM repository | Pass | Handmade products are read as symbolically "containing love"; effect stronger for gifts to loved ones than distant recipients. The honest counterweight — a filled template is not handmade. |
| 3 | Raghoebar, van Kleef & de Vet, *British Food Journal* 119(6), 2017 — Wageningen repository, CC BY | Pass | IKEA effect **failed to replicate** (n=86 children) once exposure and crafting were disentangled; pride mediated nothing. Stops the post claiming that assembly alone makes a thing better. |
| 4 | *BMC Psychology*, 9 May 2026 (n=291) | Partial — adjacent domain, same mechanism | Perceived authenticity carried 44.6% of the total effect on acceptance of products labelled AI-designed. Used only in the "wrong choice" section, explicitly flagged as a different domain. |

All four are peer-reviewed. #3 is CC BY open access; #1, #2 and #4 are open records whose
**abstracts only** were read — disclosed in the body sources list and in the audit.

Crowded publishers were avoided deliberately per the BRIEF's source-cap race note:
`frontiersin.org`, `pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov`/`europepmc.org`, `journals.plos.org`, `arxiv.org`,
`sagepub`, `wiley`, `springer`, `nature.com`, ERIC, `archive.org`, `dsal.uchicago.edu`,
`universaldependencies.org`. Three of the four chosen sources are **institutional
repositories** (`research.wu.ac.at`, `mediatum.ub.tum.de`, `research.wur.nl`), none of which
appears in waves 1–2 or elsewhere in this batch.

`capcheck.mjs` was run before choosing sources and again immediately before saving the JSON.
No chosen URL appears in the 194-URL wave 1–2 ban list; no chosen domain is at or near cap.

**Deliberately not cited:** the Norton, Mochon & Ariely IKEA-effect paper. The wave-1 sibling
already cites it (`hbs.edu/ris/Publication Files/11-091.pdf`, banned), and the rule is that a
reused domain needs a *different paper* — so the effect is introduced only through the
Wageningen replication attempt, which states the definition itself.

**Swap test:** none of the four could sit in another keyword's post in this batch. They are
about self-design, production mode and authenticity — no use at all in a quotes, wishes,
shayari, language or occasion post.

**Generic context statistics: zero.** No PIB, TRAI, Census or MEA figure appears.

## Known limitations

1. No proposal-specific first-party data exists; the `proposalpages` collection read is
   refused by this machine's production-read policy. Every figure is platform-wide across
   20 page types or sampled on `/apology-dashboard`, and says so in prose.
2. The cost section of `facts.md` is empty, so the post makes no cost claim whatsoever.
3. Three of four sources were read as abstracts only; publisher full texts were not open
   to automated retrieval.
4. Source #4 is from an adjacent domain (AI-designed museum products), used for mechanism
   rather than for a transferable rate.
5. The SERP was measured once, on 2026-09-23, as category counts only. No individual
   competitor page was fetched, so the post describes no competitor's content.
