### Honest verdict on whether this page should exist

**Yes, but with a named residual risk.**

The genuine difference is not the recipient label — it is that the sibling's
girlfriend group is two lines inside a routing page, and the register argument is
absent from it entirely. The sibling explains *which pronoun* to use. It does not
explain *why the Hindi a young Indian man has heard romance conducted in is
unusable in a message*, and it publishes no film-studies source. That argument
needs a whole page, it is specific, it is citable, and it changes which lines you
would publish — which is the real test: this page's ten lines are not a superset
or a reshuffle of the sibling's ten, they are a different set chosen by a different
rule (plain over aphoristic), and one candidate line was cut *because* it was too
close to the sibling's `चुन रहा हूँ`.

The residual risk is real and should be stated to the user: both pages are
"Propose Day + Hindi + wishes", both are on `modern-romance`, and Google may treat
them as one cluster. The mitigation applied is a one-way contextual link from this
page to the sibling by slug, with the sibling framed as the routing page for every
other recipient, and no re-coverage of spouse/friend/sibling/group here.

## The Hindi: how every line was verified

Three instruments, all fetched, none cited as a source:

1. **McGregor, *The Oxford Hindi-English Dictionary*** via the Digital Dictionaries
   of South Asia headword query
   (`dsal.uchicago.edu/cgi-bin/app/mcgregor_query.py?qs=…&searchhws=yes`).
   **Every content word** in every drafted line was looked up and its attested
   sense read.
2. **Universal Dependencies Hindi HDTB** (train + dev + test, ~350k tokens),
   indexed locally into a form→UPOS+FEATS table (19,088 distinct forms).
3. **Tatoeba Hindi sentence export** (16,475 sentences), because HDTB is newswire
   and — this is a real finding — contains **almost no second-person familiar
   address at all**: `तुम्हें`, `तुमसे`, `आपसे`, `तुझे`, `रहोगी`, `बनोगी` and
   `चाहोगी` are all absent from HDTB, while Tatoeba has 375, 41, 23, 9, 1, 0 and 3
   occurrences respectively. A treebank built from news cannot verify the register
   this page is written in.

### Dictionary findings that changed the copy

- **खास / ख़ास.** The McGregor headword `खास` (no nukta) resolves to *"f. reg. (W.)
  a square-cut bag; a load, bagful"* — **not** "special". The intended word is the
  Perso-Arabic ख़ास with a nukta, and that form breaks the DSAL query interface
  ("The transliteration code encountered a problem"). One drafted line used it and
  was **cut**. This is the same class of error as the Kannada agent's ಬಾಳು
  ("a knife", not "life").
- **बहाना** is two homographs: `बहाना¹` v.t. "to cause to flow", `बहाना²` m.
  "excuse, pretext". Line 1 uses sense 2, confirmed.
- **शायरी** = "the art or practice of poetry; poetry" — confirmed, and used
  deliberately in line 2 as the thing being declined.
- **सीधा** = "straight, direct … straightforward; plain, simple; honest, candid" —
  confirmed, which is what licences "सीधी बात" and "एक सीधा सवाल".
- **दोस्त** is m. in McGregor ("a friend"; also "a lover, sweetheart"), and the
  collocation `अच्छी दोस्त` with feminine agreement occurs **0 times** in either
  corpus. The line that needed it was **cut**.
- **मुबारक**, **शुक्रिया**, **प्यार**, **सवाल**, **आसान**, **बिताना**, **सच**,
  **जानना**, **पूछना**, **रहना**, **चाहना**, **बनना**, **अच्छा**, **साथ**,
  **भरोसा** — all confirmed as headwords with the sense used.

### Corpus findings on inflection

- `तुम` + future takes **-ोगे** (male addressee) / **-ोगी** (female addressee).
  Attested `-ोगी` forms in Tatoeba: करोगी (5), चाहोगी (3), पहनोगी, कहोगी, गिरोगी,
  तैरोगी. Attested `-ोगे`: करोगे (19), चाहोगे (2), रहोगे, बनोगे, चलोगे and seven more.
- **रहोगी** is directly attested, in this exact frame: *"तुम मेरे साथ रहोगी।"*
- **चाहोगी** is directly attested with the same postposition: *"मेरे साथ नाचना चाहोगी क्या?"*
- **बनोगी** is **not** directly attested. It is published anyway, and this is the
  one place the page composes a form: `बनोगे` is attested for this exact stem
  (*"तुम क्या बनोगे?"*) and the `-ोगी` feminine ending is attested on six other
  verbs, so the form is paradigm-regular rather than guessed. Flagged in
  `structuralLimitations`.
- **पूछूँगा** is attested (*"फिर मैं उससे उसके बारे में कल पूछूँगा।"*) but was
  removed from the final line anyway, because it is sender-gender-marked and the
  page's promise is that one form works for either sender.
- Constructions checked as collocations: `हो जाता है` (TAT 6 / HDTB 10),
  `नहीं पड़ता` (9/2), `अच्छा लगता` (51/1, incl. *"खेलना अच्छा लगता है"* — the exact
  nominalised-subject pattern of line 8), `किए बिना` (0/6), `बात करना` (27/4),
  `हाँ कहा` (1/1), `कहने का` (1/1), `सीधी बात` (0/1), `तुम्हारे साथ` (28/0),
  `मुझे तुमसे` (6/0), `मिल गया` (3+/196).
- **गर्लफ्रेंड** is an English loan; it is attested in Devanagari in Tatoeba
  (*"टॉम उसकी गर्लफ्रेंड के बारे में …"*) and is not a McGregor headword. The post
  says so in the body.
- Sender-gender neutrality was checked line by line: **no published line carries a
  first-person verb whose ending changes with the sender's gender.**
