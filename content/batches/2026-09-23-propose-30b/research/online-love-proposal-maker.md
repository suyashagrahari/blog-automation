## Phase 3 — sources (lane: defaults and choice architecture)

Lane assigned and stayed in: **how a pre-set option shapes the final result**. Explicitly
avoided (taken by siblings): mass customisation / IKEA effect, digital possessions, privacy
calculus, mediated co-presence, anticipation, ritual, script theory, online relationship
formation, mobile reading, link trust, planning fallacy.

`findpapers.mjs` queries run: `default effect choice architecture study`,
`status quo bias defaults research`, `meta-analysis default effects nudge`,
`pre-set options user behaviour research`. Crossref returned mostly SSRN and AEA registry
stubs; Europe PMC returned clinical nudging work. Neither surfaced a usable open copy in the
core defaults literature, so the final set was assembled by fetching candidate repository and
author-hosted copies directly and verifying each.

**Europe PMC / `pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov` is at the 3-post cap and was deliberately avoided**, as
were `frontiersin.org`, `journals.plos.org`, `dsal.uchicago.edu` and `archive.org`.
