# Halloween templates — what they actually do (source: each live page's own FAQPage + HowTo JSON-LD, fetched 2026-10-12, re-verified 2026-10-13)

Describe ONLY these features. Anything else is invented. Both are `isAccessibleForFree: true` per their CreativeWork schema; neither needs an app or an account for the recipient.

## /happy-halloween — "Interactive Halloween Card with Photos & Music" (HowTo totalTime PT5M)
- Create: their name and yours, a letter, **up to six photos**, the word to carve on the pumpkin — or describe the two of you and let the AI write it. Save to preview, Publish, send the private link (WhatsApp, Instagram, text). Opens in any phone or laptop browser.
- The recipient walks through a little haunted house: **knocks three times**, chooses trick or treat and catches falling sweets, **carves a jack-o'-lantern** until the candle lights and their word glows, wipes fog off portraits to find your photos, **reads your letter by flashlight** in a dark attic, **brews a potion** from your memories (3–5 ingredients you list), and answers the question at the end. Ends with a **Boo License**.
- Question: **"Will you be my boo?"** by default (editable, up to 60 characters). The No button runs away a few times, so the answer is always yes. **You get an email the first time they answer**, with how many times No "ghosted" them first.
- **Who it's for: partner, bestie, family or kids** — the question and licence change (e.g. "Will you be my boo-st friend forever?" for a best friend, "Will you be my favourite little pumpkin?" for family).
- **Kids tone:** spooky-cute, round blushing ghosts, no blood, no gore, no jump scares; trick becomes confetti spiders, nothing goes dark, the No button waits as "maybe later?". Reduced motion respected.
- Music: paste a **YouTube link** (audio only, optional start time) or pick one of **eight Halloween favourites such as This Is Halloween, Monster Mash or Thriller**, or the built-in spooky music box. Music starts after they knock.
- **Boo License:** a **1080×1920 (Instagram Story size)** card with their photo, the costume you chose for them (ghost, witch, vampire, pumpkin, black cat, skeleton, mummy or bat), your reason, and "Valid every 31 October, forever"; downloadable PNG, with a "Boo someone back" option.

## /happy-halloween-premium — "Cute Halloween Website" / "Boo-tiful Night" (HowTo totalTime PT10M)
- A ghost pops out of a haunted mailbox with your letter and your song starts on the first tap. Candies from a trick-or-treat bag (each a sweet line or a cute dare), a pumpkin that lights up with your initials, ghosts hiding your photos, a love potion from three memories, your letter delivered by a bat, a spirit radio playing your voice, then "Will you be my boo?" — on yes the song hits the chorus, the night turns pink, the moon becomes a heart and bats make a heart around your photo.
- Premium differences (its own FAQ): **voice note up to 60 seconds**, **up to 12 photos**, **letter up to 3,000 characters**, a sealed P.S., an **optional unlock time** (until then the link shows only their name and a countdown), a **reply box** (one reply up to 280 characters, emailed to you), chorus-timed big moment, Boo License with a certified-boo stamp.
- Partner, best friend, family or kids; for family and kids the No button never appears. Cute-spooky, never scary.

Both (own FAQ): optional password at publish; edit and republish any time, same link shows the latest version. Premium: if a YouTube song cannot play on other sites, a built-in melody plays; every swipe/drag/hold also has a button.

Honest limits to state where relevant: UI is English; these are one-person greeting pages, not party invitations for a crowd, costume shops, or a way to find a date.
