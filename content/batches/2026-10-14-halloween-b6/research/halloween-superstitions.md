## Corrections to framing
- "b5 found shelters don't ban black cats" is only partly right. Carini 2020 says HSUS acknowledges that shelters once pulled black cats at Halloween, and it cites a 2016 Smithsonian report implying some still did. The paper is not a policy survey.
- Kelley's 1919 "sacred to Freya" origin for cats, owls and bats is unsourced.
- In Macbeth the witches' cats are grey (Graymalkin) and brinded, not black.
