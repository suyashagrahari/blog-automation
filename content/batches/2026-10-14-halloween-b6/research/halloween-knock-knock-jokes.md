## Framing claims checked (BRIEF §0)
- "The 1936 craze is often cited" — TRUE as a craze (NPR quotes June–Sept 1936 papers), FALSE as an origin: the dialogue is in print by c. 1597 (Jonson), 1606 (Macbeth), and as a children's game by 1929 (Bett); "Do you know Arthur?" precursor recalled from c. 1900.
- "Shakespeare invented the knock-knock joke" (common on SERPs and blogs) — FALSE per Winick.
