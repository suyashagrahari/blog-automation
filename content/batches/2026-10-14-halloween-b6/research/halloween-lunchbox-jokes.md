## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: a list of Halloween jokes for kids, printable/cut-out format.
- Gap: no page sets a length per reading level, gives character counts, separates pre-readers (pictures), cites any reading-speed or lunch-length data, or pairs jokes with encouragement wording. Every list recycles the same classics.
- Fan-out: how long should a lunchbox note be; notes for kids who can't read; how many for October; test-day / nervous-day notes; reusable notes; how long is school lunch; sending to a child far away; when is Halloween.
- Cannibalisation: no sibling shares 4+ SERP URLs. b1 halloween-jokes-for-kids (6 lunchbox lines) and b1 halloween-messages-for-kids (lunchbox messages) read; none of their lines or ideas reused. Knock-knock format avoided entirely (owned by halloween-knock-knock-jokes).
- Angle: wins by being the only post with 31 original notes carrying character counts, limits derived from published reading-fluency medians, a sourced lunch-length line and effort-focused nervous-day pairs.
