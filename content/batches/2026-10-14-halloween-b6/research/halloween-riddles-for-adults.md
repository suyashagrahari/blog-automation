## Phase 3: sources (all fetched and read)
1. Devanand et al. 2022, NEJM Evidence, PMC10457124 (NIH author manuscript, full text via the PMC OAI record). The study had 107 participants with MCI, mean age 71.2. ADAS-Cog went from 9.59 to 8.61 for crosswords and from 9.53 to 9.93 for games. The trial had no no-training control.
2. Cutting et al. 2023, Royal Society Open Science, PMC9890114, CC BY, full text read. n=311, aged 18–56. 100% of the easy group won and 94.1% of the hard group lost. There was no significant difference in enjoyment.
3. NASEM 2017 highlights PDF, full text read. The report calls the evidence for cognitive training "encouraging, although inconclusive" and finds no evidence that training prevents MCI or dementia.
4. Poe, "A Few Words on Secret Writing", Graham's Magazine, July 1841 (eapoe.org), full text read. Of about 100 ciphers sent in, Poe solved all but one, and that one was random characters.

Publishers checked: NEJM Group, Royal Society, National Academies and the Poe Society. None of these is Elsevier, SAGE, T&F, Frontiers or Dove. Heliyon (Elsevier) and JAD (SAGE) were rejected. No megajournal is used, and ERIC is not used, so its last slot stays open.

Searched and not used: "word puzzle frequency cognitive function" (Brooker 2019 IJGP, Wiley, not OA), "lateral thinking puzzles question asking" (Nurse Educator 2018, paywalled; nothing OA), ERIC "lateral thinking puzzles" / "situation puzzles" (nothing relevant), COGIT-2 protocol (Int J Clin Trials, venue doubtful).

## Original-content check
All 18 riddles were written for this post. Each was checked against the sibling posts' riddle and joke lists: halloween-riddles, knock-knock, b5 trivia, b3 jokes-for-adults and b4 games-for-adults. None repeats a sibling riddle or format. The sibling's take-away-a-letter riddles, witch/cat counting, leg counting and weekday riddle were avoided.

Template use was also kept apart from the sibling. halloween-riddles used the carved pumpkin word, sealed P.S., unlock time and reply box. This post uses the flashlight letter, potion ingredients as clues, candy lines as hints and the voice note.

The answers were verified by hand. The Caesar, Atbash and QWERTY answers were checked letter by letter. The mirror-clock answer is 12:00 − 2:35 = 9:25. The Hollow Lane count is 10 + 6 − 2×2 = 12. The candles reach the 2:1 ratio at t = 2.4 h, so 8:24 p.m. The sweets bowl works backwards to 14, and the toffee-apple puzzle has a unique solution, C. For New York at 7 p.m. EDT on 31 Oct, Sydney (AEDT) shows 10 a.m. on 1 Nov in both 2026 and 2027, because US DST ends 1 Nov 2026 and 7 Nov 2027.
