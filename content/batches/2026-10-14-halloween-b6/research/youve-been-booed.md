## Origin verification (task prompt claim: "~1990s Midwest, chain-letter style")
- The **1990s Midwest claim is NOT verified.** We searched Europe PMC full text ("been booed": 4 irrelevant hits), Internet Archive full-text advancedsearch (no booing hits), Google Books Ngram ("You've been booed": empty series; "been booed" is sports usage), the Google Books API (over its daily quota, 429) and Wayback CDX on likely domains (dltk-holidays, kaboose, halloween.about.com, deseretnews: no hits or timeouts).
- **Earliest dated record found:** About.com Family Crafts, "You've Been BOOED", captured 21 Sep 2005. It calls the game "sort of like a May Day basket" and gives a window of **2 days**, with 2 copies, 2 treats and 2 signs.
- beenbooed.com, captured 22 Aug 2009, gives "only one day to act". Its FAQ answers "Must I Participate? Nope."
- Finding for the orchestrator: the "24 hours" rule in the task prompt is the later version. The 2005 rule allowed two days.
