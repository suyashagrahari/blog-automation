## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched, full text read)
1. Mercado Limones, "Más allá del disfraz y pedir calaverita", Espacio Diseño 244, UAM, Oct 2016 — calaverita c.1970, pan de muerto 28 Oct–3 Nov, 1980s Halloween "batalla de identidad", Catrina after 1988. (Faculty magazine essay, not peer reviewed.)
2. Lorente Fernández, Estudios de Cultura Náhuatl 60 (UNAM, 2020), peer-reviewed — Texcoco arrival calendar 28 Oct–2 Nov, bread from ~27 Oct, petal path 1 Nov midday, bells 2 Nov midday. CC BY-NC-ND.
3. Barajas-Rodríguez et al., Agro Productividad 13(3) 2020 — Tempoal, Veracruz cempasúchil: 83% would pay MXN 15–20/bunch; flower's only local use is Xantolo.
4. Rühse, InMedia 9.1 (online 15 Jan 2022), peer-reviewed — Spectre (1,600 extras), real parade 2016, ~1,000 performers, 425,000 → >2,000,000 spectators, none in 2020; Oaxaca 1990s Halloween-inspired parade; tourism promotion since 1971; Lomnitz quote. CC BY-NC.
5. Ley Federal del Trabajo, Art. 74 (diputados.gob.mx, last reform DOF 14-05-2026) — rest-day list excludes 31 Oct, 1 Nov, 2 Nov.
