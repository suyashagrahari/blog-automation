## Phase 3: Sources (all fetched and read; Europe PMC links, exempt host)

Full text read. André Walker system created in the 1990s by stylist André Walker, originally to market his products; numbers 1–4 = straight/wavy/curly/coily, A–C = loosest to tightest; criticised for not covering full range, multiple types on one head, density/porosity; mainstream systems developed by haircare companies to promote sales, not independently validated. Four systems: Walker, LOIS, FIA, L'Oréal (8 types).

Satin wig cap or velvet wig band where wigs rub the frontal hairline; avoid cotton and nylon (friction, absorb moisture); natural hair, loose hairstyles and wigs associated with least traction risk; ask stylist to loosen braid if pain or stinging.
