## Corrections for REPORT
- Burg Frankenstein Halloween event is no longer at Darmstadt (moved to Burg Königstein, Taunus, from 2024).
- "State cancelled Fasching 1991" is Tschorn's wording; carnival committees cancelled the parades.
- "Stiller Feiertag dance ban" is not uniform: Bavaria has no outright Allerheiligen dance ban.
