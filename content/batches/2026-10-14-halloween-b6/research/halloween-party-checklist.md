## Phase 2 — gap and angle
- Table stakes: timeline by weeks, invitations, food/drink, decor, set-up.
- Gap: no calendar dates; no evidence on why timelines slip (planning fallacy); no RSVP/no-show data; no budget split; no host liability, insurance or access checks; no "day after".
- Fan-out: when to start planning; when to send invites; RSVP deadline; no-show rate; budget split; what to buy day before; host liability; insurance; wheelchair access; Halloween 2027 Sunday; day after.
- Angle: only checklist that dates every deadline for 2026 and 2027 with an if-then line per step, backed by Koole 2000 and a Cochrane reminder review, using Raksha Bandhan lead-time data to justify slack.
