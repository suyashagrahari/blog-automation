## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched 2026-10-14)
1. eDIL s.v. samain — dil.ie/36129. Read in full: 1 Nov + festival; All Saints in religious contexts; sam-fuin and sam-suan glosses (Tochm. Em.); feis Temrach each Samain; síd open at Samain. No deity sense.
2. CELT T301021 — Meyer 1888 Wooing of Emer: two divisions of the year; "Samuin night" at Ruad's house.
3. CELT T100054 — Keating, History of Ireland (completed about 1634, per CELT preface): Fire of Tlachtga, druids "on the eve of Samhain", three-pence tax. CELT T106500D Tlachtga poem read: no fire, no Samain (named, not linked).
4. Persée — McKay 2016, Études Celtiques 42 (peer-reviewed, open via page view; PDF 403 not worked around): 62 lunar months/5 years, 2 intercalary; SAMONIOS 3 / TRINVX SAMONI; 19/25/30-year debate "raged for the last century".
5. dimitrafimi.org — Fimi, Gramarye 11 (2017), reposted 2019-10-28: Hutton 1996 pp. 362–364; Rhys 1901; Coligny "seems to begin the year in the summer", none conclusive; Wicca took up the four feasts.
6. ONS Census 2021 religion bulletin (29 Nov 2022): Pagan 74,000; Wicca 13,000 (the one context statistic; on-subject).
