## 5. VERDICT — do not write this post

Two independent grounds:

1. **B ≡ E** (3 shared URLs in the top 10, position 1 identical, 6 at a wider window).
   The keyword's dominant intent is a school English exercise. Gate 2 cut on page type.
2. **A∩B = 3** in the content-page window (2 in the raw window — weak, corroborative
   only). Its non-homework half is a generic English quote list that four existing
   SubhSandesh posts already cover.

The slug is free (`filters[slug][$eq]=raksha-bandhan-lines-in-english` → 0 matches) and
is left free deliberately.

**The orchestrator's hypothesis for this keyword was wrong, and it is worth naming
why.** The brief proposed that "`lines in english`'s plausible distinction from
`quotes` is length and function — a 'line' is something you write *in* a card, a
'quote' is something you attribute or repost." That is a reasonable English-usage
intuition and it does not survive an Indian SERP: "lines" in Indian school English
means a counted composition. This is the same error class as the Gujarati substitution
recorded in `BRIEF.md` §"Gujarati stop-check" — reasoning from a plausible model of the
searcher rather than from the searcher's results. Cheap to catch with one control
query; expensive to catch after drafting 1,700 words.
