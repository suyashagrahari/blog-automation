# CROSSLINK-MAP — batch `2026-09-15-virtual-gift-30`

Generated at quiescence 2026-09-16 from the blog files on disk.

## Why this exists

The thirty wave-2 posts were written in parallel, so no agent could link a
sibling that did not exist on disk yet. **42 of 58 posts carry zero blog
cross-links**, and across the four clusters below there is exactly **one**
sibling link in total. Meanwhile the SERP-overlap audit found eleven pairs of
these posts sharing 4+ of 10 organic URLs.

A cluster with an explicit hub-and-spoke link structure reads to a search engine
as a set with one primary. The same pages with no links between them read as
rivals for the same query. That is the entire reason for this pass.

## Rules

- Each spoke links **the hub** plus **one or two** genuine siblings - not all of them.
- The hub links **every** spoke in its cluster.
- Anchor text is descriptive and specific to the target post. Never `click here`,
  never the bare slug.
- Place a link **after** the section that makes the reader want it, never in the
  opening paragraph.
- A link must earn its place. If two posts have nothing to say to each other,
  do not force it and say so.
- Blog cross-links are additional to the 2-4 `TEMPLATE_LINKS` template links and
  are excluded from that count by `verify-batch.mjs`.
