## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes (all five cover):** a one-sentence definition; delivery by email/SMS/link;
the "e-gift card = digital gift card" equivalence; a types list; the speed/convenience
benefits; redemption mechanics.

**The gap — and it is a large one.** Every ranking page, including both India-specific
ones, treats *digital gift* as a synonym for *digital gift card*, and then writes for a
merchant, an HR buyer or a payments funnel. Nobody:

1. Splits the term into its actual families — stored value, access, digital goods,
   personalised pages. Three of the four are simply missing from the SERP.
2. Writes for a person sending something to someone they love at 11pm.
3. Names the failure mode of the dominant family. Unredeemed balances are the industry's
   revenue model and appear on none of the five pages.
4. Applies Indian regulation. The RBI's ₹10,000 Gift PPI cap, the no-reload and no-cash-out
   rules and the one-year minimum validity are absent even from Razorpay's India page.
5. Carries any behavioural evidence about what recipients actually do with a digital gift.

**Stale data:** 99minds leans on an Aug-2024 global market projection to 2030; Razorpay's
India figure is the Mar-2025 databook and is the freshest number on the SERP. Both are
market-size numbers, which is the only kind of data any of the five pages carries.

**Unanswered questions raised and dropped:** "is a digital gift impersonal?" (raised by
three pages as a benefit-framing, resolved by none); "what happens if it is never
redeemed?"; "how is this different from a virtual gift?".

**Fan-out sub-queries → H2s:**

| Sub-query | Becomes |
|---|---|
| what is a digital gift / meaning | H2 "What is a digital gift?" |
| digital gift vs virtual gift vs e-gift card | H2 "Digital gift, virtual gift, e-gift card — are these the same thing?" |
| types of digital gifts | H2 "The four families of digital gift" (4 × H3 → `ItemList`) |
| which digital gift is best / comparison | H2 "Compare the four on delivery, expiry and what can be measured" (table) |
| digital gift website / personalised gift page | H2 "Where personalised pages sit…" |
| are digital gifts impersonal / do they work | H2 "What research says about sending a gift as a link" |
| when not to send one | H2 "When a digital gift is the wrong choice" |

**Angle (recorded as `batchMeta.angle`):** wins by being the only post that treats a
digital gift as a four-family category rather than a synonym for gift cards, and the only
one that can put measured behaviour behind the personalised-page family — 4,357 pages,
40.6% password-protected, a 6.2-hour median build.

**Differentiation from the sibling `what-is-a-virtual-gift`:** that post defines the
virtual gift and how to send one. This post never re-runs that definition; "virtual gift"
appears here only inside the disambiguation H2, one paragraph long, whose job is to hand
the reader off. This post's spine is the four-family taxonomy and the regulatory /
behavioural evidence for each family.

**Head-term note (BRIEF §6):** `digital gift` is a 1–2 word head term and unwinnable for
this domain. It is deliberately demoted to a secondary keyword; the primary is the
long-tail `what is a digital gift`. Recorded here and in the audit rather than pretended
away.
