## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

- **The gap.** Every page treats "gift for boyfriend" as a product category problem.
  None acknowledges that the Valentine's gifting *script* runs the other way — the
  consumer-research literature on this holiday is overwhelmingly about men buying for
  women — which is precisely why the searcher is stuck.
- **Stale data.** Ranking pages cite nothing, or year-stamp 2022/2024 with no data at all.
- **Unanswered questions:** how long does building one of these actually take; will he
  even know I sent it; what stops it being forwarded; is a link cheaper than flowers in India.
- **Fan-out sub-queries → H2s:** what counts as one · why this direction is harder ·
  which ones suit a boyfriend · ask him or surprise him · link versus courier versus
  restaurant · when to start building · when a link is the wrong gift.
- **Angle:** wins by being the only post that treats the girlfriend-to-boyfriend
  direction as the under-scripted one the Valentine's research admits it is, and answers
  it with SubhSandesh's own build-and-send data (40.6% password-protect; 6.2-hour median
  build gap).
