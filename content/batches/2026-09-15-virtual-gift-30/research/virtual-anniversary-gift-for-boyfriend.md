## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes** — a definition of a virtual/digital gift, a list of formats, a
nod to long distance, and some acknowledgement of privacy.

**The gap.** Nothing on this SERP treats the *anniversary* as anything other than
a purchase occasion. None of them:

- names the three dates an anniversary artefact is actually built from;
- does the date arithmetic (a real day count between an anchor date and the year);
- distinguishes a one-off surprise from a repeating ritual, or cites the
  research showing the ritual framing is what carries the benefit;
- publishes a single number about how long building one takes, how often such
  pages actually get finished, or how often they get locked;
- names a case where a link loses to an object.

**Stale data** — not applicable: the incumbents carry no dated data at all,
which is itself the finding.

**Fan-out sub-queries → H2s.** what counts as a virtual anniversary gift · which
date do we count from · what formats exist and which suits us · what does each
one cost me in preparation · does an anniversary gift have to be a surprise ·
what should go in the timeline · when is a link the wrong answer.

**Angle.** Wins by being the only page on this query that builds the gift from a
real date count and treats the anniversary as a couple ritual — Garcia-Rada,
Sezer and Norton's mutual-agreement finding — instead of a shopping occasion,
priced against SubhSandesh's own 4,357 pages, 6.2-hour median build and 40.6%
password rate.

**Differentiation from the sibling `digital-anniversary-gift-for-girlfriend`:**
direction (she builds, he receives) and spine. This post is organised around
date arithmetic, the shared timeline and the year count; the girlfriend-directed
sibling is not. No shared sources.
