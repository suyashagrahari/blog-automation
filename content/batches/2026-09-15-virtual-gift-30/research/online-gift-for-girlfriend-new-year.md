## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes** (present across the ranking set, so the post must cover them):
what a digital/online gift is, a list of concrete ideas, something about
personalisation, something about it being instant/free to send, a nudge about
long distance.

**The gap.** Every ranking page treats New Year as a *label* pasted onto the same
evergreen romantic-gift list. None of them treats **1 January as a temporal
landmark** — a date whose only real property is that it separates a year you had
from a year you have not had yet. Nobody writes about the year boundary as the
thing the gift is *about*.

**Stale / absent data.** No page in the set cites a dated source of any kind.
There is no measured statement anywhere in the set about how long people take to
build one of these pages, or how late they leave it.

**Unanswered questions** the ranking pages raise and drop: which midnight to use
across time zones; whether to send on 31 December or 1 January; what actually
goes *inside* a retrospective; whether a prediction game is a gift or a gimmick.

**Fan-out sub-queries** → became the H2s:
1. What counts as an online New Year gift for a girlfriend?
2. Why does 1 January change how the same gift is received?
3. Should it look back over the year or forward into the next one?
4. What do you actually put in a year-in-review page?
5. What do you write in a capsule you both open in a year?
6. When should I build and send it?
7. When is a link the wrong choice?

**Angle (`batchMeta.angle`):** wins by being the only post that treats 1 January
as a temporal landmark rather than a label — pairing the New Year landmark and
resolution research with SubhSandesh's own 3.7% occasion-dated share and 6.2-hour
median build gap, so the reader gets a dated deadline instead of an idea list.

## Phase 4–5 — Draft decisions

- First-party pair in the opening: **3.7% occasion-dated / 96.3% romantic-and-
  everyday**, and the **6.2-hour median first-save-to-last-edit gap**. The
  batch-worn pair (6.2 h + 40.6% password) is deliberately split up; 40.6% is
  used later, in the wrong-choice section.
- The comparison table is the five measured page types with their real creation
  counts, because that is the only first-party column that has an honest value
  per row — and the point it makes is the post's point: not one of the five most
  built page types is organised around a year.
- Raksha Bandhan lead-time lines are used for the deadline section with **both**
  mandatory disclosures in the body prose: the measurement is Raksha Bandhan and
  not New Year, and n = 89 is too small to derive any per-template rate from.
- Internal links (4, all from `TEMPLATE_LINKS`): `/rewind`, `/capsule`,
  `/love-gf`, `/templates`. None in the opening paragraph.
- No price is claimed anywhere: the `facts.md` Pricing block is empty.
- `structuredData`: an `@id`-matched enrichment block on
  `https://subhsandesh.in/blog/online-gift-for-girlfriend-new-year#post` carrying
  `citation` one-to-one with `batchMeta.sources`, plus `about`/`mentions` with
  Wikipedia + Wikidata QIDs verified through the Wikipedia API
  (New Year's Day Q196627, New Year's resolution Q2155091, Gift Q184303,
  Long-distance relationship Q1406917); plus an `ItemList` mirroring the six H3s
  in order.
