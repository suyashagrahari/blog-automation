## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes** (covered by all five, so the post must cover them):
what a gift link is; that it holds photos, music, video and a countdown; that it is
sent over WhatsApp; that there is a private/password option; that no app install is
needed on the recipient's side.

**The gap.** Every page describes *what you can make*. Not one describes *what the
link is and how it behaves*:

- No page explains that an unlisted gift URL is a **capability URL** — the address
  is the permission — or what follows from that for forwarding and screenshots.
- No page explains the **preview card**: that WhatsApp and Instagram build it from
  Open Graph tags on the page's markup, so a password does **not** hide the title
  and image from anyone who sees the chat. This is the single most useful and least
  known mechanic in the category.
- No page discusses **what happens to the link over time** — decay, who owns it,
  what a break-up does to a shared page.
- No page distinguishes **Instagram story link stickers** (name only, no image)
  from **DMs** (full card), although the two leak very differently.

**Stale / absent data.** There is no data at all on these pages, stale or fresh —
which means any dated, sourced number is a differentiator rather than a supersession.

**Unanswered questions raised and dropped by the competitive set:** can I edit after
sending? does the music autoplay? can I tell if they opened it? what if I lose the
link? can I shorten it? These became FAQs rather than H2s, per BRIEF §8.
