## 2. Gap analysis

**Table stakes (inferred):** what a digital/online gift is; a list of ideas; how
to send it; some comparison with a physical gift; an FAQ block.

**The gap this post takes:**

1. **A wedding anniversary is not a surprise.** Both people hold the date. Every
   sibling post and, per BRIEF §1, the whole competitive set is built on surprise
   mechanics — hide it, spring it, watch the face. That premise is simply wrong
   for a married couple on a shared milestone, and no page in the cluster says so.
2. **"Free" is in the keyword and cannot be answered.** `content/facts.md` ships
   an **empty Pricing block**. The honest move is to say what the dataset covers
   (pages, views, devices, editing behaviour) and state plainly that it contains
   no cost figure for SubhSandesh or anything else — then answer the *other* thing
   "free" often means, which is a gift with no visible price tag on it.
3. **The reciprocation angle.** The GRAS paper (below) gives the "free" framing a
   research spine nobody in this category uses: an expensive-looking gift can hand
   the recipient a bill.

**Swap test.** Every source is specifically about marriage or long-term
partnership except GRAS, which covers both romantic and marital relationships —
and GRAS is used here only for the price-tag/reciprocation argument, which is
unique to the "free" keyword. None of the five could sit unchanged in a
girlfriend or boyfriend post in this batch.

**Fetch reality, disclosed in the body as well as here.** Only source 1 returned
HTTP 200 to a direct fetch. Sources 2–5 were verified through the **Europe PMC
REST API** record — title, journal, publication date, licence and full abstract —
because `europepmc.org` article pages return 403 to automated requests from this
sandbox, as do SAGE, MDPI, Elsevier/Cell and Wolters Kluwer. **Only the abstracts
were read for those four.** Nothing in the post is inferred past an abstract.
Audit item "3–6 outbound links, all fetched and verified to contain the cited
fact" is recorded as a **FAILURE** for this reason.
