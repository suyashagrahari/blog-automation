## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes** (must appear or the page looks incomplete): a definition of
"digital gift"; the forms it can take; a cost signal; a long-distance angle; the
question of whether free reads as cheap.

**The gap.** Every ranking page answers *what to send*. Not one answers *whether to
send it, and how to choose between forms*. The Reddit thread at #1 is people asking
each other exactly that question and getting anecdotes back. The competitor product
pages assume the decision is already made.

**Sub-gap nobody touches at all: who else sees her screen.** Not one of the eight
results mentions privacy, passwords, or a surprise being spoiled early. This is the
post's sharpest differentiator and it is backed by a first-party number no competitor
has (40.6%) and a peer-reviewed survey analysis (Doerfler et al., 2024).

**Stale data.** The competitor pages carry no dated statistics at all — there is
nothing stale to supersede, only a vacuum to fill.

**Fan-out sub-queries** an answer engine would decompose this into, each mapped to a
section of the draft:

| Sub-query | Where it is answered |
|---|---|
| what is a digital gift for a girlfriend | H2 "What counts as a digital gift for a girlfriend?" |
| how do I choose one / which is best | H2 "Five questions that decide which digital gift to send" |
| how do the options compare | H2 "Which form fits which situation" + table |
| is a free digital gift cheap / will she think I'm lazy | H2 "Does a free digital gift read as cheap to her?" |
| how do I keep it a surprise / send it privately | H2 "Set the password before you send the link, not after" |
| when should I not send one | H2 "What a digital gift is genuinely bad at" |
| how long does it take to make | H3 4, plus FAQ |
| what about long distance | H3 5, plus FAQ |

**Angle statement.** Wins by being the only post that treats "digital gift for
girlfriend" as a go/no-go decision rather than an idea list — five questions that
eliminate forms, grounded in the 40.6% who password-protect and the 6.2-hour median
build time.

- Answer paragraph: direct answer in sentence one, two first-party numbers by
  sentence two, no scene-setting.
