## Phase 2: gap analysis

- **Table stakes:** group themes, office-appropriate, cheap/DIY, inclusion line.
- **Gap:** none sorts ideas by team type (IT, sales, finance, healthcare, school, retail, Zoom); none says how many people an idea needs, whether it needs everyone, what the boss wears, how to vote, how to opt out without being singled out, who pays, how to judge, or the 2026 and 2027 weekend dates (Halloween falls on a Saturday, then a Sunday, so costume day moves to Friday 30 October 2026 or 29 October 2027).
- **Stale data:** none cited by the competitors.
- **Fan-out sub-queries:** costumes for a team of 4; how to vote; what if someone won't dress up; who pays; contest judging criteria; hospital and school rules; Zoom group costume; date of costume day; should the boss dress up; religious inclusion.
- **Angle:** wins by being the only post that sorts team costumes by 11 team types and tags each idea with opt-in level and the boss's part, plus a private opt-out, backed by small-sample studies on names and faces, office-party ritual and distributed-team cohesion.
