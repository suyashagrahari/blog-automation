## Phase 3: sources (all fetched and read)

1. **Acerbi A, Ghirlanda S, Enquist M. The logic of fashion cycles.** *PLOS ONE*, 7 Mar 2012, CC BY. https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC3296716. I read the abstract and the full-text XML. The paper finds that fashion dynamics emerge when people copy preferences as well as traits; that fast-rising traits are abandoned fast; and that status-signalling and random-copying models fail. The Fig. 1C caption says trait revival is possible. This is the required OA paper on fashion cycles. It is a PLOS ONE megajournal paper, which is not banned, and it is new to b4.
2. **Fajardo R. Settling secondhand sales.** *British Journal of Sociology* (Wiley, CC BY), first published 17 Nov 2024. https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC11890435. I read the full-text XML. It covers 31 US resellers, 26 of whom sourced at thrift stores, with Goodwill pricing jeans at $6 and shirts at $4. Reseller "Bradley, 18, Florida" used the tag string "vintage, cyber, y2k, … mall goth, emo, punk rock, t-shirt". A 2000s shirt was listed at $25 against eBay copies at $45 and $60. The publisher is not a banned one.
3. **V&A T.220:1, 2-2004.** Juicy Couture tracksuit, "pink tracksuit of cotton towelling with green lettering on jacket", United States, 2004, given by Juicy Couture. https://collections.vam.ac.uk/item/O110643/ (page HTTP 200, plus V&A API). The page gives no publication date, so none is recorded.
4. **V&A T.137:1 to 3-2009.** Walé Adeyemi ensemble, Britain/Germany, 2004. The summary describes it as "Adeyemi's take on the mass-market tracksuit". https://collections.vam.ac.uk/item/O1135872/. The V&A domain is new to b4 (b2's 80s post used it in another batch).
5. **Google Books Ngram, "skinny jeans"** (English, case-insensitive, smoothing 0, values for all case forms from the json endpoint): 4.58 (2004), 37.13 (2008), 248.81 (2016). books.google.com is cap-exempt.
6. **Wiseman E., The Guardian, 13 Nov 2022, "Skinniness is back in fashion, but did it ever really go away?"** I read the full text. It links belly-baring Halloween costumes to "the return of Y2K fashion", with baby tees and low-rise jeans. One topical newspaper source.
