## Phase 2 — Gap analysis
- **Table stakes:** a numbered list of treats; easy/no-bake options; some kid appeal; healthier swaps (Clemson).
- **Gap:** no page sorts treats by *destination* (door vs class vs allergy vs adults); none gives allergens, shelf life and cost per item; none says which treats are accepted at the door, with the official FDA commercially-wrapped rule; none covers UK-14 vs US-9 allergens; none mentions THC copycat candy or dogs.
- **Stale data:** Food Network 2022/2024, babygizmo 2022; no statistics anywhere.
- **Fan-out:** what counts as a treat / is home-made OK; easy treats; class-party nut-free; allergy-friendly; low-sugar; no-bake same-day; adult treat bags; safety (tampering, edibles, dogs); how long treats keep.
- **Angle:** wins by being the only post that sorts Halloween treats by where they go, with make time, US-9/UK-14 allergens, shelf life and cost per 20 for every item, plus the FDA's commercially-wrapped rule and THC copycat warning.
