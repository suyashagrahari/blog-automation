## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: handprint/footprint ghosts, paper-plate and cotton-ball ghosts, toilet-roll bats, dot stickers, "supervise", "non-toxic washable paint".
- Gap: nobody bands by age in months, names the motor skill, gives adult prep vs toddler minutes, or scores mess. Nobody cites the actual under-3 small-parts rule; two rankers actively suggest choking-size materials to toddlers (dried beans, pumpkin seeds, googly eyes, pom-poms). Nobody explains that "Conforms to ASTM D-4236" is a labelling statement, not a non-toxic badge. Nobody gives carers research-backed permission for imperfect results.
- Stale data: none cited by anyone.
- Fan-out: what can a 1-year-old make; are googly eyes safe; is washable paint safe if eaten; how long will a 2-year-old craft; AP seal meaning; scissors at 3; preschool crafts; less mess; should I fix their craft; what to do with it.
- Angle: wins by being the only post that sorts toddler Halloween crafts by age in months with motor skill, prep minutes and mess score, and checks every one against the CPSC under-3 small-parts rule.
