## Brief claims checked

- Prompt asked for India–US offsets "verified against clock changes": confirmed; the US stays on daylight time through 31 Oct in both years, so India–New York is 9 h 30 (not 10 h 30).
- 31 Oct 2027 is itself the UK clock-change day (GOV.UK) — a trap for anything scheduled in London that morning.
- The template spec states the premium unlock time exists but not which time zone it uses; the post tells readers to check the countdown rather than claiming "opens at midnight their time" works automatically.
