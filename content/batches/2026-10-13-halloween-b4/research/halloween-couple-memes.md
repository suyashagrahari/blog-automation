## Phase 2: gap analysis

- **Table stakes:** the "couples costume idea" parody format; funny captions about couples.
- **Gap:** relationship situations (scary vs cosy, costume negotiation, candy theft, horror night, long distance, parents, singles, costume-or-breakup) as reusable formats; why and when to send memes to a partner; text-first templates that avoid copyright problems.
- **Stale data:** every roundup is from 2017 and depends on embedded tweets.
- **Fan-out sub-queries:** what is a couple meme; funny Halloween memes for my partner; long-distance Halloween memes; memes for married couples; single on Halloween memes; what is pebbling; is meme sharing good for relationships; how to make a meme without copyright problems.
- **Angle:** wins by being the only post that writes Halloween couple memes out as reusable formats sorted by relationship situation, with the sending habit grounded in meme-sharing and bids-for-connection research and SubhSandesh's 48.3% phone-open rate and 1,434 recipient hug taps.
