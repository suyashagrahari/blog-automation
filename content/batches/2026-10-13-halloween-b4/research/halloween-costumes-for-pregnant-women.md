## Phase 3: sources (all fetched)
1. Shamsaei et al. 2022, "Essential Criteria for Designing Healthy Maternity Wear: A Narrative Review", Iran J Nurs Midwifery Res 27(6):492–495, Medknow, CC BY-NC-SA. Europe PMC PMC9881558, full text read. The review says maternity clothing should be adaptable, comfortable and stylish, with thermal and tactile comfort and easy dressing. It also lists minimising "the protrusion of enlarged areas of the body", which the post quotes and gently disagrees with for Halloween. Peer-reviewed, open access, not a megajournal.
2. Griffin 2026, "A qualitative exploration of positive body image during pregnancy", New Vistas 12(1), UWL doctoral conference, CC BY 4.0. Only the abstract page was read: n=7, five themes including "Celebrating the pregnant body".
3. ACOG FAQ119, "Exercise During Pregnancy" (updated Sep 2024, reviewed Nov 2025): overheating, especially in the first trimester; loose clothing; water; centre of gravity and fall risk; standing motionless pools blood; dizziness is a warning sign. The page is about exercise, and the post says so.
4. NHS, "Back pain in pregnancy" (reviewed 10 Jan 2024): wear flat shoes to distribute weight evenly. This is nhs.uk's last b4 slot.
5. Health Canada, "Use cosmetics safely" (page dated 2019-02-28): make-up can cause allergic reactions; patch test on the forearm and wash after 24 hours; discard a product that changes in smell, colour or feel. This replaced the FDA novelty-makeup page after the orchestrator flagged fda.gov FULL in b4. It is not pregnancy-specific.
