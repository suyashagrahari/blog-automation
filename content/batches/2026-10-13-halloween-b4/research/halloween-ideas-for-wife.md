## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched via Europe PMC REST / PMC; full text read)

2. Aydin & Buyukcan-Tetik 2021, PLOS ONE — PMC8092658. Three studies, N = 1,102; social function of relationship memories positively associated with intimacy and satisfaction; exploratory: sharing-social link with intimacy significant only at longer relationship duration. (Megajournal — noted; chosen for the long-married stage.)
