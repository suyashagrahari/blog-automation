## Phase 2 — gap analysis

- **Table stakes:** decor, food, games, a movie, ghost stories, age-appropriate scare level.
- **Gap (none of the five):** a timed arrival-to-pickup run sheet; ideas split by age band (8–12 / 13–17 / adults) with time and materials; named, verifiable public-domain stories with word counts and read-aloud times; a parent group-chat checklist (allergies, emergency numbers, pickup, phones, film ratings, private matters); a plan for the child who wants to go home at 11 pm; sleep-duration maths; an adult "grown-up sleepover" version.
- **Stale data:** none of them cite any data; ehow last updated 2022.
- **Fan-out sub-queries → H2/H3/FAQ:** what makes it different from a party; ideas by slot; timeline at a glance; what to tell other parents; homesick child; how much sleep; adult version; which story to read; how scary the film should be; under-8s.
- **Angle:** wins by being the only post that lays the night out as a timed run sheet across three age bands, with stories counted to the word, a parent checklist, AASM sleep maths and a kind go-home plan, using the 48.3% phone-open figure to justify a phone basket over a ban.
