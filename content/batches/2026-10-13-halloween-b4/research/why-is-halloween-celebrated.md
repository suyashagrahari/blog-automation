## Phase 2 — gap analysis
- **Table stakes:** All Saints' eve, Samhain, trick-or-treating, costumes.
- **Gap:** every ranking page answers "why" with an origin story. None explains what the holiday *does* now (psychological, social, commercial, identity functions), none grades claims by evidence, and none addresses who does not celebrate and why.
- **Stale data:** no page carries a number; NRF 2026 (published 22 Sept 2026) is fresh.
- **Unanswered:** why people enjoy fear; why neighbours open doors; why it is spreading; whether Samhain is real.
- **Fan-out:** why on 31 October; is it religious; why scared; why costumes; why trick-or-treat; how much spent; why some Christians object; Samhain link; vs Day of the Dead; fine not to celebrate.
- **Angle:** wins by being the only post that answers the "why" as functions, graded by evidence type, with the opt-out case included, using SubhSandesh's Raksha Bandhan lead-time data (49.4% built on the day) as evidence that fixed dates prompt people to reach out.
