## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes** (must appear or the page looks incomplete): flowers/marigold, a fabric or paper
backdrop, lighting, a chowki/mandap frame, rangoli, diyas, an eco-friendly section, a mention of
thermocol alternatives, and a nod to what fits a small flat.

**The gap — four things none of the five do:**

1. **Durability against the actual span.** Every page assumes a single photograph. Nobody rates an
   element against 14→25 September. Marigold, banana stem and mango-leaf toran behave completely
   differently across twelve days, and that is the reader's real problem on day four.
2. **Cost and build time.** Zero rupee figures except one shop's own SKUs. Zero minutes-to-build.
3. **The thermocol ban as law, not preference.** Livspace has a "without thermocol" heading and
   frames it as an aesthetic choice. Polystyrene (thermocol) *for decoration* is on India's banned
   single-use plastic list — notified 12 August 2021, in force from 1 July 2022.
4. **Rangoli treated as a structured folk art rather than a stencil.** All five show rangoli photos.
   None explains the pulli/dot-grid that makes a design reproducible, the regional naming, or what
   the powder in the packet actually is.

**Stale/absent data:** there is no data at all to supersede — which is itself the opening.

**Fan-out sub-queries → H2/H3/FAQ map:** how long do marigold garlands last · what to use instead
of thermocol · how much does home ganpati decoration cost · simple ganpati background decoration ·
ganesh chaturthi rangoli designs · is rangoli powder safe · when should I start decorating · what to
do with the decoration after visarjan · how to show the mandap to family who can't come.

**Angle (`batchMeta.angle`):**
Wins by being the only Ganesh decoration page that rates each element against the real 14–25
September 2026 span with a cost, a build time and a survival count — and the only one that says
thermocol for decoration is banned by law — closing on SubhSandesh's platform-wide 49.6% mobile-open
rate to tell the reader how to shoot and share the mandap.
