## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes (all five):** wishes segmented by recipient; a boss section; a colleague section; a line about keeping it professional but warm; a note that personalisation helps.

**The gap — what none of the five covers:**

1. **Direction.** Every page is written for the sender with power (a company, a manager, a vendor). Nobody writes for the individual employee sending sideways and upward.
2. **The risk in the upward message.** No page acknowledges that a warm message to a boss can read as currying favour, and none mentions the ingratiation / impression-management literature that studies exactly this.
3. **The group-message problem.** No page mentions that twenty people will post the same forwarded image in the team group, and none answers whether to reply to twenty colleagues individually.
4. **Calendar reality.** No page notes that Diwali 2026 is a **Sunday** (8 November), which removes the entire "send it on the day" advice that bigsmall.in gives.
5. **Tenure.** New joiner versus five-year veteran is never distinguished.
6. **Remote colleagues.** advantageclub.ai mentions hybrid teams only to pitch its automation product; nobody treats the greeting as the one non-work ping of the year.
7. **A "leave out" field.** Every page tells you what to say. None tells you what not to say — and "your guidance lights our path", the single most common line on these pages, is precisely the line to cut.

**Stale data:** not applicable in the usual sense — none of the five cites any data at all. That is itself the opening: a page with named research and dated figures is differentiated on a query where the incumbent set has zero citations.

**Fan-out sub-queries → H2s / FAQs:** what to write to a boss; is it inappropriate to wish a boss; individual vs group message; when to send in 2026; can I send on Diwali day; what not to say to a manager; what does a new joiner send; how to greet remote colleagues; what if a colleague does not celebrate.

**Angle (recorded as `batchMeta.angle`):** wins by being the only post written from the employee's side of the hierarchy — an upward Diwali wish handled as an impression-management risk with named research, and the Sunday-8-November-2026 timing problem solved — priced against SubhSandesh's own 51.8% phone-open rate and 12.1 views per created page.
