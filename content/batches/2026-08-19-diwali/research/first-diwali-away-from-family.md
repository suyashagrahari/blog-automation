## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes (all five cover):** homesickness is normal; the social-media feed makes it worse; decorate the room you have; cook or order Indian food; video-call the family; find or join a local Indian community; the festival is about light and homecoming.

**The gap — none of the five does any of this:**

1. **States the year's dates.** Not one page gives a date. Diwali 2026 is **Sunday 8 November**, five days **6–10 November**. The Sunday is load-bearing: it makes a Friday-night-to-Monday-morning domestic trip cost no leave, and it means family abroad are not squeezing the puja around a workday.
2. **Separates the domestic move from the diaspora case.** All five collapse "away" into one audience. A hostel room in Pune is a travel decision; a flat in Toronto is a substitution decision.
3. **Solves the call, rather than saying "video call your family".** Nobody addresses the actual failure: calling mid-puja gets you nine seconds with eleven people. The two-window plan (short video window *inside* the puja + a real call the next morning) and the IST→GMT/ET/AEDT offsets appear on none of them.
4. **Asking for a job, not a slot.** Ritual participation as a role rather than as spectatorship is absent everywhere.
5. **Cites anything.** No page cites research that homesickness measurably eases, which is the one reassurance a first-timer wants and the one that should not be asserted from sentiment.
6. **Send-home timing.** No cut-off dates, no "money instead of parcels across a border".
7. **Names a downside.** No page says when a digital greeting is the wrong choice.

**Stale data:** nothing to supersede — there is no data on any of the five pages.

**Fan-out sub-queries → H2/FAQ mapping:** when is Diwali 2026 · is it normal to feel this bad · does it get easier · when should I call · how do I handle time zones · what is the minimum I can do alone · should I host something · what do I send home and by when · is a link better than a parcel · should I travel home.

**Angle:** wins by being the only post that handles the domestic move and the diaspora case as separate problems — a two-window call plan against real IST offsets for the 6–10 November 2026 puja days, priced against SubhSandesh's own 51.8% phone-open rate and 6.9-hour median edit gap — while stating plainly that no Diwali template exists.
