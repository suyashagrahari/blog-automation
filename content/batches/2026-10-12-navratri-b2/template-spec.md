Describe ONLY these features. Anything else is invented.

- Create: add their name and yours, **nine short lines (one for each night of Navratri)**, a note, **up to six photos**, and the night's details — date, time, venue, dress code. Or describe the two of you in a few lines and let the AI fill it in. Save to preview, Publish, send the private link on WhatsApp or Instagram. Opens in any phone or laptop browser, **no app and no account** for the recipient.
- Recipient's experience: clash a pair of dandiya sticks to open an embroidered envelope → light **nine diyas round a garbo** (each shows one of your lines) → a short **dhol-beat dandiya game** → pick the **colour they will twirl in** and see yours beside it → your photos dance round the garba circle → your note → the invitation ticket → "Will you be my Dandiya partner?" → a downloadable **Dandiya Pass**.
- The No button: drops its sticks and twirls away a few times with a new excuse each time; **after five tries it turns into a second Yes**. Sender gets an **email the first time they say yes**, with how many times No twirled away.
- Ticket: shows date, time, venue, dress code; **Add to calendar** for Google Calendar and Apple/Outlook. Empty date → "Any night you pick".
- Who: **your love, your crush, or your best friend as a garba buddy** — the question, pass and reply change ("Will you be my garba buddy, all nine nights?"). Every line editable.
- Music: paste a **YouTube link** (optional start time, audio only) or pick one of **eight garba/dandiya favourites such as Chogada, Nagada Sang Dhol or Shubhaarambh**; also a built-in dhol-and-melody tune. Music starts only after the first tap.
- Dandiya Pass: **1080×1920 PNG (Instagram Story size)** with your photo, both names, date, venue, the colour they picked and a "Partner Confirmed" stamp; one-tap WhatsApp reply.
- Optional **password** at publish; edit and republish any time, same link shows the latest version.

Honest limits to state where relevant: the page UI is English; it is a one-to-one invitation (not a group/society/office invite or a ticketing tool); it does not sell garba passes or find you a partner.
