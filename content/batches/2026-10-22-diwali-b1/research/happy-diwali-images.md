## Phase 2 — gap analysis
- Table stakes: images to send; Diwali greeting text; WhatsApp sharing.
- Gap: none of the 10 says whether a forwarded image is legal to use, how to read a licence, or why WhatsApp degrades it; none offers make-your-own ideas.
- Stale: year-stamped galleries (2023, 2025).
- Fan-out sub-queries → H2s/FAQs: is it legal to forward Google images; what CC BY-NC/ND mean; how to credit; why WhatsApp blurs; HD on Status; image size for Status/Story; photos of other people; Diwali 2027 date.
