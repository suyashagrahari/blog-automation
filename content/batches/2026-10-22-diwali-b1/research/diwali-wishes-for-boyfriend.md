None cites research on whether men care about compliments. None has a table, and none gives Diwali 2027 send days.

Cited:
1. Eckstein et al. 2023, Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience (OUP), PMC9976881, full text. N = 84 including 43 couples. Messages were built from a list of 23 trait and relationship areas. "We found no sex differences in any comparison." Partner compliments drew more insula and amygdala activity than self-compliments. The ventral striatum was active when choosing a message. Only happy couples were included (Mannheim, Germany).
2. Chopik et al. 2025, Innovation in Aging 9 Suppl 2 (OUP), PMC12762657. This is a conference abstract only. 954 couples, together 27.39 years on average. Matching on love languages explained virtually nothing beyond personality and attachment.
3. Drik Panchang 2027 puja calendar, used as a date reference only: Lakshmi Puja is Fri 29 Oct 2027 and Tamil Deepavali is Thu 28 Oct.
