- **Table stakes:** themes, games/activities (antakshari, tambola, musical chairs, photo booth), menu, decor, invites.
- **Gap:** none of the top 10 gives 2027 dates or says which night to host; none writes out game rules with players/time/kit; none gives food quantities; none has a timed run-sheet. Paperless Post describes taash "often involving real money" and teen patti as a betting game without any legal or household caveat.
- **Stale data:** no page cites any data at all.
- **Fan-out sub-queries → H2/FAQ:** which night in 2027; themes and names; games for mixed ages; why cards on Diwali / is it legal to play for money; food for N guests; invite timing; party without crackers; including someone who can't come.
- **Angle:** wins by being the only page in the India top 10 that dates the 2027 party nights against the Drik Panchang festival week, writes out original rules for six games, frames the card tradition with a sourced reason for no-money play, and sizes food for 20, framed by the 48.3% phone-view share and Raksha Bandhan 2026 lead-time analogue.
