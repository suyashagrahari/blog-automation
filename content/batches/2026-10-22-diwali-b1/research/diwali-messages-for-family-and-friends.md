## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched)

2. Saramäki et al. 2014, PNAS — europepmc.org/article/MED/24395777 — abstract only (full-text API returned 500): old contacts replaced or get fewer calls as new ones join.
3. Roberts & Dunbar 2015, Human Nature (CC BY) — europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC4626528 — full text: n = 25 over 18 months; inner-layer kin 102/145 (70.3%) retained vs friends 69/142 (48.6%); outer-layer move cuts closeness for friends not kin; affinal kin follow genetic-kin rules (citing Burton-Chellew & Dunbar 2011).
4. Dunbar 2016, Royal Society Open Science — europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC4736918 — full text: layers ~5/15/50/150; friendships decay without contact; occasional face-to-face needed.
