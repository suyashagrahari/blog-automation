## Phase 2 — Gap

- Table stakes: dhan + teras etymology; Dhanvantari from the churning; Hima/Yama legend; Yama lamp; Lakshmi/Kubera.
- Gap: none of the five names a primary text for any strand; all present the Hima story as fact; none separates *dhana* (wealth) from Dhanvantari (*dhanvan*); none mentions the Ayurveda Day change; ET gets the paksha wrong.
- Fan-out: what does Dhanteras mean; is it named after Dhanvantari; what is Yama Deepam / how to do it; where does the Hima story come from; who is Dhanvantari; Ayurveda Day on Dhanteras?; regional name (Gujarat amanta month).
- Angle: wins by being the only post that traces each reason to a text it opened, separates dhana from dhanvan, and says the Hima story is untraced.
