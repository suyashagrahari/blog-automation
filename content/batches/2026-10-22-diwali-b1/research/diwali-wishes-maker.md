## Phase 2 — gap
- **Table stakes:** personalise with name/photo/message; share on WhatsApp/Instagram or print; how-to steps.
- **Gap:** every top page is ONE maker selling its own templates; none compares maker TYPES, none says what happens after sending (forward labels, editability, reopening), none says when its own type is the wrong choice; none describes an interactive greeting.
- **Stale data:** egreetings portal's 2016 contest content.
- **Fan-out sub-queries:** what is a wishes maker; which type for whom; app vs no app; for company; video vs image; can I edit after sending; can AI write it; song/photos; motion sensitivity.
- **Angle:** This post wins by being the only one in the top 10 that compares maker types by what the recipient receives and what happens after sending, using SubhSandesh's own 11.1 views per page and 48.3% phone-open share plus WhatsApp's forwarding rules.
