## Phase 3: sources (all fetched and verified 2026-10-22)

1. **Underhill, *The Hindu Religious Year* (1921), p. 63**, archive.org `cu31924079584821`, public domain. Read in the djvu text:
   - On Yama or Bhaubija dvitiya, every man and boy dines at his sister's house; "failing a sister a cousin is considered an equivalent".
   - "In exchange for the feast provided, he is expected to make a present of clothing, coin or jewellery, according to his means."
   - Type: primary text for the ritual. archive.org is cap-exempt.
2. **Viry & Herz (2025-08-18), *British Journal of Sociology*, PMC12668247**, CC BY. Full text read:
   - Sample: 549 adults, 3,345 ties; siblings are 15% of the people named.
   - Sibling ties are more likely than child ties to involve reciprocal material support.
   - Only material reciprocity declines with residential distance.
   - Emotional reciprocity is more likely in spatially dispersed families.
   - Type: peer-reviewed, open access.
3. **Artamonova et al. (2026-06-20), *J Gerontol B*, PMC13439929**, CC BY. Abstract read in full:
   - Geographic proximity strengthened sibling support exchanges.
   - "A 10 km threshold seems adequate for approximating help and care exchanges between siblings."
   - Type: peer-reviewed. Also cited by `bhai-dooj-2027`, which makes this the 2nd and last allowed post.
4. **RBI Liberalised Remittance Scheme FAQ** (updated 2023-04-06):
   - A rupee gift to an NRI/PIO close relative by electronic transfer is credited to the NRO account.
   - It falls within the USD 2,50,000 per financial-year LRS limit.
   - Cap: rbi.org.in is now in 3 posts, which is at the cap.
5. **BIS hallmarking overview** (undated): "two precious metals namely gold and silver have been brought under the purview of Hallmarking". bis.gov.in is a separate host from services.bis.gov.in.
