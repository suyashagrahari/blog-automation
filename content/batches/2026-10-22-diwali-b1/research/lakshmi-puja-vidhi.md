## Phase 2 — gap

- **Table stakes:** clean and set chowki, kalash, Ganesha first, the upachara sequence, aarti, diyas through the house, samagri list.
- **Gap:** no ranking page explains *why* the steps are what they are (Purusha Sukta-linked sixteen upacharas; Vishnu Purana I.9 lotus and elephant-bath scene); none gives a short form with honest time estimates; none dates the samagri for 2027; Drik's "16 steps" page lists 34 numbered steps without reconciling the count; regional variation appears only in Hindi Wikipedia, and nowhere as practical guidance (Kali Puja 28 Oct 2027 vs Lakshmi Puja 29 Oct; Chopda Pujan; Marathi broom and dhane-gul; South Indian Naraka Chaturdashi).
- **Stale data:** livehindustan 2025 dates, webdunia 2016; neither is wrong on ritual, but neither is dated for 2027.
- **Fan-out sub-queries → H2/FAQ:** what is the vidhi; where it comes from; steps; samagri and when to buy; regional differences; diya safety; short form / no-space homes; photo vs murti; mantra without Sanskrit; old murti; Bengal on Diwali; Dhanteras buying.
- **Angle:** This post wins by being the only page in the measured top 10 that ties each step of the vidhi to the sixteen upacharas and their Vishnu Purana source, gives a dated samagri timeline, and states regional practice as variations, using SubhSandesh's Raksha Bandhan 2026 lead-time data.
