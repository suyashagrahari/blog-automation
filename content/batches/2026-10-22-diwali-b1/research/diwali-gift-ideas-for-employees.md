## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched 2026-10-22)
1. Income-tax Rules, 2026 PDF (incometaxindia.gov.in), notified 20 Mar 2026, in force 1 Apr 2026. Rule 15 row 4: nil if below ₹15,000 in aggregate during the tax year. Also cross-checked on /w/rule-15-23.
2. ITD perquisites page (1962 Rules): ₹5,000. No publication date (upload date 30/04/2026).
3. CBIC press release, 10 July 2017: employer gifts up to ₹50,000 a year are outside GST.
4. Kube, Maréchal & Puppe, KIT WP 25 (Apr 2011; AER 2012). Full text read. Cash +5%, bottle +24.8%, origami +29.3%, choice +24.5%, and 18 of 22 chose cash.
5. Bradler, Dur, Neckermann & Non, ZEW DP 13-017 (Mar 2013; Mgmt Sci 2016). Full text read. 363 hires; thank-you cards raised performance; non-recipients drove it.
