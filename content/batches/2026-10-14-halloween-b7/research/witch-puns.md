## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched and verified 2026-10-14)
1. Wiktionary "witch" — homophone "which" tagged wine–whine; OE wiċċe/wiċċa. Cap-exempt.
2. Wiktionary "coven" — Latin conventum < convenire; doublet of convent. Cap-exempt.
3. Wiktionary "hex" — first attested about 1830, Pennsylvania Dutch hexe < German Hexe; hexadecimal/hexagon senses separate. Cap-exempt.
4. Rose, Spalek & Abdel Rahman 2015, PLOS ONE, PMC4482729 (Europe PMC, full text read, CC BY): 88 native German speakers; puns, not plain jokes, co-activated alternative homophone meanings (Schloss lock/castle).
5. Prenger et al. 2023, J Neurosci, PMC10711695 (full text read, CC BY): 26 adults; comprehension engages DS + VS, appreciation only VS.
