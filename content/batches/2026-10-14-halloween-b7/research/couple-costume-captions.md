## Phase 2 — gap
- **Table stakes:** a long list of copyable captions and a pun-heavy tone.
- **Gap:** no ranking page sorts captions by what the costume pair is. None gives a caption length or job per type, and none says when to name the pair.
- **Stale data:** none cited anywhere. The Elite Daily 2019 URL is year-stamped.
- **Fan-out:** captions for food pairs, opposites, famous pairs and pun costumes; caption length; whether to name the costume; whether to tag or ask the partner; posting privately; the Halloween date.
- **Angle:** this post wins by being the only one that keys couple costume captions to duo archetype, with a length and caption job per type. It is grounded in Holt (1887) and Lear (1871) pair sources, plus SubhSandesh's phone-view and password rates for the private version.
