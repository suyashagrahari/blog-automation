- Table stakes: a playful Halloween framing, some copyable words, "read the vibe".
- Gap: no page names a specific plan with a time commitment, prints a no option into the ask, matches the plan to what she likes, or warns against public / party asks. None separates spooky from non-spooky invites.
- Stale data: none cited at all; elitedaily dates to 2018.
