## Phase 3 — sources (all on europepmc.org, which is cap-exempt)
1. **Ghadri et al. 2016, *European Heart Journal* (OUP), PMC5841222.** Open access; full text read. Of 485 emotionally triggered takotsubo cases, 20 (4.1%) followed happy events. Table 1 lists a "surprise farewell celebration" and an "unexpected visit from favourite nephew". The happy-trigger patients were 95.0% women, with a mean age of 71.4.
2. **Wiley et al. 2026, *Emotion* (APA), MED 41264500.** Abstract only. Five studies at three haunted attractions found a subjective sense of closeness, but the pre-to-post change in closeness was tiny or null. Post-experience processing may be critical. Also cited in b1 and b6 (caps reset per batch); not used elsewhere in b7.
3. **Faunce, Blumenthal & Waugh 2022, *Psychophysiology* (Wiley), PMC9539862.** CC BY; abstract read. n = 31. Trait anxiety predicted a weaker initial-value dependence in startle habituation. The post's "may not wear off" line is an inference from this, worded with "may".
4. **Rhabneh et al. 2026, *Oxford Medical Case Reports*, PMC13343773.** Open access; full text read. Case report of happy heart syndrome after a daughter's wedding party, with full recovery.
