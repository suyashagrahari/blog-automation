## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: a short list of products, a removal note, "practise first".
- Gap: every ranking page is a list of looks. None teaches the transferable skills (base, shadow, line, blend, set) that every look is built from; none explains why shadow reads as depth or why it can flip under uplighting; none cites anything on sponge hygiene, eye-area colour rules or how dermatologists run a use test.
- Stale: hollywoodmirrors titled "2024"; allthingsbeauty 2022.
- Fan-out: what to buy first; can I use normal makeup; why contour looks muddy; powder over paint; sharing sponges; cracking paint; eye-area labels; removal; how long to practise.
- Angle: wins by being the only beginner post that teaches five transferable skills instead of looks, backed by a shape-from-shading study (lighting flips contour), a used-sponge contamination study and the ESCD use-test method, with SubhSandesh's 48.3% phone-view share as the reason to judge the face in a phone photo.
