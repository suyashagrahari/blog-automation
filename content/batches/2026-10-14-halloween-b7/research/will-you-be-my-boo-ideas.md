## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: a list of cute themed asks; pumpkin/ghost/candy props; signs.
- Gap: every ranking page frames the ask as something the other person "can't say no to"; none gives make times and materials per prop in a consistent format, none builds a no option into the prop, none addresses public vs private asks or what to do after a no.
- Fan-out sub-queries → H2s: what "be my boo" means; ideas with make time; comparison; public vs private; rules; what if they say no; digital option; when not to ask.
- Angle: wins by being the only page giving nine make-time-costed props each with a written no, backed by research that rejected proposals skew public, and SubhSandesh's 38.5% password and 48.3% phone-open rates.
