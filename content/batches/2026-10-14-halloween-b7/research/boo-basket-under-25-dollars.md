## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: a container, candy, small fillers, a budget total.
- Gap: no page splits the budget by line (container vs filler vs candy); every price is one retailer's, 2–6 years old (stale, e.g. a $1 dollar-store base price from 2020); no currency equivalents for UK/CA/AU/IN; no dollar-store vs DIY time trade-off; nothing on whether cheap gifts are received as cheap; nothing on candy inflation.
- Fan-out: how much candy, cheapest container, can I make two, is dollar store tacky, $25 in £/CA$/AU$/INR, how long does it take, homemade snacks.
- Angle: wins by being the only page that costs every line as a dated estimate, splits the budget, converts it into four currencies, and grounds "note over spend" in a 2025 gift trial plus first-party phone-open and publish rates.
