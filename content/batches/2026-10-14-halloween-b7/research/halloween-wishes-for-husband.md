## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched and read)
1. Hahn & Campbell 2016, EJOP 12(3), europepmc PMC4991048 — 116 married couples, ~10 years married; humour styles similar, more so for positive humour. Full text.
2. Kindt, Vansteenkiste, Cano & Goubert 2017, Motivation and Emotion 41(6), author manuscript at biblio.ugent.be — 64 chronic-pain dyads, 14-day diary, mean relationship 21.27 years; perceived gratitude → more autonomous help same and next day. Full text. Sample caveat disclosed.
3. Haase 2023, Annual Review of Developmental Psychology 5, europepmc PMC11210602 — older couples use more "we" words, more affectionate conflict, more good-natured humour over time (secondary citations). Full text.
4. Saroglou, Lacour & Demeure 2010, EJOP 6(3), ejop.psychopen.eu — 98 married + 48 divorced couples (N=292); constructive humour ↔ satisfaction; aggressive humour (esp. men) predicted divorce. Full text.
