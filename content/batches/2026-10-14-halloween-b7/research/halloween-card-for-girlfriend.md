## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes (from intent, since the SERP has no editorial pages): message examples, a DIY option.
- Gap: nobody says how much fits on each card type, nobody cites how long people actually write, nobody covers postcard/pop-up mailing rules for a romantic card.
- Fan-out: what to write; how long; per card type; gift-tag wording; new relationship; girlfriend who dislikes scary things; handwriting/layout; DIY; can a pop-up be posted; digital alternative; Halloween date.
- Angle: wins by being the only page that sizes girlfriend card messages to four card types and their real writing space, using the 88-word median miss-you letter (a small folded card holds ~60% of it) and the 48.3% phone-open rate.
