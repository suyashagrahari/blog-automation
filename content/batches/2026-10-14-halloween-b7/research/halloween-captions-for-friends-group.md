## Phase 3 — sources (all via europepmc.org, cap-exempt; journals each used once in this batch)
1. Dunbar 2022, Phil Trans R Soc B (PMC9489299) — full text read: conversation and laughter groups asymptote ~3.5; mean laughter group 2.7; social groups fragment into subgroups.
2. Dunbar, Frangou, Grainger, Pearce 2021, PLOS ONE (PMC8362988) — full text read: laughter ~4x more likely in groups of four than alone; Exp 2 (n=52, groups of 3–4) laughter raised bonding to group.
3. Manninen et al. 2017, J Neurosci (PMC6596504) — ABSTRACT ONLY: social laughter with close friends triggered opioid release, n=12 males.
4. Dunbar 2025, Ann N Y Acad Sci (PMC11918532) — full text read: layers 5/15/50/150; 5-layer contact weekly, 15-layer monthly.
