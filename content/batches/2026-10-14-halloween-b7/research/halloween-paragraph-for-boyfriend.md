## Phase 3: sources (all via Europe PMC, which is cap-exempt)
1. Floyd & Morman 2020, PsyArXiv PPR330442. Full text read from the OSF docx. Study 1, N = 2,616: men 4.65, women 4.66 (9-point scale, p = .93). Study 2, Census-matched N = 1,121: no gender effect. This is a preprint and is not peer reviewed.
2. Barkley et al. 2026, PLOS Mental Health, PMC12880673. Full text read. 24 interviews in Alexandra, South Africa: masculine norms discourage showing emotion, but participants valued talking to someone trusted.
3. Niemyjska & Curcio 2021, Current Issues in Personality Psychology, PMC10535543. Full text read. 119 Italian adults (59 men): no gender or masculine-norm effect on using a partner's photo during separation. Letters were not tested.
