## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched)
1. USDA ARS Agriculture Handbook 66, "Pumpkin and Winter Squash" (J.K. Brecht), rev. Feb 2016 — 10–13 °C, 50–70% RH, higher RH → decay, 2–3 months max, chilling injury <10 °C, pits at 0–4 °C, infection via wounds. Scholarly handbook; subject test pass.
2. Arvayo-Ortiz et al. 1994, HortTechnology 4(3):253 (peer-reviewed, ASHS) — 'Delica' C. maxima at 20 °C/75% RH: 3.6% weight loss at 4 wk, 10.2% at 12 wk. **Abstract only** (Crossref metadata; publisher page bot-walled).
3. ACMI Classroom Art Materials Safety — AP seal ≤ grade 6; CL seal grade 7+, never to ≤ grade 6.
4. eCFR 16 CFR 1500.14(b)(8) — LHAMA 18 Nov 1988; D-4236 law from 18 Nov 1990; conformance statement wording. (ecfr.gov now 3 posts in b7 — AT CAP.)
5. CPSC Art Materials business guidance — toxicology review at least every 5 years; conformance statement required regardless of hazard.
6. Project Gutenberg #1065, Poe, The Raven (1845) — exempt domain; public-domain text for design 7.
