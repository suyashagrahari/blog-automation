## Phase 3: sources (all fetched 2026-10-14)
1. Royal Mail, International Tracked: Europe 3–5 working days (3–4 to 10 named countries), worldwide 5–7; £50 cover, £250 for £3.10; large letter 250g £9.90 Europe / £12.05 WZ3; 2kg small parcel USA £20.20; senders must check prohibitions and destination rules.
2. Laughter, Brown & Anantheswaran 2024, Food Sci Nutr (Wiley, CC BY), Europe PMC PMC11561827: chocolate softens at 28–32°C and melts at 34–36°C. Full text read.
3. Australia Post, prohibited and restricted items: nail polish, paint, perfume are flammable liquids; lithium batteries only when installed (max two batteries / four cells).
4. Australia Post, international post guide: India: foodstuffs need receiver permissions and paperwork plus FSSAI compliance; food prohibited on International Courier. (The US guide, read but not linked, lists foodstuffs among the items to confirm with local authorities.)
