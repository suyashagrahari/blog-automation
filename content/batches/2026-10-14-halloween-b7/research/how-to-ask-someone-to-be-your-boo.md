## Phase 3: sources (all full text read via the Europe PMC REST API on 2026-10-15)
1. Baxter et al. 2022, PNAS (PMC9659375): n = 559, 6,600+ speed dates. Relationship-effect OR was 1.56 for contact initiation and 1.43 for hanging out or corresponding. This paper is now also cited by 340, which puts it at the URL cap of 2.
2. Chang et al. 2021, SCAN (PMC7812630): 55 participants. Directional body-sway coupling predicted long-term interest beyond rated attractiveness. The sample was other-gender seekers only.
3. Silver et al. 2026, SCAN (PMC13597127): fMRI study, n = 30, ages 18–29. Positive compatibility feedback raised romantic interest and negative feedback lowered it.
4. Kranz et al. 2024, Archives of Sexual Behavior (PMC11335949): n = 445. Heterosexual participants showed negative affect and avoidance toward same-gender flirters at the same level that lesbian and gay participants showed toward other-gender flirters.
