There were about 5 weak results in the top 9. There was no AI Overview data. Overlap with b2 halloween-decorations-ideas and diy-halloween-decorations: 0 shared URLs measured (different SERPs), so there is no cannibalisation by URL.

**Table stakes:** colour palette (orange, black, purple), candles, pumpkins, budget props.
**Gap:** none of the pages gives a single measurement (cover size, runner length, centrepiece height) and none covers safety at the table (flame near plastic or paper, dry-ice fog in drinks, decorative ceramics holding food). None organises by table size or shape.
**Fan-out:** what is a tablescape; how tall should a centrepiece be; how much space per place setting; runner length; real candles on the table; dry ice in drinks; candy in decorative bowls; small table for two.

**Angle:** this post wins by being the only one that sizes décor to the table (60 × 35 cm covers, free centre strip, centrepiece height from seated eye line), backs the height rule with a 2026 visual-contact study, and adds table-specific fire, fog and food-contact rules.

## Phase 3: sources (all fetched 2026-10-14)
1. Koul & Novembre 2026, *Ann NY Acad Sci*, PMC13271046 (CC BY, full text read). 38 dyads, 2-minute silent trials. Heart-rate and pupil synchrony with visual contact dropped significantly when a screen or occluder separated the pair. This is the centrepiece-height argument.
2. Zaldo Arredondo et al. 2025, *Cureus*, PMC12103062 (CC BY, full text read). A 34-year-old had a gastric perforation after a liquid-nitrogen cocktail. Mechanism: rapid gas expansion.
3. London Fire Brigade, Candles (undated). LED candles described as much safer. Tea lights can melt through plastic surfaces without proper holders. Keep flames away from paper and clothes. Stop, drop, roll.
4. US FDA, Q&A on Lead-Glazed Traditional Pottery (content current as of 2017-10-27). Problem types include pottery brightly decorated in orange, red or yellow. Decorative-only pieces may carry a "Not for Food Use—May Poison Food" stamp. Washing does not remove lead.

Read and dropped:
- Gaffney et al. 2026, *Nutrients* (MDPI), PMC13415140. A systematic review: 4 studies, 64 older adults; red tableware went with 24.6% more food eaten. Dropped because MDPI is already at 3 posts in b7, so BRIEF §10's "one MDPI slot left" was stale.
- Food Quality and Preference 2020, on lighting and table linen. Elsevier, banned.
- Frontiers conversation-synchrony papers. Banned publisher.

Bot walls not worked around: FENZ and Ottawa (Incapsula), West Midlands FS (verification page). NFPA's candles page is JS-rendered. USFA, Scottish FRS and CFA candle URLs returned 404.
