## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched 2026-10-13)
1. Meta for Developers, IG User Media reference (updated 2026-09-28): feed JPEG 8 MB, 4:5–1.91:1, 320–1440 px; story 8 MB, 9:16 recommended; alt_text for image posts from 2025-03-24, Reels/Stories not supported.
2. TechRadar, 17 Aug 2023: WhatsApp HD option, standard 1600×1052 vs HD 4096×2692 (beta screenshots). Mainstream tech news, not a competitor. faq.whatsapp.com is client-rendered (empty to fetch).

4. Yu, Shamsi & Kwon 2022, J Vis 22(10):4, PMC9465940 — peer-reviewed, OA (CC BY-NC-ND, cited only). n=14; contrast/blur slow reading; background luminance no significant effect.
5. Creative Commons, About CC Licenses — six licences; ND unadapted only; SA same terms; NC definition.
6. Chiarella, Yarbrough & Jackson 2020, Nat Commun 11:5803, PMC7670408 — CC BY comment; 11.84% of 9M+ tweets had images, 0.1% of those described; alt-text practices.
