## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched 13 Oct 2026)
1. Orui et al. 2023, Neuropsychobiology (Karger), PMC10614439, CC BY-NC (cited, not reproduced) — full text: 30 healthy young adults, 3-minute Netcraft task alone / parallel / nonparallel; parasympathetic activity significantly higher parallel vs alone.
2. Mak et al. 2023, Nature Medicine, PMC10504079, CC BY — full text: N = 93,263 aged 65+, 16 countries; hobby linked with fewer depressive symptoms, higher health, happiness, life satisfaction; prevalence 51.0% Spain to 96.0% Denmark. Hobbies of any kind (HRS etc.), not crafts specifically.
3. Israel, Mackenstadt & Adams-Price 2021, Innovation in Aging 5 Suppl 1, PMC8679504 — ABSTRACT ONLY: 239 women aged 40-84; identity with creative hobby linked to more health anxiety/depressive symptoms; age moderated stress (middle-aged high-identifiers most stress, older least).
4. NFPA Candle Safety page (Firecrawl scrape; plain fetch returned empty JS shell) — 2020-2024: 5,894 home candle fires/yr, 63 deaths, 532 injuries; over half started when decor such as paper was too close; 12-inch clearance. No publication date.
