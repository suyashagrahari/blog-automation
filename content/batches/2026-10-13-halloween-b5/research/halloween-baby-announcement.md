## Phase 2: gap
- Table stakes: photo ideas, a few captions.
- Gap: nobody covers *timing* (dating scan 10–14 weeks; 31 Oct 2026 Sat / 2027 Sun), *who to tell before posting*, *pregnancy-loss sensitivity* (October is US Pregnancy and Infant Loss Awareness Month since 1988; UK Baby Loss Awareness Week 9–15 Oct), *scan-photo privacy*, or lines sorted by audience (grandparents, sibling, public, rainbow baby, birth, work).
- Opinion carried: skip skeleton-baby / "Deady and Mummy" death puns (both incumbents use them).
- Fan-out: when to announce; who first; is Halloween insensitive; wording by audience; photo ideas without a bump; formats compared; sending to a far-away grandparent.

Angle: wins by being the only post that treats the announcement as a timing and sensitivity decision with 30 original lines sorted by audience, backed by the 38.5% password rate and 11.1 views/page.
