## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: a long list of short captions, some funny, some spooky, some cute.
- Gap: nobody sorts by the shot (mirror, close-up, half-done, low-light, no-costume), nobody says how long a caption should be for each, nobody explains Story vs grid, nobody cites anything.
- Stale: both Elite Daily pages are 2018–2020 content.
- Fan-out: what to caption a mirror selfie / a face-paint close-up / a dark photo / no costume; how long; Story or feed; why people post selfies; selfie quotes vs captions; posting friends/kids; sending to one person.
- Angle: wins by being the only post that sorts Halloween selfie captions by the shot itself with a length per type, tied to Fam 2024's four selfie motives and Qutub 2021's Story-vs-grid audience finding, using the 48.3% phone-open share and 11.1 views per page.
