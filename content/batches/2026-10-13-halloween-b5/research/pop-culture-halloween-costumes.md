## Phase 2: gap
- Table stakes: KPop Demon Hunters, Toy Story 5, the year's horror villains, superheroes, Michael Jackson.
- Gap: no page ties a trend to a primary figure. Every incumbent leans on a retailer's list, a gambling brand's survey or a TikTok video. None gives box office, the streamer's own top 10, international reach or whether a costume will still read next year.
- Fan-out: what is trending (H2 list), what makes a costume trending (definition), is KPDH still big, the biggest film of 2026, horror picks, will it work in 2027, what travels internationally, cheap options, legal and character ownership, how to share the photo.
