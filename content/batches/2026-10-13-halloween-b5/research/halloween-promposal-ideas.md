# Research brief — halloween-promposal-ideas

- Keyword: **halloween promposal ideas** (absorbs "halloween hoco proposal") · rank 244 · theme couple
- Batch: 2026-10-13-halloween-b5 · written 2026-10-13
- SERP method: **WebSearch (US index)**, 2 searches used (budget 2): the keyword, plus one for a published school rule on promposals.
- Audience: high-school students aged 14–18. Clean, consent-aware, school-rule-aware.

## Phase 0 — first-party data

There is no Halloween, promposal or teen data in `content/facts.md`. Three platform-wide lines are used verbatim: 48.3% of views on a phone (57,456 views), 11.1 average views per page, and 38.5% password rate, all measured 2026-09-24. The first two sit in the first 150 words and set the rule that a sign must read in a phone photo. The template spec supplies product facts: the question is editable to 60 characters, the regular card's No button runs away (so it cannot record a real no), and premium has a 280-character reply box.

## Phase 2 — gap

- Table stakes: themed ideas and sign wording.
- Gap: no ranking page tells a teen to ask privately before a public ask, mentions food allergies for candy asks, or quotes a school rule. None notes that most Halloween asks are hoco asks, since prom is in spring.
- Fan-out: what is a Halloween promposal and when; how to ask privately; ideas by how public they are; sign lines; candy and allergies; school rules; using a card; when to skip.
