## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: a list of copyable thank-you lines; some puns.
- Gap: nobody sorts by sender → recipient (host→all, host→helper, guest→host, parent after kids' party, organiser→volunteers, host→neighbours); nobody gives channel + send-by dates; nobody addresses public vs private thanks; nobody admits handwriting may not matter.
- Fan-out: what should it say; how soon; group chat vs individual; guest thanking host; kids' party thank-you for gifts; volunteers; neighbours; puns; handwritten vs text.
- Angle: "Wins by being the only post that sorts Halloween party thank-you messages by who is thanking whom and through which channel, with send-by dates for 2026 and 2027 and two gratitude studies on public versus private thanks, using SubhSandesh's 48.3% phone-open share and 11.1 views per page."
