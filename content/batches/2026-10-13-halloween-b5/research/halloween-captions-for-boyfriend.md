- Fan-out: what caption for his costume photo; tag him or not; soft launch; hard launch; long-distance; ask first; length; hashtags; private alternative; date.
- Angle: wins by being the only post that treats the boyfriend as the subject, with a per-shot tagging rule and an ask-first check grounded in co-owned-photo research, set against 38.5% of creators password-protecting their pages.
