## Phase 3: sources (all fetched)
1. https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC13355920 (Hu, Liang, Du, Annals NYAS, July 2026; Wiley; full text via Europe PMC XML). 118 Mandarin-speaking preschoolers; singing beat rote recitation for speed, accuracy and fluency; advantage persisted at 6 months. Peer-reviewed, open access, subject test pass.
2. https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC10445917 (Andreetta et al., Open Research Europe, v2 23 Feb 2023, first published 28 May 2021). Meter integrity no significant effect for Dante; for Ariosto removing each component downgrades memory; fully metric versions took longer. Open peer review mixed: v2 one Approved (List), one Not Approved (Blohm and Versace); v1 had two Not Approved. Disclosed in body as "reviewers split".
3. https://www.hsa-haiku.org/hsa-definitions-2004.html (HSA Definitions Committee, 18 Sep 2004). Most English haiku are three lines of 17 or fewer syllables; about 12 English syllables approximate 17 Japanese on; senryu are often humorous.
4. https://web.law.duke.edu/cspd/publicdomainday/2026/ (Jenkins and Boyle, Jan 2026). Works from 1930 enter the US public domain on 1 Jan 2026; US law only. Also used by b5 cartoon-couple-costumes (URL count now 2, the cap).
