## Phase 3: sources (all fetched)
1. Wassiliwizky et al. 2017, SCAN (OUP), europepmc PMC5597896. Full text read: N=27, all reported chills, 11 (40.7%) piloerection on video, and chills cluster at the ends of lines, stanzas and poems.
2. Xu, Jia & Zheng 2026, Communications Biology (Nature), europepmc PMC13627084. Full text read: 70 participants, a four-day intervention on the first six stanzas of The Raven, where only prosody training raised arousal and rhythmic performance, transferring to untrained stanzas.
3. gutenberg.org/ebooks/1279, Poems and Songs of Robert Burns: "Halloween" with Burns's own footnotes (nut charm). The 1785 date is inferred from the chronological placement before "To A Mouse, November 1785".
4. copyright.cornell.edu/publicdomain, updated 1 January 2026: works first published in the US before 1931 are in the public domain.
