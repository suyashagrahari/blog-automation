## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes** (must be present or the page looks incomplete): actual Hindi lines in Devanagari; timing and privacy advice; the difference between a confession and a marriage proposal; something for the "she said give me time" case.

**The gap — four things nobody on this SERP has:**
1. **The register decision.** तुम / आप / तू is a real, binding grammatical choice in Hindi, and getting it wrong is the failure mode. No competitor mentions it.
2. **Spoken Hindi versus ghazal diction.** प्यार versus मोहब्बत/इश्क़ is the difference between "I said this" and "I forwarded this". No competitor names it.
3. **A reason to use Hindi at all.** The L1-emotion literature is real, strong and entirely absent from this SERP.
4. **Transliteration discipline.** Devanagari + Roman + English gloss on every line, with an explanation of why both scripts ship.

**Stale data:** not applicable — there is no data on these pages to be stale.

**Unanswered questions they raise and drop:** what if she does not read Devanagari well; what does a woman speaker change; is shayari a good idea or not; which line is a marriage proposal and which is not.

## Hindi line audit

**13 Devanagari lines shipped**, each with Roman transliteration and English gloss. **5 drafted lines cut** as wrong or unverifiable:

1. `मैंने बहुत सोचा, और हर बार जवाब तुम ही निकलीं` — gender/number agreement between masculine जवाब and feminine निकलीं does not resolve cleanly.
2. `क्या तुम मेरे साथ रहना चाहोगी?` — grammatical but ambiguous; reads as a cohabitation proposal.
3. `मुझे तुमसे मोहब्बत है, तुम मेरी रूह हो` — mixes Urdu-derived register into a spoken frame in a way I could not place as natural speech.
4. `तुम्हारे बिना मैं अधूरा हूँ` — correct but a film cliché, and not a proposal; cut under the post's own rule.
5. `क्या तुम मेरी हो जाओगी?` — correct and common; cut for possessive framing, not for grammar.

One further line, `तुम्हारे इश्क़ में हम फ़ना हो गए`, is shipped **only as a labelled counter-example** of ghazal register, never as a recommendation.
