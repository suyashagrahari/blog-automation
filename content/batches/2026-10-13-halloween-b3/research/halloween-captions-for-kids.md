## Brief claims checked (§0)
- Prompt framed asking "a child aged 7+". The only child-voice study found (Sarkadi 2020 via Berg 2024) says children from age 4 want to be asked, and the 4–6 group was the LEAST accepting of posting. The post therefore says ask from ~4, treat a no as final from ~7 (MedlinePlus: body image forms ~6). 7+ alone would understate the evidence.
- Research is thin: only 68 of 8,643 participants in the field's 27 studies were children.
