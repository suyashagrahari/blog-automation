2. Australian Museum, Spiders — https://australian.museum/learn/animals/spiders/ — eight walking legs; usually eight eyes, some fewer; most see poorly; Goliath spider leg span up to 28 cm. Undated.
3. Agnarsson, Kuntner, Blackledge 2010, PLOS ONE (CC BY), PMC2939878 — dragline toughness ~150 MJ/m³ outperforms steel and Kevlar; C. darwini 350 MJ/m³ average, up to 520, >10× Kevlar. Full text read. Note: the comparison is TOUGHNESS, not tensile strength — the post says "tougher, not stronger".
4. Wardenaar et al. 2017, Psychological Medicine (Cambridge), PMC5674525 — 25 surveys, 22 countries, n=124,902; lifetime specific phobia 7.4%; animal subtype most common at 3.8% lifetime. Full text read (PMC HTML; Europe PMC fullTextXML unavailable, link via europepmc per BRIEF §9).
