## Phase 2 — gap
- **Table stakes:** grouped copyable lists; puns; couples; pumpkin/ghost/witch themes; short lines.
- **Gap:** every ranking page sorts by mood, pun type or costume theme. None sorts by *where* the celebration happened (office/school, trunk-or-treat, haunted attraction or festival, travel, first Halloween abroad, home alone, after a hard year). None addresses Día de los Muertos respectfully, the expat/first-timer (relevant to the India/global audience), or cites any research. None has an outfit-only "look" set distinct from makeup.
- **Stale data:** Romper 2021, Plannthat 2022.
- **Fan-out:** what is a celebration caption; office-safe caption; trunk-or-treat caption; haunted house caption; travel/Salem/Edinburgh/Mexico City caption; first Halloween abroad; alone; after a loss; halloween look/OOTD caption; caption length; send to one person.
- **Angle:** Wins by being the only post that sorts Halloween celebration captions by where the celebration happened plus a separate Halloween look set, backed by open-access research on international students, expatriates and migrant festival-goers, using SubhSandesh's 48.3% phone-open rate to keep lines phone-sized.

## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched via Europe PMC REST; all CC BY)
1. Asatsa et al. 2025, Proc. R. Soc. B — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC12404807 — mourning rituals helped process grief and reinforced social ties; some rituals perceived negatively. Abstract + methods read in full text. (Used in "after a hard year".)
2. Zhao 2025, PLOS ONE — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC12064037 — 438 Chinese students in Malaysia; all three social-media motivations improved adaptation, entertainment most; mainstream (host) orientation contributed more. Abstract + sample section read.
3. Billah, Akhtar & Khan 2023, BMC Psychology — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC10148631 — 21,439 expatriates (COVIDiSTRESS, 30 Mar–30 May 2020); moderate correlation of perceived stress with loneliness; recommends strong migrant–local community links. **Claims limited to the abstract.**
4. Abbasian 2022, IJERPH — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC9819510 — 280 mostly immigrant participants at Stockholm's Fire Festival (March 2019, 16,000 attendees, five hours); good feelings, then integration and community, return of spring, nostalgia. Abstract + context read.
