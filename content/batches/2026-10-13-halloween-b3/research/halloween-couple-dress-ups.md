## Phase 3: sources (all fetched)
1. **ACCC Product Safety, Nightwear for children mandatory standard** (productsafety.gov.au). Sizes 00–14; 4 categories; Categories 1–3 low fire hazard label "CAUTION: Keep away from heat and flame"; Category 4 high fire hazard warning label; too-flammable garments can't be sold; **excludes "detachable capes, hoods, wings, tutus and similar costume accessories"**. Undated. The live site returned 403 after a few requests, so the text was verified on the web.archive.org snapshot of 14 August 2026.
2. **Cancer Council Australia, UV Index** (cancer.org.au). SunSmart steps whenever UV ≥ 3; 15 minutes unprotected at that level can start skin damage; SPF 50+, reapply every 2 hours. Undated.
3. **Horsham et al. 2020, JMIR mHealth uHealth** (Europe PMC PMC7531871, CC BY, full text read). Gold Coast schoolies festival, November 2019: UV Index above 3 for over 6 hours a day; average max 28°C; 31.4% (59/188) sunburnt; long-sleeve and hat use fell among sensor users. Peer-reviewed, open access, Australian.
4. **Tarkang, Alola, Yumuk 2022, DECISION** (Europe PMC PMC8753943, full text read). 513 married couples in Turkey; holiday decision tactics positively associated with emotional well-being and couple satisfaction. Correlational, holiday-specific; caveated in the body. Not used by b1/b2. (PMC13203665, the obvious couple-leisure paper, was rejected because b1 used it heavily.)

Rejected: Sage (SPPS PMC13588490), Taylor & Francis (Leisure Sciences couple-vacation paper), ARPANSA (fetch failed).
Searches tried for face paint over sunscreen ("face paint contact dermatitis heavy metals", via findpapers) found nothing on the combination, so the body labels those steps as our own rules of thumb.

## Brief claims checked
- The prompt asked for the ACCC position "on costumes". The nightwear standard explicitly **excludes** costume accessories, and we found no Australian mandatory standard for adult costumes. The post says so.
- The productsafety.gov.au URLs we guessed from older site structure 404'd. The current URL is `/business/search-mandatory-standards/nightwear-for-children-mandatory-standard`.
