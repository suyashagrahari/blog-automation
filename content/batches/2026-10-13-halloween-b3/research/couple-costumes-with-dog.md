## Phase 3: sources (6; all fetched and read in full)

1. Ding & Liu 2026, *Behavioral Sciences*, PMC13603805, via europepmc.org, CC BY. A survey of 565 pet owners: attachment is linked to wanting co-fashion, mediated by relational identity. The paper also says pets "cannot negotiate the symbolic meaning of a shared appearance". It was used in b1 (halloween-fancy-dress-for-couples) **and in b2 (halloween-couple-outfit-ideas)**; the prompt mentioned only b1. This is its first use in b3.
2. Gardeweg, Picard & van Herwijnen 2026, *PLOS ONE*, PMC13042741, CC BY. Covers 530 owners and 117 non-owners. Nose licking and yawning scored lowest as pain signs. Owners may read turning away and freezing as stress or fear.
3. Hall, Carter & O'Neill 2020, *Scientific Reports*, PMC7303136, CC BY. Covers 905,543 UK dogs in VetCompass. Brachycephalic dogs had an odds ratio of 2.10 and dogs over 50 kg an odds ratio of 3.42 against dogs under 10 kg. There were no heat-related illness events in October 2016.
4. Merck Veterinary Manual, chocolate toxicosis. Written by an ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center DVM; full review September 2024, updated February 2026. Thresholds: 20 mg/kg mild signs, 40–50 mg/kg heart effects, ≥60 mg/kg seizures.
5. Merck Veterinary Manual, xylitol toxicosis in dogs. Updated June 2025. Above about 100 mg/kg causes hypoglycaemia; above 500 mg/kg can cause liver failure.
6. GOV.UK, "Get your dog or cat microchipped" (first published 2023-08-24). Dogs must be chipped by 8 weeks, must wear a collar and tag with name and address in public, and an unchipped dog can bring a fine of up to £500.
