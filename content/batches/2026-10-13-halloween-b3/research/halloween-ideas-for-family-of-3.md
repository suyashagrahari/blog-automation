## Phase 2 — gap

- **Table stakes:** licensed-character trios, a baby/toddler option, DIY vs bought.
- **Gap:** nobody sorts by the child's *role*; nobody gives child comfort (warmth, toilet, mobility) per set; nobody
  covers one parent + two kids or parent + grandparent + child as distinct shapes; no night logistics for two adults
  and one child; no only-child angle; no sources at all.
- **Stale data:** none cited anywhere to supersede.
- **Fan-out:** easy with a baby · cost · one parent two kids · grandparent · child refuses to match · who hands out
  candy · only child + friend · toddler safety on a long walk · sending photos to grandparents · Halloween 2027 date.
- **Angle:** wins by being the only post that sorts family-of-3 costumes by the child's role, with comfort, age and
  cost per set, a 7pm hand-off plan and evidence-checked only-child and three-generation notes.

## Sibling de-duplication

Read b2 `family-halloween-costumes` (24 sets by family shape), b1 `trio-halloween-costumes` (36 friend trios) and b2
`halloween-costume-ideas-for-kids`. No costume idea repeated. b3 `couple-costumes-with-baby` and
`halloween-ideas-for-toddlers` were not on disk at writing time. **Brief claim found false-ish:** the prompt's connector
example "sandwich filling" is already b1's "Two slices of bread and the filling", so it was omitted. Licensed
characters used by the top 5 (Oz, Incredibles, Addams, Poppins, Waldo, chef/spaghetti, beekeepers) also avoided.
