## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: a long list of short punny captions; Instagram framing.
- Gap: no page sorts party captions by the party's timeline or by who posts (host, guest, contest loser, parent); none covers how to get usable photos at a dark party, consent before posting guests, posting other people's children at a kids' party, or the morning after. Three of the four readable pages recycle the same "creepin' it real" line.
- Stale data: none of the pages cite data at all.
- Fan-out: host décor caption; arrival caption; costume contest captions; snack table; dance floor; photo booth; late night; morning after/thank-you; kids' party; should I ask before posting; when to post; dark-room photos.
- Angle: wins by being the only post that sorts Halloween party captions along the party's timeline and by role, paired with a host's photo checklist (one photographer, backdrop, night lighting, consent), backed by 2023–2025 open-access studies, using SubhSandesh's 48.3% phone-view and 38.5% password-protect rates.

## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched; full text read via Europe PMC XML unless noted)
1. Lurie, Fabrizio & Westerman (7 Apr 2025), *Memory & Cognition* (Springer/Psychonomic), CC BY — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC13332969 — three experiments; memory consistently worse for photographed or screenshotted images; screenshotting particularly detrimental. Supports "one person on photos".
2. Hendriks, Le, Gebhardt, van den Putte & Vanherle (13 May 2023), *IJERPH* 20(10):5820 (MDPI), CC BY — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC10218174 — N = 292 Dutch students aged 16–28; most did not consider alcohol posts a problem; the paper's intervention idea 4 cites earlier research on next-day regret after drunk sharing (secondary claim, attributed as "the authors cite").
3. Conti, Del Parco et al. (30 Jul 2024), *Italian Journal of Pediatrics* 50:135 (BMC), CC BY — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC11290302 — 228 parents; 98% on social media, 75% of them published child content; sharing personal events the first reason; 93% unaware of law and risks. NOTE: also used by b3 `halloween-captions-for-baby` → this paper is now at the 2-post cap.
4. Apple Support, "Take Night mode photos with your iPhone camera" (undated, checked 2026-10-13) — https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/take-night-mode-photos-iph1a3c5b4c3/ios — Night mode turns on automatically in low light on supported models; tripod tip.
