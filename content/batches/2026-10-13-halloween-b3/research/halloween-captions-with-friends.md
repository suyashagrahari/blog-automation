## Phase 2 — gap analysis
- Table stakes: pun-heavy list, "ghoul/boo/witch" wordplay, Instagram framing.
- Gap: nobody sorts by the group photo actually taken; nobody addresses who posts or asking friends before tagging; no data at all; no clean group-vs-best-friend distinction.
- Stale: elitedaily lists dated 2019/2020.
- Fan-out: what caption for a group photo; who posts; do I need to ask; Instagram-ready; quotes with friends; one friend didn't dress up; blurry photo; work friends; date of Halloween.
- Angle: wins by being the only post that sorts Halloween captions with friends by group photo type (3+ friends) and pairs each with who posts and how much asking it needs, backed by a photo-sharing workaround study and a group-friendship study.
