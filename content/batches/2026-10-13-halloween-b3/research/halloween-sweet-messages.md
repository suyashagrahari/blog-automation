## Phase 2: gap
- **Table stakes:** grouped lists by recipient (kids, friends, family, partner), puns, short simple lines.
- **Gap:** none of the pages treats "sweet" as candy wordplay meant for a physical treat tag. None gives tag sizes or word budgets per reader, and none offers lines for people avoiding sugar. None cites any research.
- **Stale:** Hallmark's list dates from 2021.
- **Fan-out:** what to write on a treat tag; tag size and word count; sugar-free or diabetes-friendly notes; partner versus kids lines; bag labels; coworker and neighbour treats; does a small note matter.
- **Angle:** Wins by being the only post that serves both meanings of "sweet" (candy-wordplay treat tags and tender lines), gives tag sizes and word budgets per recipient, and includes respectful sugar-free lines grounded in a 2025 UK diabetes-stigma study.

## Phase 3: sources (all via findpapers.mjs and Europe PMC, no search budget used)
1. Lim et al., KIND Challenge RCT, Soc Psychiatry Psychiatr Epidemiol, 19 Aug 2024, CC BY, full text read. https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC12031860. 4,284 adults in the USA, UK and Australia; at least one act of kindness for a neighbour per week for 4 weeks; small loneliness reductions in USA (d=-0.13) and UK (d=-0.21), none in Australia. **This is the prompt's kindness RCT.**
2. Kumar & Epley, J Exp Psychol Gen, online 18 Aug 2022: givers underestimate the positive impact of random acts of kindness (hot chocolate in a park). Abstract only. https://europepmc.org/article/MED/35980709
3. Schaefer, Reinhardt, Garbow & Dressler, Psychological Research, 27 Apr 2020: 517 participants; sweet candies versus salty snacks raised attractiveness ratings and confirmed prosocial effects. Abstract only. https://europepmc.org/article/MED/32338299
4. Housby et al., Diabetic Medicine, online 13 Dec 2025, CC BY, full text read. https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC12857862. 96% endorsed at least one stigma instance; among 337 responses, "unsolicited comments and advice" was an experience sub-theme, with quotes about remarks on sugary food.
