## Phase 2 — gap analysis

**Table stakes.** A long list of wishes in Devanagari; categories by relationship
(elder/younger brother, long-distance); short WhatsApp-length options; the festival date.

**The gap.** Not one page helps the reader *evaluate* the Marathi they are about to
send. Every page assumes the reader can already tell Marathi from Hindi typed in
Devanagari. Most of the audience for this query cannot — that is precisely why they are
searching in English for wishes in Marathi. Specifically, nobody covers:

1. The Hindi loanwords that expose a machine translation (रिश्ता, भाई, ज़िंदगी, हमेशा, दिल,
   प्यार, खुशी, बहुत) and their Marathi equivalents.
2. Marathi's two second persons (तू / तुम्ही) and which a sister uses for a brother.
3. Festival vocabulary that only exists on the Marathi side — औक्षण (the aarti before
   tying), ओवाळणी (what the brother gives afterwards), माहेर (a married woman's
   parental home).
4. An English gloss under every wish, so a sister who speaks Marathi but reads it
   slowly can choose deliberately.
5. Whether the Devanagari will actually render on the recipient's handset.
6. What to do with the wish once copied. Every gifting-brand page resolves to
   couriering a physical object, which they cannot avoid — the shipment is the revenue.

**Stale data.** marathiya.com carries the 2025 date on a page published 2026-03-12.
tenorshare.com is dated 2024-07-25 and still labelled with condolence-message headings.

**Fan-out sub-queries → H2s:**
- what makes a wish Marathi rather than Hindi → H2 2
- marathi rakhi wishes for brother → H2 3 (8 numbered H3s)
- short marathi rakhi message whatsapp → H2 4
- narali purnima / puran poli / rakhi in maharashtra → H2 5
- when is raksha bandhan 2026 → inside H2 5
- when not to write in marathi → H2 6
- how to send a marathi rakhi wish → H2 7

**Angle.** Wins by being the only post that shows a reader how to tell genuine Marathi
from Hindi typed in Devanagari — a word-swap table of eight Hindi tells, the तू/तुम्ही
register rule, and an English gloss under all 14 wishes — paired with SubhSandesh's 11.8
average reopens across 31,081 views of 2,632 pages.

## Phase 6 — where this post is weak

Recorded in full in `batchMeta.auditReport.failed`. In short:

1. The slug keeps the stop words "for" and "in" (fixed by the assignment).
2. The census figure is sourced one step removed from the primary table.
3. Wikipedia appears as four link instances (two entities, each linked twice).
4. Every first-party number is platform-wide, not rakhi- or Marathi-specific.
5. IGP entered this SERP after the batch brief was written, so the "uncontested" premise
   in the brief no longer holds.

**The load-bearing risk.** The entire advantage rests on the Marathi being genuine
rather than translated. It is written using Marathi-only vocabulary (माहेर, औक्षण,
ओवाळणी, जिव्हाळा-register words like काळीज, पाठीशी), Marathi verb morphology (आहेस,
कळतं, यायचा, मागते with feminine agreement for the sister-speaker), Marathi correlatives
(तेवढंच, कितीही, यंदा) and Marathi particles and vocatives (रे, भावा). If that judgement is
wrong, the post has no advantage left over the pages analysed.
