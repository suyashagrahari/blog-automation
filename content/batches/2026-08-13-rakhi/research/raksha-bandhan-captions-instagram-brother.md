## Phase 2 — gap analysis

**Table stakes** (present on all five, so the post must not omit them): captions
segmented for a brother specifically; a mix of emotional / funny / short; Hindi
or Hinglish flavour; explicit "copy-paste" framing; the festival named and dated.

**The gap — and it is the whole thesis.** Every ranking page is an
undifferentiated list of 100–400 captions. **Not one of them addresses
caption/photo fit or caption length.** Specifically, none covers:

- which caption suits which post format — carousel of childhood photos vs a
  single portrait vs a Reel;
- Instagram's actual caption limits (2,200 chars / 30 hashtags / 20 @ tags);
- where the caption truncates in-feed (~125 characters) — so rakhibazaar's
  40–60 word captions are mostly invisible in the feed and the page never says so;
- the 10-item carousel cap, or that Reels cannot sit inside a carousel;
- character counts for the captions they supply.

This is verifiable against primary Meta documentation, which is what makes it
defensible rather than merely a different opinion.

**Stale data:** ScoopWhoop is dated 18 Aug 2024 and Wolf Global 26 May 2024. Both
predate the current Meta docs cited here (updated Jun and Aug 2026).

**Unanswered questions the pages raise and drop:** how long should a caption be;
will the whole thing show; how many hashtags; what to post when the brother is
abroad; when during the day to post.

**Fan-out sub-queries → became H2s or FAQs:** how many characters can an Instagram
caption be · where does Instagram cut a caption off · caption for a carousel ·
caption for a Reel · when is Raksha Bandhan 2026 · what time to post · caption for
a long-distance brother · how many hashtags · how many people can I tag.

**Angle:** wins by being the only Raksha Bandhan caption page that matches caption
length and type to the actual post format — carousel, single portrait or Reel —
against Instagram's documented 2,200-character cap and 125-character in-feed
truncation point, grounded in SubhSandesh's own 52.5% phone-open rate across
31,081 views.

## Resolved in the audit fix pass (2026-08-13)

1. **Slug reissued before first publish** from `raksha-bandhan-captions-for-instagram`
   to `raksha-bandhan-captions-instagram-brother`. The old slug dropped "brother",
   the qualifier that carries the exact primary keyword; the new one carries every
   content word of "raksha bandhan caption for instagram with brother" while staying
   free of stop words, which the publish checklist requires of slugs. `article.slug`,
   `canonicalURL` and the `#post` `@id` were all updated together. Nothing was
   published under the old slug, so no URL was spent.
2. **FAQPage block deleted** from `structuredData`. The apparent contradiction between
   `references/structured-data.md` and `references/article-json-schema.md` was resolved:
   `client/components/JsonLd.tsx:622-637` merges a block whose `@id` matches an existing
   graph node and only type-filters unmatched blocks, so the `@id`-matched `BlogPosting`
   enrichment survives and carries the citations, while a bare `FAQPage` was dead weight.
3. **All 7 stated caption character counts re-verified** character-by-character against
   the caption strings (80, 84, 127, 73, 83, 106, 115). All exact.
