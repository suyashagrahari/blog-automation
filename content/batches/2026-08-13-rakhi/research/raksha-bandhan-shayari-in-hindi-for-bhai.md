## The shayari — provenance note

All eight shers in the post were composed for it. Each was built radif-first, then
qaafiya, then scanned by matra count (laghu = 1, guru = 2) to bring the two misra within
one or two matras of each other, and each holds a turn in the second line. Metre, qaafiya
and radif are labelled per sher in the body so the claim is checkable rather than
asserted. None of the eight appears in the four competitor pages indexed above, nor in the
AI Overview couplet set.

Honest limits: these are single standalone shers, not a full ghazal (a ghazal needs five
or more couplets on one qaafiya/radif scheme, per poets.org). The matra scansion is my
own, done by hand; no automated Hindi prosody checker was used. Two rhymes are near rather
than exact — `हिम्मत / क़िस्मत` and `उम्र / सब्र` — which is normal practice in modern
Hindi shayari but would be marked loose in a strict Urdu mushaira.
