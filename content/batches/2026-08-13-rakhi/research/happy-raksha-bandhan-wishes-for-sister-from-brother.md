## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes** — present on all five, so the post must carry them:
ready-to-copy message lines; some grouping mechanism; the festival's meaning;
a personalisation prompt; short/WhatsApp-length options.

**The gap** — none of the five do any of this:

1. **Direction.** Every page is written for a *sister wishing her brother*. The
   brother's half is a subsection at best. The #1 result is literally titled
   *replies to sister* — a page ranking first on a query about the brother's side is
   evidence the demand exists and is being served by a 2022 post with an "admin"
   byline.
2. **Timing.** Not one page tells the reader *when* to send. The muhurat on
   28 August 2026 is a 3 h 51 m window (05:57–09:48 AM). Every competitor is an
   undated wall of quotes.
3. **Why she is the one who is far away.** Nobody names the structural reason.
   PLFS 2020-21: female migration rate 47.9% vs 10.7% male; 86.8% of female
   migration is marriage. The distance in most sibling pairs was created by her
   wedding.
4. **A segmentation that is not tone.** All five segment by *heartfelt / funny /
   short / emotional*. None segments by the brother's actual situation (replying
   late, mid-argument, no money this year).
5. **An honest limit.** None names a case where its own recommendation fails.

**Stale data to supersede:** CaratLane at #2 states Raksha Bandhan 2026 falls on
**Saturday, August 9** — a carryover from 2025. Verified wrong against both Drik
Panchang and Wikipedia (28 August 2026, Friday). FlowerAura's #1 page is dated
July 2022. Neither is cited or linked; the win is simply being correct.

**Unanswered questions the pages raise and drop:** what do I reply when she messages
first; do I wish before or after she ties the rakhi; what if I cannot send money;
what if we are not on speaking terms.
