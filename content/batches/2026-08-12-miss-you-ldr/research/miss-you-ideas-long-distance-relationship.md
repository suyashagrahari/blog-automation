## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes** — all five cover some combination of: message/text ideas,
virtual date nights (watch-together, cooking, book club), care packages and
snail mail, countdowns to the next visit, and scheduled calls. The post must
touch these or it reads as incomplete.

**The gap.** Every page is either *words* (quotes to copy-paste) or *objects* (a
$99 device, a shipped care package). Nobody covers what sits between them: a
thing you make and send instantly as a link. More importantly, **not one of the
five publishes a single number about what happens after a gesture is sent** —
whether it gets opened, reopened, or read once and buried. That is exactly what
SubhSandesh's database measures.

Secondary gap: the entire SERP is US-centric — dollars, USPS, no time-zone maths
that applies to India. There is no Indian-context page for a query that a very
large Indian audience runs.

**Stale / weak data.** Lovebox's "14 million Americans in LDRs" traces to a
non-authoritative statistics aggregator with no publication date. Superseding it
with dated, official figures (MEA, India Post, Pew, a peer-reviewed study) is a
straightforward citation win.

**Unanswered questions raised and dropped.** How long anything actually takes to
arrive; whether saying "I miss you" often is counterproductive; what to do when
the time difference makes live contact impractical; when *not* to send something.

**Fan-out sub-queries** (each became an H2 or FAQ): what counts as a good miss-you
gesture · what to send when you miss someone long distance · how to say it without
sounding needy · how long a gift takes to reach someone abroad from India · do
long-distance relationships make couples closer · how to handle a large time
difference · when a digital gesture is the wrong choice.

**Angle.** Wins by being the only post that ranks miss-you gestures by whether
they actually get reopened, using SubhSandesh's own 11.7 average opens per shared
page and 52.6% phone-open rate, instead of another copy-paste quote list or a
physical-gift upsell.

## Phase 8 — Audit remediation

source (Kumar & Epley) was added, and the platform-wide caveat on the 11.7 figure
now appears in the body next to the claim. The India Post charter-version failure
