### The finding that decides the post

Pages 2 and 3 publish **sibling wishes under a corporate heading**. Their sample lines are
"warm greetings to you and your loving sibling", "enjoy this day with your brothers and
sisters". Sent by an employer to 200 employees, that is a message about the recipients'
families, from a party with no standing in them — and it silently assumes every recipient
has a sibling and observes the festival. Nobody in the SERP notices this. Page 1 sells logo
placement without ever asking whether a logo belongs on a festival greeting. Page 4 gets
closest with the phrase "to those who celebrate", then stops.
