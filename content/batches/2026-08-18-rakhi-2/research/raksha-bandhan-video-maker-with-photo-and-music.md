## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes (all of them cover these):** photos plus music as the core promise;
rakhi-themed frames or templates; "share on WhatsApp / Instagram"; a 3-step or
few-taps workflow; a personalisation claim (name, message, caption).

**The gap.** Nobody answers the question behind the query. A person searching
"video maker with photo and music" wants *a moving, music-carrying, photo-bearing
thing they can send* — and the format decision (file vs link) determines whether it
survives WhatsApp, whether the audio is legally theirs to post, whether the music
plays at all on the first tap, and whether they can tell it was opened. Every page in
the SERP sells a workflow and hides the tradeoffs.

**Stale data.** None to supersede: there is no data on these pages to be stale.
That is itself the finding — a post carrying six fetched, dated sources is doing
something no result currently does.

**Unanswered questions the SERP raises and drops.** "Add your own music" — from
where, and is that legal on a public post? "Share on Instagram and YouTube" — what
happens when Content ID matches? "HD quality" — HD at what file size, after the chat
app has re-encoded it?

**Fan-out sub-queries** (each became an H2 or an FAQ):

- what does a raksha bandhan video maker actually produce
- can I use a Bollywood / film song in a rakhi video
- when is a song free to use in India
- why doesn't music autoplay when someone opens my link
- can I tell whether my brother opened it
- does it work without internet
- what kind of song works best for a memory video
- how many photos can I include
- is a video or a link better for WhatsApp Status
- can I make it private

### Sourcing failure, recorded rather than papered over

I could not source the **WhatsApp media re-compression / size-limit** claim. It is
common knowledge that a forwarded video is re-encoded, but `blog.whatsapp.com` and
`developers.facebook.com` are banned for this batch, `faq.whatsapp.com` article pages
render client-side and returned no readable text, and one candidate FAQ id 404'd. So
the claim was **removed from the draft**, and the narrower verifiable point kept
instead: YouTube publishes the exact container/codec/sample-rate it wants for uploads.
The comparison table lost its "file size" row as a result. Search terms tried:
`whatsapp media size limit official`, `faq.whatsapp.com sending media`,
`whatsapp video compression documentation`, plus direct fetches of two FAQ ids and
the Terms of Service.

`help.instagram.com/402084904469945` (licensed music library) was also fetched and
returned an empty JS shell, so no Instagram-specific audio claim is made anywhere in
the post.

### One source swapped for batch-domain reasons

The first draft cited Talamini, Eller, Vigl & Zentner, "Musical emotions affect memory
for emotional pictures", *Scientific Reports* 12 (2022-06-23), full text read at
`pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9219376/`, for the finding that pictures emotionally
congruent with the preceding music were recognised more accurately (d′ 1.48 vs 1.35,
t(199) = 3.81, p < 0.001, n=200). Two sibling files in this batch had already taken
`pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov`, and the batch brief caps a domain at two posts, so keeping it
would have pushed the domain to three. `nature.com` and `doi.org` were both rejected as
replacements — the former returns a bot wall (305-byte body) so the claim could not be
re-verified there, and `doi.org` is already at two posts in this batch. The Fišer et al.
familiarity study replaced it: same field, verifiable, and on a domain only this post
uses.

- The rakhi template `app/(templates)/happy-rakshabandhan-to-brother/` contains **no
  `<audio>` element and no `new Audio(` call**, and `server/src/models/RakshaBandhanPage.ts`
  has no music/audio field. Fifteen other templates do carry audio (`love-gf`,
  `darling`, `missyou-gf`, `apology-dashboard`, `birthday-gf`, `holi`, `eid-mubarak`
  and others). This is the honest core of the post.
- Server-side content caps: `MAX_MEMORIES = 12`, `MAX_GREETING_LINES = 6`,
  `MAX_LEDGER = 8`; client upload cap `MAX_BYTES = 12 MB` per image.
- Animation is framer-motion scene transitions (cover → ritual → memories → promise →
  gift), so "animated page" is accurate and "video" is not.

**Limitation, stated plainly:** there is no rakhi-specific first-party number. Every
figure used is platform-wide. They pass the subject test for "making and sharing a
personalised page" but they are not festival research, and the post does not dress
them up as such.
