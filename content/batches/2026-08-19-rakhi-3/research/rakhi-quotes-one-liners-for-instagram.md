## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes:** a substantial set of short lines; sibling-specific variety; something usable
as a caption and as a status.

**The gap — none of the seven cover:**
1. Feed truncation, and that a one-line caption is the only format with nothing to hide.
2. Any evidence that brevity works, let alone causal evidence.
3. Why a second clause weakens a line (processing fluency).
4. Why lines that scan outperform lines that do not (rhyme-as-reason).
5. Which photograph each line suits — a line under the wrong picture reads as borrowed.
6. How to write your own.
7. Why a borrowed line is detectable, and what to do about it.
8. Any case for *not* using a one-liner.

**Stale data:** indiatvnews.com is stamped 2025.

**Fan-out sub-queries → H2s:** why one line beats a paragraph · where the line gets cut (table)
· why the second clause ruins it · the one-liners themselves (H3s by register) · Hindi and
Hinglish one-liners · how to write your own · why borrowed lines fail · when a one-liner is wrong.

### Angle

> Wins by being the only post that treats the one-liner as a form with a measurable rule rather
> than an inventory — brevity's causal effect on message success, processing fluency, and the
> rhyme-as-reason effect — against a SERP of 101+, 145+, 200+ and 250+ caption lists that
> explain nothing.

- Body **1,530 words** excluding FAQs; 10 FAQs. This post came in **under** the floor at 1,390

## The one-liners — an honest note

written for this post.** Every English line was checked to be **twelve words or fewer**

but a Hindi speaker should confirm before publishing.
