## Phase 2 — gap analysis

**Table stakes** (all five pages have them, so the post needs them): wishes addressed to a brother; acknowledgement that the sister cannot tie the rakhi in person; the option of sending a rakhi by post; some nod to celebrating over a call.

**The gap.** Not one of the five organises its wishes by *what the wish does*. Every one groups by sentiment ("emotional", "heartfelt", "funny") — which is a label on the mood, not on the job, so two lines in different buckets can carry the identical proposition. None labels a wish with **when to send it** or **who it suits**, so a reader scanning for one line has to read all 25 and then judge. And none counts its own repetition.

**Stale data.** There is no data on any of the five pages to be stale. sendrakhi.com is a 2023 post still ranking; igp.com and photojaanic.com year-stamp for 2026 with no 2026-specific content.

**Unanswered questions the pages raise and drop:** is it rude to send the same wish to two brothers? does a long message read as more caring than a short one? what do you send if you have already sent nothing for two years? what do you send when he does not reply?

**Fan-out sub-queries** → became H2s or FAQs: *why do all these lists sound the same* · *how do I pick one in ten seconds* · *what should a rakhi message for a far-away brother actually contain* · *how long should it be* · *when should I send it* · *can I reuse a wish for two brothers* · *is a message enough without a rakhi* · *what if he does not reply*.

**Angle (recorded as `batchMeta.angle`):** wins by being the only page in this SERP that measures the repetition it is competing with — 7 distinct propositions across the leading list's 25 lines, three of them covering 20 — and then delivers 25 originals classified by the job each one does and labelled with when to send it, grounded in 2,739 pages created and a 51.8% phone-open rate.
