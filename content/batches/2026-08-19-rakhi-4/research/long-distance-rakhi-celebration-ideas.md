## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

### Table stakes (all five cover; I must not look incomplete)

Posting a rakhi ahead of the day; some form of remote ceremony; a gift or hamper; a letter or note; a shared playlist or photo montage; cooking or eating the same thing; a plan to meet later.

### The gap — what none of the five cover

1. **A celebration that is not on a screen.** Not one page entertains the possibility. A night shift, a jailed sibling, a hospital ward, an elder with no smartphone, an estrangement, a 12.5-hour timezone gap — the SERP has nothing for any of them.
2. **Real lead times, in numbers.** Five pages recommend posting things and none says when to post. "Send it early" is the entire guidance.
3. **Two of everything.** Every page assumes one household sends and one receives. None suggests buying and posting *duplicates* so both houses hold identical objects and can run the same sequence — the mechanic that makes a parallel ritual possible.
4. **The same minute as the coordinating device.** Synchrony is treated as an accident of the call, never as the design.
5. **Food that survives transit.** "Send sweets" appears repeatedly; the moisture, milk-solid and customs problem does not.
6. **Celebrating twice as legitimate.** confettigifts has "Plan a Post-Rakhi Get-Together" as idea #10 — framed as an add-on, not as one celebration in two instalments. Nobody notices that 2026's Friday date makes it trivially practical.
7. **Asynchronous participation.** Shared albums appear as a *gift* you send, never as a container both sides add to across a day when neither can be awake at the same time.

### Stale data
