## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes** (must appear or the post reads incomplete): a video call as the core of the
celebration; dressing up and a thali; sending the rakhi ahead; sharing photos and memories;
something recorded or written; a sweet.

**The gap — and this post's mandated differentiator: the group case.** Not one of the readable pages
distinguishes two households from four. Nobody covers:

1. Who hosts, and on what basis (connection quality vs seniority).
2. What scales with eight people and what only works with two.
3. The timezone spread when a family is split IST / Gulf / UK / US, and the honest answer that two
   calls beat one bad one.
4. Crosstalk, echo, thali clatter, and the audio-anchor household.
5. Recording it, and consent inside a family group.
6. Bridging in an elder who cannot unmute, or who only has a phone.
7. A run-sheet with a time budget.

**Stale data:** not applicable — the SERP cites no data at all, which is itself the opening. Every
number in this post is either first-party (measured 2026-08-19) or from a dated, fetched source.

  retired in April 2025, and the UMass DSpace record carries no abstract, so the claim could not be
