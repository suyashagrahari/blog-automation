# Which 30, and why these

Source: `content/keywords/2026-09-25-miss-you-global/keyword-inventory.csv` (150
keywords, 19 markets), filtered to `action = new-page` and `gate_failed = ""`,
then to keywords **typed in English**, then sorted by expected clicks.

**The batch is English-only, by decision.** 13 of the top 30 rows by expected
clicks are Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Turkish or Indonesian
keywords. A page only ranks for those if the page itself is in that language, and
subhsandesh.in is an English, India-facing domain with no hreflang. Those 13 are
left in the inventory for a native-language wave that is a product decision, not a
content one. The Brazilian and Turkish SERPs are the weakest measured in the whole
run, so that wave is worth having — it is just not this batch.

**Five English rows were also dropped, with reasons:**

| Dropped | Why |
|---|---|
| `miss you` | 2 weak results, and the SERP is shared with the Rolling Stones, Oliver Tree and blink-182. A head term the skill requires be flagged unwinnable rather than drafted |
| `missing you` | Same, plus the John Waite entity |
| `missing you quotes` | Would cannibalise `miss you quotes`, which is in the batch. Same SERP, one page |
| `i miss you gif for him` | Gate 5 — the searcher copies an image and leaves |
| `miss you text art copy paste` | Gate 5, same reason |

That leaves exactly 30.
