## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes** (present on 5+ of 8, so the post must not look incomplete without them): lines for long distance; short lines usable as a status or caption; something about memories; lines for a best friend specifically as against friends generally.

**The gap, in three parts:**

1. **The register problem.** A best friend is not a romantic recipient, but almost every line on this SERP would sit unchanged in a `miss you quotes for her` post. Sent to a friend — and in India particularly between friends of different genders — a borrowed romantic line is a real misfire, and it is invisible until it has been sent. Nobody on this SERP names it. This post's central section.
2. **Friendship has no occasion calendar.** A sister has Raksha Bandhan and Bhai Dooj; a partner has an anniversary. A friendship has nothing that obliges the sentiment on a date, which is exactly why the missing has no sanctioned moment and why people reach for a borrowed line instead of composing one. The unanswered sub-question is "is it weird to send this out of nowhere?" and the psychology literature answers it directly.
3. **Sorting by the structural reason the friend is not here** rather than by mood. Four reasons that each take a different line: an open-ended move, a dated absence, a drift where nothing happened, and no absence at all.

**Stale data on the SERP:** none of the eight cites a study, a survey or a dated statistic of any kind. There is no stale number to supersede — there are no numbers.

**Fan-out sub-queries → H2s:** what is the difference between a miss you quote and a message · what do I send a best friend who moved abroad · what do I say when we have just drifted · is it weird to text a friend I miss them for no reason · will a soppy line sound romantic · what do I send a group of friends versus one person · how long should the message be.

**Angle:** wins by being the only page on this SERP that sorts miss-you lines for a best friend by the structural reason they are not here, answers whether it is odd to send one with no occasion attached using a replicated psychology finding, and names the romantic-register misfire that zero of the eight results mention.

Used for: the friend who moved is spending the first months building a new circle. *Subject test: passes — about people who have moved far from home.* JSPR appears once elsewhere in this batch (anchor row); this is its second post, under the cap of 3.

## Phase 4–5 — Draft decisions

**Structure** — inherits the cluster shape mandated by `BRIEF-WAVE2.md §0` (sort by the structural reason the person is not here, never by mood), instantiated for a friendship:

1. they moved and there is no return date; 2. they are away until a date you both know; 3. nothing happened and it has been months; 4. they are reachable and the only obstacle is that you have no occasion.

Reason 3 is where this post departs from the anchor deliberately. The anchor's third situation is "you have just argued", and its committed recommendation is **do not borrow**. For a best friend the equivalent situation is almost never a fight — it is a drift with no incident in it — and there the recommendation inverts: **borrow, precisely because there is nothing to admit** and a composed message would demand an explanation neither of you has.

**Lane held against the sibling `miss-you-quotes-for-friends`** (in flight, no file in `blogs/` at time of writing, so its H2s could not be read): this post is the **one person**; theirs is the group. The post says so explicitly and does not target `miss you quotes for friends` as a secondary. Shared SERP surface is **3 domains** (uncommonquotes, wishesmsg, wishes.com.in) but only **2 exact URLs** (wishesmsg `/miss-you-messages-for-friends/`, wishes.com.in `/miss-you-quotes-and-messages-for-friends/`) — the two uncommonquotes URLs differ between the two SERPs. That is a small correction to the task prompt's "~3 URLs".

**Differentiation from the anchor** `miss-you-quotes-for-him`: different situations (a friendship has no "late at night and I don't want to seem needy" problem; it has a "no occasion exists" problem instead), an inverted recommendation in situation 3, and a register section arguing the opposite direction — the anchor's reader *wants* the romantic register, this reader must avoid it.

**Differentiation from the sister row**: the sister row sorts by the structural reason too, but its reasons are kinship-structural (patrilocal marriage, relocation, estrangement) and its register section leans on the word *sister* itself doing the platonic signalling. A best friend has **no kinship term to fall back on** — that is the specific thing this post argues.
