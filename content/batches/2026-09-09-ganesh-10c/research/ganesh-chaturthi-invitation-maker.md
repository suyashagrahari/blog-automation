returned HTTP 200 with a `noscript` redirect to `/httpservice/retry/enablejs` and **zero result

wall the BRIEF hit as HTTP 429.

### What it leaves genuinely open — and why that is not enough for a second post

My assignment's `expectedGap` was real and it survives: **no page in either SERP, and not the AI
Overview, states how long the job takes, what you need ready before you start, or what fraction of
people finish.** The live post does not close that gap either — it answers *what to write* and
*where it goes*, not *how long and how many finish*.

But a gap is not a keyword. The gap sits inside a SERP the sibling already occupies with five
shared articles above it, and the honest read is that a second SubhSandesh page competing for the
same five tool results would split the cluster's own signals rather than add coverage. The
completion-rate material belongs **inside the live post**, not in a new one.

### Recommendation, for whoever picks this up

If the 96.0% / 5.4-hour completion story is worth telling — and the assignment's `angleSeed` is
right that it is the one keyword in the wave where the first-party data *is* the subject — the
cheapest correct move is a **new H2 added to the live `ganpati-invitation-card` post**, not a new
URL. Something to the effect of "How long a Ganpati invitation actually takes, and how many people
finish", carrying:

- 96.0% of started pages are published and shared (3,737 of 3,894) — measured 2026-09-09, platform-wide across 15 page types
- median first-save-to-last-edit **5.4 hours** on festival pages (n=89, Raksha Bandhan)
- both mandatory BRIEF §1 disclosures: the measurement is **Raksha Bandhan, not Ganesh Chaturthi**, a one-day festival against a ten-day one, so the shape is suggestive and not measured for this festival; and **n=89 is small** — no per-template or per-region rate may be derived from it, and no count of Ganesh pages projected

The HCI research lane assigned to me was **not spent**, so it is fully available to that edit or to
a reserve keyword. Searches were prepared but not executed against the literature, since the gate
tripped first: `template-based authoring defaults user output`, `form abandonment completion
web survey`, `creativity support tools template constraint`, `wizard interface task completion
HCI study`. Nothing was cited, so no batch source cap was consumed by this assignment.
