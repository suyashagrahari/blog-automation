## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes** — what any page on this query is expected to carry: flowers and garlands, a
fabric or paper backdrop, lighting, a chowki or mandap platform, rangoli, diyas, an
eco-friendly section, thermocol alternatives, and a nod to small flats.

**The gap.** All six competing pages, and the live sibling too, organise by *element* —
"marigold garlands", "fairy lights", "backdrop". Not one organises by **where in a real flat
the thing can physically go**, and not one treats the two constraints the qualifier "at home"
actually names:

1. **Floor area.** The average urban Indian dwelling is **46.1 sq m** (NSS 76th round,
   n=106,838 households). Every ranking page photographs a mandap standing in clear floor
   space that a 46 sq m flat does not have spare. Nobody states the footprint of anything.
2. **Reversibility.** **36.2% of urban households do not own their dwelling** (same source),
   so drilling, painting and permanent fixing are off the table — and after visarjan on
   25 September there is nowhere to store a built frame. Nobody mentions takedown at all.

Two further gaps my lane surfaces and nobody else touches:

3. **Ventilation.** A home mandap means dhoop and camphor burning twice a day in a room the
   size of the 30 m³ test volume used in the IJERPH incense study. Zero competing pages
   mention air, and it is a genuine small-flat problem.
4. **The removal rule is written into the regulation.** CPCB's Revised Guidelines for Idol
   Immersion (12 May 2020), clause 2.0(iii), requires that worship material — flowers,
   patri, vastras and "decorating materials" — **be removed before immersion and segregated
   in colour-coded bins.** Reversibility is not a decorating preference; part of it is a
   compliance step. The live sibling cites the thermocol ban via PIB; this is a different
   clause, a different document and a different point.

**Stale data in the competing set:** not applicable — none of the six cites any data of any
vintage. The only dated elements on those pages are the "2025"/"2026" year-stamps in titles.

**Unanswered questions the competing pages raise and drop:** what any of it costs; how long
it takes; whether it can be done without drilling; what to do with it on 25 September.

**Fan-out sub-queries → H2/FAQ candidates:** where to keep ganpati in a small flat · how to
make a mandap without drilling · ganpati decoration cost at home · what to do with
decorations after visarjan · how many days ganpati stays at home · is a dining table allowed
for sthapana · how to decorate a windowsill mandap · ventilation and dhoop in a small room.

**Angle statement.** Wins by being the only post on this keyword that treats floor area and
reversibility as the design brief — six mandap positions rated by square feet, rupees,
setup time and what they leave behind at takedown — and the only one that pairs the CPCB
removal clause with SubhSandesh's own festival lead-time measurement (median 0 days, n=89).
