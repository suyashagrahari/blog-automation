## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes** (present across the informational results; the post must carry them):

- What a WhatsApp sticker is, and where the sticker tray is.
- That packs come from somewhere outside WhatsApp and must be added one by one.
- Some handling of the video-status intent, because it is the folded secondary.
- The correct 2026 date early on.

**The gap — what nothing on either SERP covers:**

1. **Nobody publishes the format constraints.** Not one ranking page states 512 × 512, WebP,
   ≤ 100 KB, or 3–30 per pack. Those numbers are on WhatsApp's own sticker repository and explain
   every visible property of a sticker: why it is small, why it is reused, why it cannot carry a
   paragraph, why it cannot carry a name.
2. **Nobody says a downloaded pack is not personalised.** It is the same file for every sender. The
   two how-tos treat "download a pack" as the answer and stop there.
3. **Nobody distinguishes a reply from a message.** Stickers are studied — as graphicons, as turn
   constructional units, as pragmatic markers — and none of that literature appears anywhere on
   either SERP.
4. **Nobody has a dated number of any kind.** Both informational results are stamped **2024** and
   carry no statistics. There is no stale statistic to supersede because there are no statistics.
5. **The video-status intent is answered only by redistribution farms**, none of which states where
   the footage came from. Nobody names that as the problem.
6. **Nobody says when the reader will actually do this.** The competition is written for someone
   browsing at leisure; the only festival this platform has measured says people arrive on the day.

**Stale data:** both informational results year-stamped 2024 (`gizbot`, `business-standard`);
`craftyartapp` and the status-video farms carry 2025/2026 stamps but no data.

**Unanswered questions raised and dropped:** why a sticker looks the way it does; whether a
downloaded pack says anything about the sender; what to do when the same pack arrives from nine
people; what to send when a sticker is not enough.

**Fan-out sub-queries → H2/H3 targets:**

- what is a whatsapp sticker / sticker size → definition H2
- ganesh chaturthi sticker pack download → H3 1
- how to make your own whatsapp sticker → H3 2

- ganesh chaturthi whatsapp dp / photo → H3 4

- sticker vs message / are stickers rude → "reply, not a wish" H2 + objection H2
- when to send ganesh chaturthi wishes → Raksha Bandhan lead-time H2

**Angle (`batchMeta.angle`):** wins by being the only post that states WhatsApp's own published
sticker constraints — 512 × 512 WebP, ≤ 100 KB, 3–30 per pack — and uses CMC research on graphicons
to explain why a sticker is a reply that closes a thread while a personalised page is an opening,
