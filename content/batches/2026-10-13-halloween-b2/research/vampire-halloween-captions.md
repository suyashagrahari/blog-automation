Europe PMC PMC7458347 — Alterauge et al., PLOS ONE, 2020-08-31, CC BY. 95 prone burials, 60 sites; vampire in written sources of Balkans/Eastern Europe from 11th c.; 18th-c. attack reports limited to Serbia, Romania, Poland, Lithuania, Russia; modern perception shaped by 19th-c. English literature.
5. Europe PMC PMC4245124 — Gregoricka et al., PLOS ONE, 2014-11-26, CC BY. Drawsko 1 (17th–18th c.), n=6 deviant burials, 5 with sickle over throat/abdomen, 2 with stones beneath chin; Sr isotopes from 60 individuals: all suspected vampires local.
6. Europe PMC PMC12137733 — Bonczarowska et al., Scientific Reports, 2025-06-04, CC BY. No evidence of "wampir" in medieval or early modern Poland; "upiór" ≠ "wampir"; padlock, stones, sickle, poppy seeds, aspen stake.
7. FDA "Novelty Makeup" — content current as of 2022-11-22 (no publication date). Don't use things not intended for skin; arm patch test for a couple of days; remove makeup/glue as label says; luminescent zinc sulfide (approved Aug 2000) not near eyes.
