## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: kids favors, homemade favors, adult favors, candy-plus-toy bags.
- Gap (none of the 5 cover it): unit cost per guest; scaling 10 vs 25 guests; age bands; choking flags; allergy and non-candy options; sugar-free flags; budget-tiered bags; any cited source.
- Stale: clearbags 2017, momsandmunchkins 2014.
- Fan-out: what counts as a favor / bag filler (UK); favors by age; cost per child; non-candy and allergy-safe; toddler-safe; adult favors; eco bags; DIY; when to pack; thank-you notes.
- Angle: wins by being the only post that prices every favor per guest at 10 and 25 guests and flags each for choking risk (against the real 16 CFR 1501 exemptions) and sugar, backed by two open-access studies on non-candy choices.
