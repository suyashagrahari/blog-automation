## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched)
1. **Wang & Tang 2026, Behavioral Sciences (MDPI), CC BY — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC13203499.** Full text read via Europe PMC REST. n = 116 employees + 17 supervisors, one Chinese state-owned power company. Sociality-oriented fun (jokes) → affect-driven innovation via high-motivated positive affect; assistant-oriented fun (team party) → judgment-driven innovation via low-motivated positive affect + job satisfaction. Authors: effectiveness depends on activities being seen as "authentic, appropriate, and compatible with the work context"; involve employees in choosing. Limitations: single firm, small sample. **Note vs prompt:** the prompt asked for "fun at work and engagement"; this paper measures innovation and job satisfaction, not engagement. Chosen because it is open access, not Frontiers/SAGE/T&F, and unused by either batch (B1 puns-for-work used PMC10813194 and PMC12372801). The 2016 Employee Relations "Fun times… workplace engagement" paper is Emerald, not open access.
2. **EEOC Harassment — https://www.eeoc.gov/harassment.** Protected traits list; offensive jokes, slurs, ridicule/mockery, offensive objects/pictures; isolated incidents not illegal unless extremely serious; victim can be anyone affected. No publication date.
3. **EEOC Religious Discrimination — https://www.eeoc.gov/religious-discrimination.** Reasonable accommodation incl. dress practices; Groff v. DeJoy (2023) "substantial in the overall context of an employer's business". No publication date. (Also cited by B1 halloween-puns-for-work; caps are per batch; eeoc.gov 0 posts in B2 before this one.)
4. **OSHA 3170-02R (2007) — https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/osha3170.pdf.** "Clothing, jewelry, long hair, and even gloves can get entangled in moving machine parts"; prohibit loose clothing/jewelry near conveyors. PDF text read. osha.gov now in 2 B2 posts (halloween-nails + this).
