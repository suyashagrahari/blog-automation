## Brief-vs-source findings (structural notes)
- The prompt frames modern lines as a copyright problem. Circular 33 says words and short phrases are uncopyrightable, so a 3-word catchphrase is likely not anyone's copyright; the no-modern-quotes rule is BRIEF §6 editorial policy plus trademark/longer-passage risk. The post says this plainly.
- English intertitles of German silents (Nosferatu, Caligari) are translations; a modern restoration's new translation can carry its own copyright. Post tells readers to use an old print or translate the German. The 1922 film itself is PD.
- 1931 date verified by Duke's rule and its Mood Indigo 1931→2027 example; registration date for each Universal film not checked in the Catalog of Copyright Entries (Duke notes an earlier registration year would control). Wikipedia states 2027 for both.
