## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched)
1. Roberts & Dunbar 2015, *Managing Relationship Decay*, Human Nature (Springer, CC BY), PMC4626528. I read the full text. 25 students were followed for 18 months. Closeness to kin rose while closeness to friends fell. The decline was offset by talking (females) or shared activities (males). Distance itself did not matter; going to university did.
2. van der Mey-Baijens et al. 2025, Journal of Adolescence (Wiley, CC BY), PMC12217408. I read the full text. Sample: 187 Dutch adolescents. Best-friend support was linked to lower depression and stress, but very high co-rumination made support exacerbate stress. Caveat: the sample is 13-year-olds, not adults.
3. GOV.UK *When do the clocks change?* (updated 24 Nov 2025): clocks go back on 25 Oct 2026 and 31 Oct 2027.
4. NIST *Daylight Saving Time Rules*: in 2026, DST ends on 1 Nov; in general it ends on the first Sunday of November.
5. Drik Panchang 2027 Diwali calendar: Lakshmi Puja falls on Fri 29 Oct 2027. The 2026 page gives Sun 8 Nov 2026. This domain is cap-exempt.
