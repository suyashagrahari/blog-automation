## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched)
1. Wong, Wong, Lui & Wong 2018, *PLOS ONE*, "Revisiting facial resemblance in couples": https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC5773214 (CC BY, full text read via Europe PMC XML). Prior studies kept hairstyle/glasses/earrings/make-up/clothing, features "which could be dramatically changed easily"; Exp. 1 had 60 married couples photographed without glasses or make-up, and couples were still rated more similar than non-couples.
2. Tea-makorn & Kosinski 2020, *Scientific Reports*: https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC7550338 (CC BY, full text read). 517 couples, 153 judges + VGGFace2; similar at marriage, no convergence after 20–69 years.
3. NWS, Understanding Wind Chill: https://www.weather.gov/safety/cold-wind-chill-chart. Wind chill defined only ≤50°F and wind >3 mph. Undated page.
