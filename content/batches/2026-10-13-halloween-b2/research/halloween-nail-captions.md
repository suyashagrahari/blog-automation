## Phase 3 — Sources (all fetched as full text via Europe PMC REST; all open access)
1. Ali & Thomsen 2026, *Photography in dermatology*, Frontiers in Medicine — PMC13505408 (CC BY). Indirect daylight, avoid mixed light and flash, perpendicular camera, plain non-reflective background.
2. Benlidayi 2026, *MedPhotoPubl*, J Korean Med Sci — PMC13120865 (CC BY-NC; cited, not reproduced). White backgrounds create prominent shadows; black may diminish 3D appearance.
3. Kaliyadan & Pasquali 2026, *Esthetics and Ethics*, Indian Dermatol Online J — PMC13395270 (CC BY-NC-SA). Consent for any image use, especially social media; verbal consent insufficient for journals.
4. Zhivagui et al. 2023, Nature Communications — PMC9845303 (CC BY). 20-min UV dryer exposure: 20–30% cell death; three sessions: 65–70%. In vitro only — stated as such.
5. Kamińska et al. 2026, Postępy Dermatol Alergol — PMC13217139 (CC BY-NC-SA). 28 studies; brittleness/splitting most common; minimal cancer risk in most; SPF50 30 min before, fingerless gloves.
6. Wang & Lipner 2024, Skin Appendage Disorders (Karger) — PMC11147520 (CC BY-NC). ACD from (meth)acrylates most prevalent; 14 nail-glue burns in 8 publications; social media trends.
