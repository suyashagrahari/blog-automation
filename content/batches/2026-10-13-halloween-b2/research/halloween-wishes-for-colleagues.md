## Phase 2 — gap
- **Table stakes:** coworker, boss and client groups; funny vs professional tone.
- **Gap:** none covers opt-in or not-celebrating colleagues (workwizardry mentions "inclusive" twice in passing); none treats a remote colleague's time zone (cozywisher has one "remote" H2 with no timing); none covers new joiners or people leaving in October; none organises lines by channel (Slack, email sign-off, card); none cites research; none has a table.
- **Stale:** workwizardry is "2024 Edition", dated 2023.
- **Fan-out:** what to write to a coworker / boss / client; is it OK to wish the boss; what if someone does not celebrate; when to send across time zones; leaving-card lines; new-employee lines; puns or not; length.
- **Angle:** Wins by being the only post that sorts colleague wishes by recipient AND channel/tone, makes them explicitly opt-in (EEOC/Acas), and times them for international colleagues (New York 4 hours behind London on Halloween 2026 and 2027).
