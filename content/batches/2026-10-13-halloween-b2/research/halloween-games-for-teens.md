## Phase 2: gap analysis

- **Table stakes:** charades, trivia, murder mystery, scavenger hunt, costume contest, pumpkin decorating, horror movie, food ideas.
- **Gap:** no page gives players, minutes, materials, an embarrassment/cringe rating or a supervision note per game; no page has a comparison table or FAQ; no page addresses phones/photo consent, outdoor night safety, a parent-teen agreement, curfew or alcohol; none cites any source. Craftsy and Mums mix toddler games (pumpkin tic-tac-toe, mummy race) into "teens", the "babyish" problem.
