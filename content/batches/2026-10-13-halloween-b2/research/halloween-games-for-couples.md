## Phase 3: sources (all fetched via Europe PMC REST; none used in either batch, checked by PMCID/PMID)
Searched with findpapers: "couple play leisure satisfaction", "adult playfulness romantic relationship", "couples playing video games together relationship", "shared laughter couples", "couple leisure oxytocin board game", "cooperative competitive game romantic partners", "long-distance couples shared online activities", "couple joint leisure relationship quality daily". Also ran Europe PMC title queries for playfulness/game/humour/novelty/long-distance with couples, and for pumpkin carving and escape rooms.

1. Walker, Pinkus, Olderbak & MacCann, *Current Psychology*, 2023-03-10, PMC9999077, CC BY. n=277. Humour r=.33 with relationship satisfaction, valuing r=.43, listening r=.27. Only these three had significant relative weights. Abstract read.
2. Yang et al., *Behavioral Sciences* (MDPI), 2024-08-27, PMC11444135, CC BY. 321 participants in Korea, online survey Feb–Mar 2024, four groups. Couples who play the same leisure sport together had the highest intimacy and satisfaction and the lowest divorce intention. Full text read. Cross-sectional design, convenience sample.
3. Chen et al., *BMC Psychology*, 2025-07-09, PMC12243193, CC BY-NC-ND (cited, not reproduced). RCT with 40 couples (20 China, 20 Malaysia) and six 90-minute sessions over three weeks using the Love Lingual card game. Satisfaction improved more than in an alternative workshop control. Full text read.
4. Marcus, Green & Werner, *Preventive Medicine*, June 2004, PMID 15193901. Cadaver-hand tests: pumpkin-carving knives caused fewer and less severe injuries than kitchen knives. **Abstract only; full text paywalled.**
