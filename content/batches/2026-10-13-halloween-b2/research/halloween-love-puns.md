## Phase 2: gap analysis

- **Table stakes:** puns grouped by monster (ghost, skeleton, witch, vampire, pumpkin, mummy, bat, cat, candy); short copyable lines.
- **The gap:** no ranking page is about love puns. They are general lists with a few romance lines mixed in. None says where to use a line (card, text, caption, treat label). None explains why a pun works, none cites any research, and none gives the boo = beau etymology that makes the core pun work. Several include drinking or innuendo lines ("sheet-faced", "boo-ty"). The clean, partner-specific intent is underserved.
- **Stale data:** romper is from 2022. Hubspot cites 2023 campaigns.
- **Fan-out sub-queries:** puns for boyfriend; what does boo mean; are love puns cheesy; can I send one to a crush; what to write in a Halloween card; short couple caption; clean love puns; does humour help relationships; candy label line; pun inside a greeting page.

**Angle:** This post wins by being the only one that keeps only Halloween puns with a real love meaning, tags every group for card, text, caption or treat label, and explains the pun mechanic with open-access research on pun funniness and couples' humour. It uses SubhSandesh's platform-wide 48.3% phone-open share and love-gf's 29.7% view share to argue for short lines, plus a counted fit (49 of 60) for the template's 60-character question.
