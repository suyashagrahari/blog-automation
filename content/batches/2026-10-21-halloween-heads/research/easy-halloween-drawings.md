## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched; Europe PMC API)
1. Zubler et al. 2022, *Pediatrics* (PMC9680195). Milestones: "Draws a circle when you show him how" at 3 y; "Holds crayon or pencil between fingers and thumb (not in a fist)" at 4 y; "Draws a person with ≥3 body parts" at 4 y; each set at ≥75% of children. Full text read via the NCBI PMC OA BioC service; Europe PMC served the abstract only. cdc.gov returned 403.
2. Long, Fan, Huey, Chai & Frank 2024, *Nature Communications* (PMC10853520, CC BY). Over 37K drawings, 48 categories, ages 2–10, 30 s per drawing on a tablet; recognisability rises gradually; even unrecognisable drawings carry animacy and size information. It was also cited in 2026-09-09 Ganesh, a different batch.
3. Zhang et al. 2023, *Brain and Behavior* (PMC10636421, Wiley, CC BY). n=44 students, fear clip, then 5-minute drawing. The house-drawing (distraction) group had higher valence at the end of drawing, with no difference after a 3 min 48 s relaxation video.
4. Jia, Zhang & Liao 2026, *BMJ Paediatrics Open* (PMC13583843, CC BY-NC, cited not reused). 10 RCTs, 816 children aged 5–12; lower short-term anxiety mainly in medical and procedure contexts; no single generalisable effect. Abstract read.
- Described, not linked: Kaimal, Ray & Muniz 2016, *Art Therapy* (Taylor & Francis, banned): 39 adults, 45 minutes, lower cortisol, no difference by prior art experience. Abstract only (PMC5004743).
- Rejected: all Frontiers hits (banned), the MDPI *Behav Sci* 2026 artmaking paper (not needed), and the CDC pages (bot wall).
