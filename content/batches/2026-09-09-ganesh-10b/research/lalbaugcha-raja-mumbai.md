## Conversion honesty

Search intent here is **navigational and logistical**, not commercial. Someone typing "lalbaugcha
raja" wants a queue, a station and a time; they are not shopping for a greeting page, and pretending
otherwise would wreck the post. The single legitimate bridge is the reader who **cannot get to
Mumbai this year** — and even for them the mandal's own free live stream is named first, before the
template. The template link is one sentence in one section. `honestAssessment` says so plainly.
