## Phase 2 — gap analysis

**Table stakes** (present, in some form, on all of them): a Ganpati-themed card; Marathi and
Hindi options; a "download or share on WhatsApp" step; a mention that it is free.

**The gap — and it is enormous.** Not one page answers any of these:

1. **What time do I print on the card?** Every card maker gives you a blank time field. None
   tells you that the Madhyahna Ganesha Puja muhurat on 14 September 2026 is 11:20 AM–1:48 PM
   in Mumbai, 11:02 AM–1:31 PM in Delhi and 10:51 AM–1:18 PM in Chennai — a 29-minute spread
   that matters if the family is split across cities.
2. **What actually has to be on the card.** They give layouts, not a content checklist.
3. **What happens after you hit send.** The card lands in a WhatsApp group. Nobody discusses
   what a WhatsApp group does to an invitation.
4. **Why nobody replies.** The RSVP problem is the single most common complaint about digital
   invites and it is completely unaddressed.
5. **When paper is still right.** No tool page will ever tell you not to use the tool.

**Stale data:** there is none to supersede, because none of them cite any. Position 4 (position
3 in the SERP list above) is a 2023-dated URL, which is the closest thing to a stale signal.

**Fan-out sub-queries → H2s:**

- what is a ganpati invitation card → H2 2
- what to write in ganpati invitation card → H2 3 (seven numbered items)
- ganesh chaturthi 2026 muhurat time for invitation → H2 4
- how to send ganpati invitation on whatsapp → H2 5
- ganpati invitation card vs digital invite → H2 6 (table)
- should I send a digital ganpati invitation to elders → H2 7

**Angle:**

> Wins by being the only page that treats a Ganpati invitation as a distribution problem rather
> than a design problem — pairing the exact Madhyahna muhurat windows for five cities on
> 14 September 2026 with platform-wide SubhSandesh data on how shared pages actually behave
> (49.6% opened on a phone, 42.1% password-protected, 96.1% published).

**The honest strategic point, and it goes in `honestAssessment`:** this SERP is 100% tools. A
blog post is not going to rank for `ganesh chaturthi invitation card online free maker`, and
pretending otherwise would be dishonest. The post's job is to (a) win the long-tail
question-shaped queries no tool page answers, (b) be the citable source when an answer engine
is asked what goes on a Ganpati invitation or what time to put on it, and (c) route that reader
to `/happy-ganesh-chaturthi`, which is the tool the SERP is actually asking for.
