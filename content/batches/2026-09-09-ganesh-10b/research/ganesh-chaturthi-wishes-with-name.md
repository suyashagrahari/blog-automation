## Phase 2 — gap analysis

**Table stakes** (present on nearly all of them, so the post must cover them): the phrase
"with name" as the product promise; the idea of a greeting-card format; script/language options
(Marathi, Hindi, Gujarati); a logo/business variant; the festival date somewhere on the page.

**The gap.** Every competitor stops at the moment of generation. Not one of them addresses the
state *after* sending: the name is in the pixels, so a misspelling is permanent, a recompressed
JPEG degrades the smallest glyphs first, and forwarding the image separates it from the message
that carried the name. Nobody discusses name legibility at phone width, and nobody names a
downside of personalisation at all.

**Stale data.** There is no data on these pages to supersede — the gap is that they cite nothing.
The stale element is dating: a "Happy Ganesh Chaturthi 2023" H2 still live in September 2026.

**Unanswered questions raised and dropped:** what to do when the name is long or carries an
initial; whether the uploaded photo is retained; whether a name on the deity's image is
appropriate; how to handle forty recipients.
