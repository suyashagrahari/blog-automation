## Phase 2 — Gap analysis

**Table stakes (all six cover these; the post must too):** a numbered list of concrete ideas; desk/office objects; something personalised; an experience option; a "how to choose" frame; explicit reference to the husband/him relationship.

**The gap — the timing constraint.** A promotion is announced without notice. The reader almost always learns about it in the same few hours the recipient does, and wants to mark it *that evening*. Every page in the SERP recommends objects that need days to personalise, and none says so. Nobody in the top ten answers "what reaches him tonight?"

**Second gap — register.** Nobody names the difference between congratulation and romance, which is the whole emotional content of this query. Two pages actively confuse it.

**Third gap — audience boundary.** Nobody draws a line between a spouse's gift and a colleague's. The corporate-merch intents (`promotion gift for boss`, `for coworker`, `for men`, `what is a promotional gift`, `promotion gift basket`) were cut on Gate 5 for this batch, so the post notes the distinction so the reader can self-select, without targeting them.

**Stale data:** nothing to supersede — there is no data on these pages to be stale.

**Unanswered questions raised and dropped:** how much to spend (giftpack raises it across five H3s and never lands a figure); whether a gift card is acceptable; what to write.
