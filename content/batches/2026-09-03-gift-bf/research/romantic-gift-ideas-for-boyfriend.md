### The load-bearing pair — the passage no incumbent sets up

Sources 1 and 2 disagree, and the post makes that its central move rather than hiding it:

- **Impett, Park & Muise (2023/2024)** — the empirical work does *not* strongly support the
  love-languages model's three assumptions (one preferred language per person, five
  languages, higher satisfaction from speaking the partner's). Proposed alternative: love as
  a **balanced diet** needing a full range of expressions.
- **Mostova, Stolarski & Matthews (*PLOS ONE*, 2022)** — in **100 heterosexual couples**,
  *matching* did track outcomes: people who expressed affection the way their partner
  preferred to receive it reported greater relationship and sexual satisfaction. Empathy
  supported the mechanism among male but not female participants.

Read together they flip the practical instruction from **"find out which of five types he
is"** to **"watch how he actually receives affection"** — the framework is weakly supported,
the attentiveness it stands in for is not. That is the hub's core claim, and no page in the
measured SERP attempts it.

### A sourcing error I made, and the correction

On the first pass I ruled out three on-topic papers as "already spent". **Two of those three
exclusions were wrong, and the mistake was mine to make but the file invited it.**

`USED-SOURCES.md` contains **two** URL lists under separate headings:

| Section | URLs | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `## The 42 spent URLs — do not cite any of these` | **42** | **Closed.** Already in 2 posts; a third use breaches the URL cap |
| `## ✅ LEGAL TO CITE — every URL used exactly once, one slot left each` | **719** | **Legal.** One prior use, one slot remaining |

I ran `grep -oE 'https?://[^ )]+' USED-SOURCES.md | sort -u`, which returns all **762** and
**conflates the block list with the legal list** — so 719 citable URLs read as forbidden.
The coordinator reports the batch verifier had the identical bug earlier today. Parsing
scoped to the closed section only:

```
closed 42 · legal-once 719
journals.plos.org/...pone.0269429   in closed-42? False   in legal-once? True
journals.lww.com/10.4103/amh...     in closed-42? False   in legal-once? True
```

Both were **legal**, and both are now cited (sources 2 and 4). Consequences:

1. The **love-language matching study** is reinstated and is now the post's strongest
   passage — the counterweight to Impett et al. described above. It is cited at its own PLOS
   URL, not via its `PMC9216579` mirror.
2. The **Indian technoference study** is reinstated. It replaced the China partner-phubbing
   study (*Frontiers*, 10 January 2025) in the honest-limits section — a straight upgrade:
   same claim, India-specific rather than Chinese, and it takes `frontiersin.org` back down
   to one URL from this post while the domain sits at its 3-post cap.
3. **The earlier concession that this post's India ground is "first-party data and the
   measured SERP, not a citation" is withdrawn.** `honestAssessment` now names the
   *Archives of Mental Health* interviews with 22 married Indian couples as the India-specific
   citation, which is the axis on which `theknot.com` — a US wedding title — cannot compete.

The third exclusion stands: `icesi.edu.co/.../estudios_gerenciales/article/view/3539`
("Personal values and gift giving act") **is** in the closed 42.

**Practical note for the next batch:** never grep `USED-SOURCES.md` whole. Scope to the
closed section, e.g. cut between the `## The 42 spent URLs` and `## Heavily-used domains`
headings before extracting URLs.
