None of the four mentions the same-day case. None reports a build window. None plans
backwards from the date.

**Table stakes** (all five SERP pages carry them, so the post must not omit them): what the
thing is, what goes inside it, milestone framing, a private/shareable link, some idea of
scope.

**The gap taken**: an anniversary is the only romantic occasion with a **fixed, known,
mutually-remembered date**. There is no "will she notice" and no reveal-of-the-occasion —
only *what* lands and *when*. That makes it an execution problem against a deadline, and
nothing on the SERP or on-site treats it that way.

**Rejected angles**, and why:

- "Another list of anniversary surprise ideas" — would cannibalise three live pages and
  duplicate giftfeels' and confettigifts' structure. Rejected.
- "First anniversary specifically" — taken by `first-anniversary-digital-letter-creator`.
- "Long distance anniversary" — taken by two live pages.
- "Countdown to the anniversary" — taken by `digital-countdown-clock-long-distance-anniversary`;
  this post deliberately does **not** describe a countdown widget.

**Angle sentence**: wins by being the only post that treats an anniversary as a
fixed-deadline execution problem rather than a gift-selection problem — a schedule counted
backwards from the date, including the same-day case — anchored on the 7.2-hour median
first-save-to-last-edit window across 987 `/love-gf` pages and the 99.5% publish rate.

**Fan-out sub-queries → H2s / FAQs**: what is an online anniversary surprise · how far ahead
to start · what if it is today · what hour should it land · what changes across time zones ·
how do I keep it hidden when she knows the date · how many photos · page or video call ·
when is this the wrong choice.

**Honest note on strength**: the angle is real and uncovered, but it is narrow, and the exact
keyword has no editorial slot to win. The realistic value of this post is answer-engine
capture on the timing sub-queries, not a rank for the head phrase.
