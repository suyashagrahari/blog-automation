## Phase 2: gap
- **Table stakes:** a list of copyable Hindi shayari and a section for friends.
- **Gap:** no page does any of the following:
  - sorts friends by kind (childhood, closest, group, new, friend's family, hard year)
  - explains dost, yaar, jigri or langotiya
  - glosses the lines in English
  - says when to send in 2027
  - says when a shayari is the wrong message
- **Fan-out sub-queries:** best friend shayari, funny shayari for the friends group, the meaning of jigri / langotiya, when to send in 2027, group or private, shayari for a grieving friend.
- **Angle:** see batchMeta.angle.

## Phase 3: sources (all fetched and read)
1. Platts (1884) on DSAL. All four are reference instruments and cap-exempt:
   - dost, p. 534
   - yār, p. 1247
   - jigrī, p. 384
   - laṅgoṭiyā, p. 966
2. Dunbar 2017, *Breaking Bread*, Adapt Hum Behav Physiol, PMC6979515 (full text, CC BY):
   - n = 2,000
   - laughter 67.9%, reminiscences 51.5%, both strongest
   - jokes p = 0.174, not significant
3. Chung et al. 2024, R Soc Open Sci, PMC11521598 (full text, CC BY):
   - n = 112
   - the bonding effect depended on joint attention
4. findpapers queries tried:
   - "friendship in Hindi-Urdu poetry" and "Urdu ghazal dosti yaar friendship theme": no open-access hit; only paywalled OUP chapters.
   - "shared laughter social bonding friends", "teasing humor friendship closeness", "reaching out to old friends underestimated appreciation".
   - The candidates were PLOS ONE (at cap), Frontiers (banned) and off-topic papers.
