## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: list of ideas; 9:16; music; posting timing.
- Gap: no page gives per-idea length + second-by-second shots; none gives flame-safety notes for filming near diyas; none cites Instagram's own current length rule (several third-party pages still say 90 s or 3 min); none designs for sound-off viewing.
- Fan-out: how long should a Diwali reel be; what to film; reels without firecrackers; music rules; where to put text; when to post in 2027; filming diyas in low light; safety with children.
- Angle: wins by being the only post that gives original reel concepts as timed shot lists with a safety note each, sized to Instagram's October 2026 limits and timed to 29 Oct 2027 with Raksha Bandhan 2026 lead-time data.
- Avoided ideas already used by competitors or siblings: rangoli time-lapse, five days explained, deep clean, nani's house smells, quiet morning after, homecoming journey, chappals at the door (b1 decoration), captions/hashtags (b1 diwali-captions, b2 diwali-quotes-for-instagram).
