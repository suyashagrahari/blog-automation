## Phase 2 — gap
- **Table stakes:** diya card, rangoli card, crackers card, materials list, kid-friendly.
- **Gap:** no page gives time, difficulty, age or dated material cost per design; none matches designs to how many days are left; none names a safety issue; nobody uses colour change, a paper circuit, a pull-tab mechanism or a paper-cut rangoli medallion.
- **Fan-out:** easiest card for a toddler; how long; materials cost; colour-change card; battery safety; will it reach another city; no-scissors card; card for a 10-year-old; moving card; sending a photo.
- **Cannibalisation:** b1 `diwali-card` (types, not designs) lists domains only; shared domains youtube/pinterest, 0 confirmed shared URLs. b1 `diwali-drawing` (anchor) — none of its 10 drawings or its three-drawing card layout reused. Old `what-to-write-in-a-diwali-card` — no wording or message content here.
- **Angle:** Wins by being the only post that gives original handmade Diwali card designs each graded by minutes, difficulty, age and dated material cost, matched in a lead-time table to SubhSandesh's Raksha Bandhan 2026 build-timing data.
