## Phase 2 — gap

- **Table stakes:** bow and arrow, "Jai Shri Ram" lettering, Ram Mandir outline, diyas.
- **Gap:** no page gives a grid, size, colour count, powder quantity or time; no page names where the motifs come from in the Ramayana; no page cites a Deepotsav number; no page addresses whether Ram's name/image should be on a walked floor.
- **Stale data:** UP Tourism's Deepotsav page (undated) still says "over 12 lakhs diyas" as last year's record; Guinness lists 2,617,215 (19 Oct 2025).
- **Fan-out sub-queries:** is it okay to put Ram's name in a floor rangoli; easiest Ram rangoli; how much powder; how to write राम neatly; which colours; how many diyas at Ayodhya; why sandals; reuse for Ram Navami; how to remove; any tool.
- **Angle:** wins by being the only page that gives Ram-motif rangolis as counted dot grids with size, colours, powder and time, traced to lines in Valmiki and to Guinness-verified Deepotsav numbers, and that addresses the floor custom.
