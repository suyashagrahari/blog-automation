# Research brief: diwali-2027-around-the-world

- Keyword: diwali 2027 around the world (anchor: diwali 2027 date in india calendar)
- Phenomenon: Diwali as a public holiday outside India (Nepal Tihar, Singapore, Malaysia, Mauritius, Fiji, Trinidad, Guyana, Suriname, Sri Lanka)
- Measured 2026-10-22. SERP via Firecrawl search, location India. WebSearch was tried first but is US-only (it returned qppstudio/publicholidays aggregators), so it was not used as the SERP.

Cannibalisation: the top 10 shares 5 URLs with b1 diwali-2027-city-wise-timings and 4 each with b1 diwali-2027-calendar and diwali-2027-date. To keep clear of those posts, the angle covers only official holiday status outside India. It gives no Indian city timings and no Indian holiday lists, and it does not repeat the US/UK/Canada material in b1 diwali-2027-in-usa-uk-canada.

## Phase 2: gap

- What every page covers: India's 2027 date, Friday 29 October.
- What none of them covers: any country's official 2027 holiday date outside India, the Thursday-versus-Friday split, which governments have not published yet, or how far ahead each government announces.
- Error in a ranking page: purohit.app puts Mauritius on Thursday, but the PMO list gives Friday 29 October 2027.
- Fan-out questions: Is Diwali a holiday in Singapore or Malaysia in 2027? Why 28 vs 29 October? Which countries have the holiday? When will Fiji, Trinidad and Tobago, Guyana and Nepal confirm? What about the Sunday 8 November 2026 holiday?
- Angle: the post wins by being the only one that gives each country's status from its own government notice, with the latest official year labelled where 2027 is not out.
