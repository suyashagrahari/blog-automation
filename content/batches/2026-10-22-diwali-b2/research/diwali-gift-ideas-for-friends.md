## Phase 3 — sources (all fetched 2026-10-22)

1. Dunbar R.I.M., "Why friendship and loneliness affect our health", Ann N Y Acad Sci, epub 6 Mar 2025, CC BY — https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC11918532 — full text read. Layers 1.5/5/15/50/150; contact minima weekly (5), monthly (15), yearly (150), "minima, not averages"; mode of contact unimportant; closeness declines within a few months; ~3 years no contact to leave the 150. Subject test: pass (friendship maintenance). Swap test: weak — could fit diwali-shayari-for-friends (recorded as failed).

Study 1 N = 144, Study 2 N = 207 (children made personalised symbolic gifts → positive attitudes and social closeness improved); all outcomes back to baseline at ~40-day follow-up. Caveat: children, intergroup prejudice, Türkiye — stated in body.
