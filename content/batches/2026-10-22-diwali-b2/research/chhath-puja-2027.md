## Phase 2 — gap
Table stakes: four dates, day-by-day rites, "main day" = Sandhya Arghya.
Gap: none of the top 5 give arghya times across Bihar/migrant cities side by side, compute how long the waterless fast actually runs, state the 2027 central holiday status, or cite any study on Chhath (health). Fan-out: dates; arghya time in my city; how long is the fast; is it a holiday; what is eaten on Kharna; second Chhath; can a diabetic fast; how to take part from another city.
