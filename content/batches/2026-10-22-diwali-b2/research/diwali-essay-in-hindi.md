## Phase 2: angle

Wins by being the only page that combines all of the following:
- original Hindi essays at exactly counted 100/150/250 words
- a structure based on CBSE 2025-26 Hindi (Course A) Q12 marking (1+3+1+1)
- the 2027 dates
- UNESCO's 2025 inscription, including its line that schools help pass on the tradition
- a length gauge from SubhSandesh's 88-word median letter

Fan-out became the H2s and FAQs: how to start, paragraphs in 150 words, 2027 date, प्रस्तावना vs भूमिका, whether copying is allowed, word counting, CBSE marks, UNESCO, pollution, whether a website can write it, and the 2026 date.

## Phase 3: sources (all fetched 2026-10-22)

1. **CBSE Class X Hindi Course A SQP 2025-26 marking scheme PDF.** Q12 asks for a ~120-word अनुच्छेद worth 6 marks: भूमिका 1, विषयवस्तु 3, निष्कर्ष 1, भाषा शुद्धता 1. The text was read from a legacy-font text layer.
2. **UNESCO ICH, Deepavali (02312).**
   - Inscribed 2025, 20.COM 7.b.24.
   - The tradition is passed on informally in families: elders teach children through stories and hands-on participation.
   - Schools also play a role in passing it on.
   - It supports local economies.
3. **PLOS ONE, 4 Mar 2026, Johansen et al.** (Europe PMC PMC12959713, full text read). The sample is 3,297 students in four European countries. Among upper secondary students:
   - 64% identified copying a short paragraph as non-compliant.
   - 58% had copied short passages without quote marks.
4. **McGregor, Oxford Hindi-English Dictionary (DSAL).**
   - भूमिका and प्रस्तावना: introduction; preface.
   - निष्कर्ष: deduction; conclusion.
   - निबंध: essay.
   - दीवाली ← dīpāvali, at the new moon of Kārttik.
   - उपसंहार has no McGregor or Platts entry.
5. **drikpanchang 2027 Diwali calendar.** Used as a date reference only: 27, 29 and 31 Oct 2027.
