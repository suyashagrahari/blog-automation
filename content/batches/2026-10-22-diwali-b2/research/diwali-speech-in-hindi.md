1. Chakraborty, Dihingia, Sarmah, Sinha, Speech Prosody 2024 (ISCA archive PDF) — Table 1: native-Hindi read speech (HI NWS) 4.8 syllables/s, 225 breath groups, pause-free groups of 7+ syllables. Subject test: Hindi speech rate. Peer-reviewed conference, open access.

Subject test: public speaking anxiety.
