## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: Sanskrit lines with Hindi/English meaning; lamp and Lakshmi themes.
- Gap: no verse numbers, no translations with translator/date, no dictionary checking, grammar errors on 2 of the top 2, nothing on agreement or the dual, nothing on how to pronounce IAST.
- Unique find: Monier-Williams cites BhP iv.21.4 for dīpāvali, while the Sanskrit Wikisource edition of BhP 4.21.4 reads दीपबलिभिः — editions differ. MW spells dīpāvali with short i (dīpāvalī long ī not a headword); MW glosses śubhāśaya as "of virtuous disposition".
- Fan-out: correct way to say Happy Diwali in Sanskrit; classical shloka with source; short status; for parents; for business; pronunciation; is शुभं करोति Vedic; spelling of Deepavali.
- Angle: wins by being the only post on this SERP that gives every classical line a verse number and public-domain translation, checks every word of its own wishes against MW by page, corrects five errors printed by ranking pages, and teaches the dual — set against SubhSandesh's 48.3% phone-open rate.
