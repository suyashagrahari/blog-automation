- Table stakes: gift categories (hampers, drinkware, sweets, décor), budget bands, personalisation/logo.
- Gap: none of the 5 fetched pages mentions GST input-credit blocking (CGST s.17(5)(h)), TDS on business benefits (s.194R), the 2018 PC Act giver offences (ss.8–10) or what a client's gift policy contains. EcoKaari names "your gifting policy" as a budget factor and stops.
