## Phase 2 — gap

- **Table stakes:** Rama's homecoming; at least a mention of Lakshmi/Krishna/Jain/Sikh observances; "light over darkness".
- **Gap (none of the fetched pages):** script length or minutes by age band; questions to ask (CROWD types); which scenes to cut for toddlers (abduction, war, exile); a South Indian (Narakasura) telling written for children alongside the North Indian one; honest note on when a screen is the wrong medium.
- **Stale data:** peekaabookids prints Diwali 2025 dates.
- **Fan-out sub-queries → H2/FAQ:** what is the Diwali story (for a child) · story for a 3-year-old · how long should it be · is Ravana too scary · questions to ask after · why oil bath before sunrise (Tamil) · Diwali 2027 date · mixed class with Jain/Sikh kids · video vs reading · grandparent abroad · Diwali vs Dussehra story.
- **Angle sentence:** wins by being the only post that gives original read-aloud scripts graded and timed by age with CROWD-type questions and a per-age scene-cutting guide, using SubhSandesh's 48.4% touch-device share and 11.1 views per page.
