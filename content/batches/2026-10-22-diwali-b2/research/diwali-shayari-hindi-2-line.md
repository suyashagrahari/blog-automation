## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: ready-to-copy two-line verses in Devanagari; some grouping.
- Gap: no page writes original verse grouped by mood, gives line lengths, explains what makes two lines a sher, or helps the reader write one; none dated for 2027.
- Fan-out sub-queries → H2s: what makes a 2-line sher (Platts H2) · lines by mood (12-sher H2) · which to send whom/when (table H2) · why rhyme works (study H2) · how to write my own (3 steps H2) · something more than text (SubhSandesh H2) · when not to (limits H2).
- Excluded because b1 already covers them: second line carrying the weight (Wassiliwizky), script choice (Devanagari/Roman), Nazir Akbarabadi, radif via Platts, mourning/elder/group-of-40 limits, soan papdi joke, "elders who don't open links".
- Angle: wins by being the only page with original mood-grouped two-line Hindi shayari of measured length, Platts' door-leaf sense of misra, a peer-reviewed case for rhyme, and build-your-own steps.
