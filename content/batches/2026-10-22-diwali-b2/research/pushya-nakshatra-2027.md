## Phase 2 — gap
- Table stakes: the 2027 dates/times; that Pushya is auspicious for purchases; Guru/Ravi Pushya names.
- Gap: nobody says the Diwali 2027 Pushya is Shani Pushya and that no Guru/Ravi Pushya falls between 27 Sep and 18 Nov; nobody explains the weekday drift (27.32-day sidereal month = 3 weeks + 6.32 days); nobody notes the 26 Sep Ravi Pushya falls in Pitru Paksha (15–29 Sep 2027); nobody quotes the actual classical line (Brihat Samhita: new garment worn in Pushya → wealth); nobody explains that a nakshatra starts at one instant everywhere (so "regional" tables differing by an hour are method, not geography); no diaspora times.
- Stale/wrong: hindu-blog's "North-East-South" table runs ~70 min later than Drik and our calculation.
- Fan-out: dates in Oct 2027; Guru Pushya before Diwali?; what is Shani Pushya; days before Diwali; city/abroad timings; why people buy; Ravi Pushya Sept; 2026 Pushya; which stars.

Angle: wins by being the only post that shows Diwali 2027's Pushya is Shani Pushya with no Guru/Ravi Pushya for seven weeks around it, explains why, flags the Pitru Paksha clash, quotes the Brihat Samhita line, and frames shopping vs greeting timing with SubhSandesh's Raksha Bandhan lead-time data.
