## Phase 2 — gap analysis

**Table stakes** (all the real pages cover these; the post must too):
a private page or website; a playlist; a letter; flowers or cake to her door; a
surprise video call; a photo compilation; "make it specific to your
relationship".

**The gap — and it is a large one.** Every ranking page answers a different
question than the one asked. "Online" in these articles means *ordered online*,
not *delivered online*: catholicsingles opens with a florist and India-Post-style
snail mail, Costco ranks a physical category page, and momjunction's 48 ideas
include singing to her in person. Nobody separates:

1. surprises that need nothing but a link,
2. surprises that need a small spend,
3. surprises that need her postal address,
4. surprises that need a third person to cooperate.

And nobody touches execution at all. Not one page addresses: what hour to send it
in when you are in different time zones; how to keep it secret when she shares
her phone with family; how to check it renders on her phone before you send it;
or what to do when the surprise lands while she is in a meeting. Those are the
questions that decide whether the surprise works, and they are all absent.

**Stale data.** catholicsingles.com is last-modified 25 July 2019 — seven years
old at the time of writing. None of the ten results cite a single statistic, so
there is no stale number to supersede; there is an *absence* of numbers to fill.

**Unanswered questions raised and dropped:** how long any of this takes; what it
costs; whether it works without her address; whether she will actually open it.

**Fan-out sub-queries** (each became an H2 or an FAQ):
what counts as an online surprise · free online surprises for girlfriend · how to
surprise girlfriend online without spending money · surprise for girlfriend
online without her address · best time to send an online surprise long distance ·
how to keep an online surprise secret · will she open it on her phone · what if
she opens it at a bad time · online vs physical gift for girlfriend · how to make
a website for your girlfriend · online surprise ideas for a girlfriend in another
country.

**Angle (→ `batchMeta.angle`):** wins by being the only post that sorts online
surprises into four tiers by what each one actually costs the sender — 10 minutes,
a small spend, her address, or another person's cooperation — and then solves the
execution problems every ranking page skips, using SubhSandesh's own 3,335 created
pages, the 6.9-hour median edit window and the 50.3% phone-open rate.
